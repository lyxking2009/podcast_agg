## Overview
This weekly roundup episode of Empire, hosted by Jason Yanowitz and Santiago Roel with guest Rob Hadick, focuses on Hyperliquid's headline-grabbing move into pre-IPO synthetic perpetuals. The launch of SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI pre-IPO contracts on Hyperliquid is framed as the platform's true "breakout moment" — the point where it crosses from crypto-native venue into a challenger to traditional pre-IPO secondary markets.

The hosts then pivot to broader market analysis: whether current signals indicate a new bull market phase is beginning, an Ethereum valuation critique, and SpaceX's IPO timeline and valuation debate. The episode aired May 22, 2026.

## Implications
Hyperliquid's move to list synthetic pre-IPO perpetuals for the most anticipated private companies signals an inflection point in DeFi's ambitions: on-chain venues are no longer content to replicate existing crypto trading — they are competing directly with traditional finance infrastructure for price discovery. If Hyperliquid's SPCX and similar contracts gain liquidity, it could erode the privileged information advantage that secondary private market platforms (e.g., Forge, Nasdaq Private Market) currently hold. The broader question is whether regulatory clarity in 2026 will allow or curtail this model before it scales.
