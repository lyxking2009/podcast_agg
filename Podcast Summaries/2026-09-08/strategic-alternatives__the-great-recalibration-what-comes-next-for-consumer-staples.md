## TL;DR

RBC's Nik Modi discusses 'The Great Recalibration: Consumer Staples Edition,' arguing that consumer staples companies face long-standing valuation pressure, income bifurcation, and digital disruption. He recommends rethinking portfolio architecture, simplifying choices to combat decision fatigue, treating digital platforms as core demand-creation ecosystems, and building capabilities for a changed landscape.

## Key points

- Consumer staples companies are experiencing declining valuation premiums and persistent earnings pressure, forcing boards and management teams to rethink traditional approaches to growth, investment, and value creation.
- The K-shaped economy means spending is increasingly concentrated at the high and low ends of the market, requiring brands to rethink portfolio architecture, consumer targeting, and growth strategies.
- Rising choice overload and decision fatigue create opportunities for brands and retailers to simplify decision-making through assortment and merchandising strategies that align products with consumer occasions.
- Digital platforms function as an 'Eighth Continent' reshaping demand creation; cultural relevance, platform-native strategies, and creator partnerships are becoming as important as brand scale.
- Nik Modi outlines five board and management priorities, including capability building, organizational redesign, resilience, and treating digital as core business infrastructure.
