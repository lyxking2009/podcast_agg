# #198 - Michael Shermer - Truth, Conspiracies & The Things We Can't Explain — The Peter McCormack Show

## TL;DR
Skeptic magazine founder Michael Shermer tells Peter McCormack that we are not living in a post-truth world, defending a Bayesian, evidence-updating approach and distinguishing real conspiracies (MKUltra, COINTELPRO, Volkswagen emissions, Jeffrey Epstein) from false ones like Pizzagate. He describes building a prototype AI fact-checking tool and argues AI is making society more truthful, not less. The conversation also covers UAPs, simulation theory, and religion treated as literary rather than literal truth.

## Key points
- Shermer rejects the 'post-truth' framing, advocating 'universal realism' combined with fallibilism and a Bayesian approach — never claim 0% or 100% certainty.
- He distinguishes real conspiracies (MKUltra, COINTELPRO's FBI blackmail of Martin Luther King Jr., Volkswagen emissions scandal, Jeffrey Epstein) from false ones (Pizzagate, which nonetheless led to a real shooting).
- Shermer built a prototype called 'Show Us Receipts' in about three days that auto-fact-checked every UK MP's last 50 tweets against primary sources.
- Shermer says he is an AI optimist, citing decades of failed technological doom predictions, and cites Kevin Kelly's point that highly complex systems are harder for bad actors to exploit.
- On UAPs, Shermer says he sits on Avi Loeb's UAP Science Council and estimates roughly 90% of sightings are mundane, remaining puzzled only by WWII-era 'foo fighter' orb sightings.
- Shermer rejects Nick Bostrom's simulation argument, arguing it just pushes the question of ultimate origins back a level.
- Shermer treats Bible stories like Jonah and the resurrection as conveying literary/allegorical truths rather than literal historical fact.
- He shares a personal anomalous experience: a dead radio spontaneously playing music at his wedding, which he says he cannot explain.
