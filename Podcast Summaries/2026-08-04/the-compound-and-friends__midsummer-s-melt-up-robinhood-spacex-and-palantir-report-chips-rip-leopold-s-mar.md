# Midsummer's Melt-Up, Robinhood, SpaceX and Palantir Report, Chips Rip, Leopold's Margin Call — The Compound and Friends

## TL;DR
Josh Brown and Michael Batnick review a blowout earnings week: SpaceX's first public-company quarter beat estimates but the stock fell after hours on lockup-expiry worries; Palantir jumped 30% on 93% YoY revenue growth; Robinhood now has 13 business lines each over $100M in annualized revenue. They also dissect Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund collapse and discuss record S&P net margins and why the rally looks nothing like the 1999 dot-com top.

## Key points
- SpaceX's first quarter as a public company beat across all segments: $962M total revenue vs $835M expected; Starlink $4.29B vs $3.83B; AI segment $2.56B vs $2.18B. Stock fell about 8% after hours on 34% short interest and upcoming insider-lockup expirations.
- Palantir's Q2 revenue grew 93% YoY, Rule-of-40 score hit 155, stock rose 30% giving it a $390B market cap (~67x trailing sales).
- Robinhood now has 13 distinct business lines each generating $100M+ in annualized revenue; margin interest alone contributed $215M and prediction/event contracts generated $156M — more than crypto trading revenue.
- Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund grew from roughly $225M to a peak near $45B before collapsing after prime brokers forced a bulk sale of leveraged positions to Citadel at roughly a 10% discount, in what hosts call possibly the largest trading loss in history.
- Earnings season broadly: 64% of S&P 500 companies had reported with 29% YoY earnings growth; S&P net margins hit 14.7% versus a 12.4% five-year average.
- 88% of Russell 3000 semiconductor stocks entered their own bear market even while the broader index sat only about 3.5% off its high, attributed to a specific hedge fund's forced liquidation rather than broad-based selling.
- Hosts argue the current market looks nothing like the 1999-2000 dot-com top: the S&P 500 advance/decline line is making record highs and gains are broad-based across non-AI names.
