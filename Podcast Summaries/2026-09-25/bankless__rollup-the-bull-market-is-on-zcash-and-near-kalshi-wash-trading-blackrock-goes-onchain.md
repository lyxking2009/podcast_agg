# ROLLUP: The Bull Market is On? | Zcash & NEAR | Kalshi Wash Trading | BlackRock Goes Onchain — Bankless

## TL;DR

Bankless’s Friday ROLLUP on a market where the majors are up modestly but the middle of the market is up 30–50% in seven days, Zcash prints a near 10-year high, Kalshi gets accused of wash trading a million identical $5,500 trades, and the hosts debate whether this is Bitcoin’s cycle or the cycle of revenue-generating tokens.

## Key points

- Market state per the hosts: a bull market, but the question is ‘whose bull market is it’ — the majors up a little while the middle of the market is up 30, 40, 50% in just seven days.
- Even crypto guru Ben Cowan, who had been bearish, has said the bull market is on — Ryan Sean Adams treats that reversal as confirmation.
- Zcash hit a near 10-year high, which David Hoffman calls a fundamental change in the market.
- Kalshi’s wash-trading controversy: a million identical $5,500 trades, covered by the Wall Street Journal and crypto Twitter — the hosts dig into what a prediction market’s volume numbers actually mean.
- Vitalik Buterin blessed a prediction market called TRUO, calling it ‘the prediction market without any of the corpo slop’ — the token rallied on the shout-out.
- Macro backdrop: the 30-year and 10-year Treasury yields are printing their highest levels since around 2004 — ‘not only is it a crypto bull market, but it is also a bond yield bull market, which is bad’, prompting the question of whether Bitcoin cares about high yields (one analysis cited puts the inverse correlation at just 0.18).
- Chart work: Bitcoin’s first leg up took it from ~$63,000 to ~$78,000, and a second leg to ~$85,000; a coin-cycling reset has completed with 37% of all supply now held in the $56K–$92K range by strong hands.
- Moving averages: the 50-week MA sits at $78.8K — the hosts want consecutive monthly closes above it — and the 200-week MA is $65.5K, with this bear cycle never spending much time more than 5–8% below it (versus 35% in the last bear market).
- Rotation evidence: revenue-generating tokens with buybacks and real revenue are outperforming — Delphi Digital noted a basket of revenue-generating assets up ~17% while blue chips ETH, Bitcoin and Solana were down 30% since June, a call that has only become more true since.
- Bitcoin dominance around 59% raises the cycle question: the usual path is early Bitcoin dominance then alts — the hosts compare today to DeFi summer, when the majors were unresponsive while lower-cap assets generated the euphoria before Bitcoin went from $6,000 to $60,000 and Ether from $300 to $4,000+.
- David Feld’s ‘This is not Bitcoin’s cycle’ argument is cited on the side of cash-flow assets over the blue chips.
