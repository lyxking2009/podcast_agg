# The Chopping Block: Vitalik Says ETH Is a Store of Value, the EF's Seppuku License Lawsuit, Jensen Huang and the Alleged Chinese Spy, and McHenry Blames Coinbase — Unchained

## TL;DR
Haseeb, Tom and Tarun take apart Vitalik’s claim that ETH should be valued as a store of value — concluding that if it is, the L2 thesis only works when ETH is money, and that Zcash may have the cleaner store-of-value story.

## Key points
- Vitalik’s argument: ETH is a store of value, not a fee chain. The market’s verdict was immediate — ETH fell about 4% the day he said it.
- Tarun’s objection: a store-of-value pitch reads as giving up on the infinite garden, reducing ETH to “gold 2.1 if Bitcoin is gold 2.0.”
- Haseeb: L2 maximalism only works if ETH is a store of value — otherwise the L2s are parasitic on the L1 rather than accretive to it.
- Tom: Bitcoin earned its credibility by being hard to change, while ETH has revised its emission curve too many times to make the same claim.
- Haseeb’s “leaky bucket”: blockchains struggle to capture the value they create, whereas AI simply charges by the token.
- On quantum, Haseeb argues Bitcoin is far behind and expects a reshuffling of value within two to three years; Tarun prefers Zcash on proof-of-work, privacy and post-quantum security.
- The news segment covers a former EF employee’s lawsuit over the seppuku licence, the Jensen Huang alleged-spy story and who in crypto would get honeypotted, Trump on World Liberty Financial, and McHenry blaming Coinbase for CLARITY.

## Overview
A dense panel episode that moves from the asset thesis (what is ETH for?) through competitive positioning (Bitcoin, Zcash, L2s) into policy and legal news. The interesting disagreement is structural rather than price-based: whether a fee-burning token can survive without a fee narrative.

## Implications
If ETH’s value proposition becomes “store of value,” it competes directly with Bitcoin and Zcash on credibility rather than on utility — a much harder fight. And if L2 value accrual depends on ETH being money, the layer-2 roadmap has a valuation problem built into it.
