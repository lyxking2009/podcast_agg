# How the 1994 World Cup Transformed the Business of Football Forever

## Key Points
- The 1994 FIFA World Cup, held in the United States, fundamentally transformed the business of football (soccer) globally
- Before 1994, football's commercial model was largely European-centric with limited global commercial reach
- The U.S. World Cup demonstrated that football could be commercially viable in non-traditional markets
- The tournament's success led to MLS (Major League Soccer) founding in 1996
- TV rights, sponsorship deals, and stadium economics were permanently altered by the 1994 tournament's commercial success
- The ripple effects shaped how FIFA monetized subsequent World Cups and how clubs approached global expansion
- Connection to current 2026 World Cup preparations and the evolved commercial landscape
