## Implications
The theme — models as training datasets — is one of the most consequential shifts in AI development. If models can effectively train other models (through synthetic data generation, knowledge distillation, or self-play), it breaks the dependency on human-generated data and could accelerate capability gains. Borth's background at NVIDIA's AI Lab and DFKI positions him to discuss both the technical and industrial implications. The St. Gallen perspective adds a European regulatory and business context.
