## Overview
Guy Adami and Danny Moses discuss the current macro landscape, focusing on rising global yields and multiple stress points. Japan's weakening yen and deteriorating bond market are flagged as key risks — Japan is a major US Treasury holder and a potential carry-trade unwind could be disruptive. They note August's tendency for volatility and how attention may shift from strong earnings to macro concerns. AI-driven CapEx is pressuring hyperscaler free cash flows, raising valuation questions. They preview upcoming earnings (Apple, Microsoft, Meta, energy firms), note mixed consumer signals, and reiterate a constructive longer-term view on gold tied to Fed policy.

## Implications
The Japan carry-trade risk is a tail risk that could cascade through global markets if the BOJ continues tightening. The AI CapEx pressure on free cash flow is a key tension — strong earnings growth but at the cost of capital discipline. Gold's 'make or break' framing suggests a technical/structural level that could determine the next major move. The mixed consumer signals (Capital One vs. AmEx vs. retailers) suggest the consumer story is more nuanced than headline numbers indicate.
