- Compute efficiency improved dramatically from $0.40 revenue per dollar of compute in Q1 2025 to $2.08 currently, driven by Claude Code and Claude Co-work.

- Expected IPO terms are roughly $100 billion raised at a $2 trillion valuation, though not officially confirmed; Dan Primack believes the market will support it due to the AI story and index inclusion.
