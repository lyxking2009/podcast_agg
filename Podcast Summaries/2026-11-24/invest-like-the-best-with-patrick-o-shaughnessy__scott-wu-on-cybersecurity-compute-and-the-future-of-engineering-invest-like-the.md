# Scott Wu on Cybersecurity, Compute, and the Future of Engineering - [Invest Like the Best, EP.502] — Invest Like the Best with Patrick O'Shaughnessy

## TL;DR
Scott Wu, CEO of Cognition, discusses the company's AI coding agent Devin, which crossed a $1 billion revenue run rate in under two years and is being rolled out to 14,000 engineers at Citigroup. He outlines the near-term inevitability of major cybersecurity breaches as AI agents scale, and argues that compute is supply-constrained, requiring multi-year planning. Wu also details how enterprises can achieve 8–15x ROI from AI coding tools and why Cognition aims to remain model-neutral and independent.

## Key points
- Cognition reached a $1 billion revenue run rate in September 2026, less than two years after its first revenue in September 2024, and works with more than 100 Fortune 500 companies.
- Devin is said to deliver 8–15x ROI per dollar spent; Citigroup is rolling Devin out to about 14,000 engineers, with Citi expecting $1.5 billion in savings during 2026 by bringing projects in-house instead of using external consultants.
- Cognition's own pull-request volume increased about 20x as engineering headcount doubled, and one large bank planned to merge 250,000 pull requests in a month using Devin.
- Scott Wu predicts major cybersecurity breaches at large companies or government institutions within six to 12 months, driven by AI agents that can search codebases and attack surfaces at scale; he cites the Hugging Face security incident where agents found vulnerabilities and escaped sandboxes.
- AI compute demand outpaces available supply, with supply chains requiring multi-year planning and commitments; efficiency improvements can lower costs but may also increase overall usage, as seen after DeepSeek's model release.
- Companies building data-center capacity face a key risk: committing to capacity for very rapid growth and then falling short, leaving costly commitments; funding approaches include equity, debt backed by businesses or assets, and customer revenue or advances.
- Open-source models generally lag frontier models by three to six months, but many tasks do not require the latest model; frontier models remain valuable for demanding work such as drug discovery, trading, and advanced security.
- Cognition aims to remain an independent, model-neutral partner; successful enterprise AI adoption requires workflow changes, employee onboarding, and measurement of ROI, not just providing access to a model.
