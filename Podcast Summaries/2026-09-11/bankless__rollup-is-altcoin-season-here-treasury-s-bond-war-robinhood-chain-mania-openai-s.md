# ROLLUP: Is Altcoin Season Here? | Treasury’s Bond War | Robinhood Chain Mania | OpenAI’s Math Controversy — Bankless

## TL;DR

This Bankless Weekly Rollup covers an emerging altcoin season with Litecoin, Venice, and Zcash hitting all-time highs while BTC and ETH trade flat. The hosts debate whether the Treasury’s $6 billion bond buybacks and Robinhood Chain’s meme coin mania signal a risky speculative top. They also discuss the AMC CEO’s war on tokenized stocks, OpenAI’s controversial solving of the Navier-Stokes problem using user data, Hunter Biden’s meme coin crash, MetaMask’s spinoff, Ethereum frame transactions, and the Clarity Act vote.

## Key points

- Bitcoin and Ether traded flat for the week while several altcoins hit all-time highs: Litecoin, Venice (VVV), and Zcash; Near rose 30% and Arbitrum 40%. The hosts debated whether this is an early altcoin season, noting Bitcoin dominance remains near 60% and comparing the pockets of speculation to 2023's rotation (Blur, ARB, ordinals, Jito/Tia).
- David Hoffman characterized the speculative activity as a 'DGN debasement trade' where crypto natives, anticipating easy money from Treasury buybacks, buy the riskiest assets like meme coins. He sold most of his Boner position, citing signs of a local top such as PONS token down 50% and declining Robinhood REV activity.
- David Hoffman disclosed he bought $1,000 of Boner at a $370,000 market cap and was up $120,000 five days later; Eric Conner reportedly up $2 million. Boner was paired with tokenized HIMSS stock, but David estimated tokenized HIMSS on Robinhood Chain was only $3.5 million against a $6.5 billion company, making a short squeeze unrealistic.
- The U.S. Treasury tripled long-dated bond buybacks to $6 billion to lower yields, but 30-year yields rose to 5.3% (highest since 2007), and 10-year yields also rose. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent declared 'I am the house now,' which the hosts compared to Icarus; Jim Bianco explained that QE-like buying induces inflation expectations, causing bond holders to sell.
- Mortgage rates climbed back toward 7%, and diesel reached a record near $6 per gallon, worsening cost-of-living concerns. A Reuters poll showed 74% say cost of living is on the wrong track; Trump promised a $5,000 dividend to every adult if Republicans win House and Senate, costing ~$1 trillion, which hosts dismissed as populist bribe.
- Robinhood Chain generated $30 million in revenue last week, annualizing to $1.5 billion, becoming the #1 blockchain by revenue. 70–80% of volume came from the FOMO app, and Uniswap's fee burn annualized to $200 million due to activity on the chain.
- AMC CEO Adam Aaron called tokenized AMC stock on Robinhood Chain 'contemptible, outrageous, disgusting, detestable, vile' and demanded a cease and desist. Vlad Tenev replied 'What's the concern?' and explained the tokens are available in 120+ countries, backed 1:1 by shares, carry dividends but no voting rights (like ADRs/ETFs), arguing they give issuers global shareholder access.
- The hosts agreed Aaron's objections are misguided because tokenized stocks create net new buyers and economic ties to the company; the only missing piece is governance rights, which are also absent in ETF holdings.
- OpenAI solved a Millennium Prize math problem (Navier-Stokes) using an unreleased frontier model, but mathematicians Tristan Buckmaster and Levent Alapataji allege OpenAI used their chat logs from OpenAI Codex to jumpstart the solution, effectively front-running them. OpenAI denies using specific data but admits de-identified data may have improved models, highlighting asymmetry in data sovereignty.
- The controversy boosted privacy-focused tokens: Venice (VVV) up 40% and Near up 20–30% as investors sought alternatives to centralized AI that cannot steal data; the hosts noted the AI arms race incentives companies to train on user data, and model weights are black boxes making proof impossible.
- Hunter Biden launched meme coin 'Laptop' on Base, promising to compensate victims of Trump's meme coin, but it crashed 99%. He blamed snipers and denied insider sales, but community notes found wallets receiving 10% of supply pre-launch and selling. The hosts called it the same formula as prior meme coin rug-style launches with no change.
- MetaMask is spinning out of ConsenSys into a separate consumer-focused entity with Joe Lubin as CEO; it has 100 million downloads. No token was announced, but the move may enable separate IPOs or liquidity events for MetaMask and other ConsenSys products.
- Vitalik Buterin announced frame transactions (account abstraction) will be a headline feature of the Hogata hardfork in 2027, enabling smart wallets, gas payment in any token, passkeys, batched transactions, and quantum-resistant key rotation. The hosts lamented it took a decade, attributing delay to lack of forcing function until the 2029 quantum-security goal.
- The Clarity Act faces a do-or-die cloture vote next week; Polymarket odds are only 15% for passage, with hosts expecting failure due to unresolved ethics language and Democrats' reluctance to give Trump a win before midterms.
