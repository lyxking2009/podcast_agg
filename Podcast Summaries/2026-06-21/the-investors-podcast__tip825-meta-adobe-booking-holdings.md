## Overview

The quarterly Mastermind roundtable features Stig Brodersen, Tobias Carlisle, and Hari Ramachandra each pitching an 'unloved' stock — a company the market has punished due to AI fears. Hari makes the case for Meta as the premier AI advertising platform trading at a discount after a 20% decline. Tobias argues Booking Holdings' capital-light travel ecosystem is underappreciated despite AI assistant threats. Stig pitches Adobe at multi-year lows, arguing high switching costs and subscription revenue make it resilient to AI disruption. The discussion centers on whether the market is correctly pricing AI disruption risk or overreacting to threats that established moats can absorb.

## Implications

Investors should evaluate whether AI fears are creating buying opportunities in quality businesses with durable moats. Meta's advertising moat, Booking's network effects, and Adobe's switching costs may be more resilient than the market currently prices. The key question is whether AI disruption will be gradual (moats hold) or sudden (moats collapse) — the roundtable leans toward gradual. Valuation discipline matters: even good businesses at bad prices can underperform.
