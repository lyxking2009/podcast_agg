# Meta & Microsoft's Claude Slowdown, His Agent Leaked His Banking Info, Don't Bully Your AI


## Key Points

- Meta and Microsoft pull back on their Claude commitments - a fresh signal that Big Tech budgets for third-party frontier models are being reined in.
- Debate over whether 'standard' models (the roundup names Muse Spark) are now good enough for most tasks, reducing the premium closed-frontier models can charge.
- Question of whether Anthropic is exposed if its largest customers cut spending significantly, with Claude Code downloads reportedly slowing ahead of Anthropic's IPO.
- Elon says his 'Grok Bot' will route to all models including Anthropic's; the hosts joke that Elon is firmly 'in the Harness Hive.'
- Agent failure stories dominate: 'Muse' books the wrong doctor, and Grok Bot leaks co-host Shane Mac's banking details.
- Anthropic bans users who persistently bully Claude; Google ships a new Gemini release.

## Overview

Ranjan Roy returns for Big Technology's weekly news roundtable. The through-line is a recalibration of Big Tech's dependence on Anthropic's Claude: both Meta and Microsoft are described as dialing back their exposure, just as questions mount over whether commodity models are adequate and whether Anthropic's top-customer concentration is a risk into its IPO. The back half turns to agent reliability and safety - a leaked banking detail, a mis-booked medical appointment, and Anthropic's new anti-abuse policy - plus a fresh Gemini drop from Google.


## Implications

Practitioners should read the Meta/Microsoft pullback as evidence that model choice is becoming a cost and strategy decision, not a default to the frontier. The agent misfires (banking leak, wrong booking) reinforce that guardrails and human checkpoints still matter for anything touching money or institutions.
