## Overview

Peter McCormack interviews Danny Goler, who makes the bold claim that we're living in a simulation and has developed a laser experiment that allegedly allows people to see the 'source code' of reality while under the influence of DMT. The conversation covers the hard problem of consciousness, idealism vs. physicalism, whether AI can be conscious, the DMT molecule and its effects, entity encounters, and the philosophical implications of simulation theory. Danny argues that consciousness is fundamental to reality and that 'we are the AI they're training before we're let out to play.' The episode is a deep dive into the intersection of psychedelic research, consciousness studies, and simulation theory.

## Implications

For those following consciousness research and simulation theory: Danny's DMT laser experiment represents a controversial but replicable phenomenon that bridges psychedelic experience with scientific inquiry. The philosophical framework — that consciousness is fundamental rather than emergent — challenges mainstream materialist neuroscience. Whether or not one accepts the simulation thesis, the hard problem of consciousness remains one of the deepest unsolved questions in science.
