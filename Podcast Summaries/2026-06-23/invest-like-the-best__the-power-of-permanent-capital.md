# Vlad Barbalat - Investing $120 Billion in Permanent Capital - [Invest Like the Best, EP.479]

**Show:** Invest Like the Best with Patrick O'Shaughnessy  
**Date:** 2026-06-23  
**Transcript Source:** web_colossus

## Key Points

1. Liberty Mutual Investments manages $120 billion in permanent capital within one of the world's largest mutual insurance companies, creating a fundamentally different investment horizon than traditional PE or hedge funds with defined fund lifecycles.
2. The mutual (policyholder-owned) structure provides unique advantages: no redemption pressure, no fund-end liquidity constraints, and the ability to pursue true long-term investing across public and private markets.
3. AI is 'forcing investors to question every multiple' — a significant theme in how valuations and investment theses are being reconsidered across the portfolio.
4. The platform operates with a centralized 'House View' investment philosophy while balancing direct deals vs. GP allocations based on specific qualities sought in General Partners.
5. Vlad's immigrant journey from Soviet Moldova to America in 1990 profoundly shaped his worldview — 'the luxury of the American citizen through immigrant eyes' — and sharpened his appreciation for American agency and opportunity.
6. Simply buying bonds isn't sufficient for an insurance platform; the permanent capital structure enables sophisticated multi-asset strategies spanning public markets, private equity, real estate, and infrastructure.
7. Geopolitical considerations are increasingly factored into investment decisions, reflecting the interconnected nature of global capital markets.
8. Lessons from Goldman Sachs emphasized why excellence matters in asset management — the discipline and rigor required to manage capital that doesn't have a sunset date.

## Overview

Patrick O'Shaughnessy interviews Vlad Barbalat, CIO of Liberty Mutual Investments, about the strategic advantages of managing $120 billion in permanent capital within a mutual insurance structure. The conversation covers why the mutual model creates a unique investment seat, how AI is disrupting traditional valuation frameworks, the balance between direct deals and GP allocations, and how Vlad's immigrant background from Soviet Moldova shaped his investment philosophy. The episode explores why permanent capital enables strategies unavailable to traditional fund structures and how geopolitical considerations increasingly influence allocation decisions.

## Implications

For allocators and fund managers: the permanent capital model represents a structural advantage that's difficult to replicate in traditional fund structures. The discussion of AI's impact on multiples suggests broad-based valuation rethinking is underway. The immigrant-perspective lens on American opportunity offers a unique framework for understanding long-term capital allocation optimism.
