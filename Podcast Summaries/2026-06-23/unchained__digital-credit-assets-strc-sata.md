## Overview

Laura Shin interviews Matt Cole, Chairman and CEO of Strive, about the recent market stress for digital credit assets STRC and SATA. Cole defends the Bitcoin-backed structured credit model against critics, characterizing the recent drawdown as a liquidation event rather than a fundamental breakdown. The conversation covers the differences between direct Bitcoin ownership, digital credit instruments, and DAT stocks, the debate over Saylor's Bitcoin sales, S&P's junk rating on MSTR, and whether wrapping Bitcoin in preferred stock is a betrayal or an innovation. Cole argues that structured credit solves an income problem fiat can't.

## Implications

For crypto investors: the STRC/SATA stress test reveals the fragility of leveraged digital credit structures in volatile markets. The debate between 'liquidation event' and 'fundamental crack' will determine whether these instruments gain institutional adoption. The distinction between Bitcoin, digital credit, and DAT stocks is becoming increasingly important for portfolio construction in the crypto space.
