# CFTC Chair Michael Selig On Perps, Prediction Markets & Crypto In The U.S

## Key Points

- CFTC Chairman Michael Selig discusses the regulatory roadmap for crypto perpetual futures (perps), prediction markets, and the broader digital asset landscape in the United States.
- The CFTC is partnering with the SEC on "Project Crypto" to establish a unified federal approach to crypto asset market oversight, moving away from the fragmented regulatory environment of recent years.
- Selig announced a new "event contracts rulemaking" aimed at establishing clear standards for prediction markets, and directed staff to withdraw the 2024 proposed rule that would have prohibited sports- and politics-related event contracts.
- The Chairman agrees with SEC Chairman Atkins that "most crypto assets trading today are not securities" and supports a common-sense crypto asset taxonomy distinguishing digital commodities, digital collectibles, and digital tools from securities.
- Prediction markets like Polymarket are discussed as a legitimate price discovery mechanism, with the CFTC moving toward a more permissive regulatory stance.
- The conversation covers the intersection of DeFi protocols, centralized exchanges, and traditional finance in the evolving crypto market structure.

## Overview

## Implications

- The joint CFTC-SEC "Project Crypto" initiative signals a coordinated federal approach that could reduce regulatory arbitrage and provide clearer compliance pathways.
- The crypto asset taxonomy (digital commodities vs. securities) could become the de facto framework for determining which agency has jurisdiction over specific tokens.
