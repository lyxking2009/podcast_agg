## TL;DR
Unchained examines an estimated $100 million or more drained from Ledger hardware wallets sold through authorized Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis. Laura Shin speaks with SEAL 911 contributor Jonty and Mark Karpelès about hidden eSIM implants that read seed phrases off device screens. They trace the attack from September 8 through tampered devices from Metapod, MediaMarkt, and distributor Aqipa, and discuss whether self-custody is still safe.

## Key points
- An estimated $100 million or more was drained from Ledger hardware wallets sold through CryptoBilis, an authorized reseller in Southeast Asia; the devices carried a hidden implant that reads the seed phrase off the screen and sends it out over an eSIM.
- Jonty, founder of Pangolin Intelligence and SEAL 911 contributor, and Mark Karpelès, CEO of Karpelès Lab, join Laura Shin to trace how the attack was found; drains date back to September 8, including a tampered Ledger Flex bought from Metapod at Singapore's airport and two MediaMarkt devices in Europe traced to distributor Aqipa.
- Tether's $10 million freeze is discussed, with Jonty saying recovery could take years.
- Mark argues Ledger's manual sends mixed signals by picturing the device's insides while warning that opening it voids the warranty, then opens a Nano X on camera to show what to look for.
- The episode raises the open question of whether self-custody's best defense against malware has become its newest attack surface.
