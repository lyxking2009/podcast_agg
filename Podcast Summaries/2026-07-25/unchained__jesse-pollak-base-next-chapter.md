# How Jesse Pollak Is Mapping Out Base's Next Chapter After the App Pivot

## Key Points

- Jesse Pollak publicly admitted the Base App's social bet was a mistake — the app was "too close to web2" and didn't reflect the range of assets users want to trade.
- Pollak handed leadership of the Base App back to Coinbase, where Jordan Fish (Cobie) will oversee its development going forward.
- The Base App is pivoting to a "finance-first UX" — layering social features on top of finance rather than the other way around.
- Brian Armstrong's memecoin controversy ($BRIAN token) is addressed — Pollak explains the context and Coinbase's position.
- Robinhood Chain is outpacing Base on daily active users according to Artemis data, creating competitive pressure.
- Base is moving off Optimism's stack onto its own Azul, Beryl, and Cobalt upgrades — a significant infrastructure shift.
- The roadmap targets 20,000 transactions per second under the new B20 stablecoin standard.
- The x402 agentic payments protocol is already handling roughly 90% of Base's transaction volume — AI agents are a major growth vector.
- Pollak argues less than 1% of the world uses crypto, and Base's bet is on whoever builds the trusted rails first.

## Overview

Jesse Pollak joins Laura Shin to address the elephant in the room: the Base App's social pivot didn't work. In a candid conversation, Pollak explains why he's stepping back from app leadership, what went wrong with the social-first strategy, and how Base is refocusing on trading, payments, and AI agents as the path to becoming blockchain infrastructure for global finance. The episode covers Robinhood Chain's competitive rise, Base's infrastructure independence from Optimism, and the x402 protocol's surprising dominance in Base's transaction volume. Pollak frames the pivot not as a retreat but as a strategic realignment toward Base's true north: building the rails for a global onchain economy.

## Implications

Base's pivot from social to finance-first signals a broader industry recognition that crypto adoption will be driven by financial utility (trading, payments, stablecoins) rather than social experiments. The move to its own infrastructure stack (Azul/Beryl/Cobalt) positions Base as a more independent L2 competitor. The x402 protocol's 90% transaction share suggests AI agents may be crypto's first genuine mass-use case — far exceeding human user activity.

- "On the app, my focus is on building Base into the blockchain for global finance" — Jesse Pollak
