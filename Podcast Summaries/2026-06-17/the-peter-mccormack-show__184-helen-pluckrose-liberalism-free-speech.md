## Overview

Helen Pluckrose joins Peter McCormack to discuss the crisis of liberalism and the authoritarian turn in modern politics. She traces how liberal principles — free speech, individual liberty, viewpoint diversity — have been eroded from both the left and right. The conversation covers her academic work, the Grievance Studies affair, and why she believes defending liberal values requires separating them from partisan positions. Pluckrose argues that the current moment demands a recommitment to Enlightenment values and open discourse.

## Implications

- Liberal values (free speech, individual liberty) are under threat from both political poles
- Academic institutions need to recommit to intellectual freedom and rigorous scholarship
- The distinction between liberal principles and partisan politics is essential for healthy discourse
- Viewpoint diversity should be valued as a feature, not a bug, of democratic societies

## Notable Quotes

- "To achieve this, we must be able to separate the political principle of free speech from our desire to argue for what is true and good." — Helen Pluckrose
- "Liberalism was lost because people confused it with their preferred political outcomes"
