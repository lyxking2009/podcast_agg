# Travel Through the Lens of AI with Booking.com CEO Glenn Fogel

## Key Points

- Booking Holdings is investing heavily in AI across its portfolio of travel brands (Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, Kayak)
- Priceline's "Penny" is an AI travel assistant that handles natural-language booking queries — a concrete example of AI transforming the travel customer experience
- The "Connected Trip" vision aims to use AI to seamlessly link flights, hotels, car rentals, and experiences into a single intelligent booking flow
- Glenn Fogel addresses the "OpenAI disintermediation scare" — why Booking isn't worried about being disrupted by AI chatbots that could bypass OTAs
- Alternative accommodations (competing with Airbnb) are a growing focus, with AI helping match travelers to the right properties
- Building startups inside a $150B company — Booking has internal innovation labs that operate like startups
- AI regulation varies dramatically across countries, creating complexity for global travel platforms
- M&A strategy focuses on companies that enhance the connected trip experience

## Overview

Glenn Fogel, CEO of Booking Holdings ($170B travel empire), discusses how AI is transforming the travel industry. The conversation covers Priceline's Penny AI assistant, the Connected Trip vision that uses AI to link all travel components, and why Booking isn't threatened by AI chatbots potentially disintermediating online travel agencies. Fogel explains how AI learnings are shared across Booking's portfolio of brands while maintaining brand autonomy, and discusses the regulatory landscape for AI in travel across different countries.

## Implications

- Online travel agencies with proprietary data and booking infrastructure have a moat against pure AI chatbot disruption
- The "Connected Trip" concept — AI orchestrating entire travel experiences — could reshape how consumers plan and book travel
- Internal startup incubation within large travel companies is a viable innovation strategy

## Notable Quotes

- "We're not worried about being disrupted by AI — we're the ones deploying it." — Glenn Fogel (paraphrased)
