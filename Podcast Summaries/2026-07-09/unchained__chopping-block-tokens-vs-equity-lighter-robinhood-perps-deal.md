# The Chopping Block: Tokens vs Equity, Lighter's Robinhood Perps Deal, and Trump's $2.4B Crypto Windfall

## Key Points

- Vladimir Novakovski of Lighter joins to discuss one of crypto's oldest debates: what happens when tokens and equity coexist in the same capital structure
- Robinhood selected Lighter to power its perpetual futures offering — a major validation of Lighter's zero-fee trading model
- The crew debates whether tokens can ever function alongside equity without one cannibalizing the other
- Trump's $2.4B crypto windfall is discussed in the context of political figures entering crypto
- Lighter's design tradeoffs: less-aggressive ADL (auto-deleveraging) approach, insurance fund buffers, and trader-friendly risk parameters
- Zero-fee perps debate: Lighter's free retail tier + paid pro tier raises the question of whether crypto is headed for a Robinhood-style fee reset
- TVL is the most honest signal of trust in perp exchanges, especially during market stress
- RWAs and FX perps are outperforming expectations — Euro and index perps are gaining traction
- Token launches vs equity: the crew discusses how crypto M&A (acquihires of Axelar, Tensor) leave tokenholders behind

## Overview

The Chopping Block crew (Haseeb Qureshi, Robert Leshner, Tarun Chitra, Tom Schmidt) welcome Vladimir Novakovski of Lighter to debate the tokens-vs-equity tension in crypto capital structures. The discussion covers Robinhood's selection of Lighter for perpetual futures, the economics of zero-fee trading models, and how crypto M&A deals are increasingly leaving tokenholders behind. The crew also discusses Trump's crypto ventures and the growing RWA/FX perps market.

## Implications

- Robinhood's partnership with Lighter signals mainstream adoption of DeFi-native perps infrastructure
- The tokens-vs-equity debate will intensify as more crypto companies pursue traditional M&A
- Zero-fee perps could compress margins across the perps exchange landscape
