# Why Strategy Dumped Its Biggest Bitcoin Tranche Yet

## Key Points

- Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) sold 3,588 Bitcoin for roughly $216 million — its largest single tranche sale ever
- The sale raises questions about whether Michael Saylor's company has flipped from Bitcoin buyer to seller
- The sale was made to fund the STRC preferred dividend — Strategy's new digital capital framework
- Laura Shin analyzes whether this marks a philosophical shift for Strategy from "never sell Bitcoin" to active capital management
- The Digital Credit Capital Framework raises the STRC dividend to 12%, authorizes $2B in buybacks, and formally permits selling bitcoin at scale
- This is a short-form analysis clip from a longer conversation (full episode with Parker White, CFA of Apyx)

## Overview

In this short analysis clip, Laura Shin examines Strategy's record $216M Bitcoin sale — the largest single tranche in the company's history. She frames the sale within Strategy's new Digital Credit Capital Framework, which explicitly permits selling bitcoin to fund preferred stock dividends. The clip raises the fundamental question of whether Strategy has shifted from a pure Bitcoin accumulation play to an active capital management vehicle, and what this means for investors who bought into the "never sell" thesis.

## Implications

- Strategy's shift to active Bitcoin sales for dividends changes the investment thesis from "leveraged Bitcoin exposure" to "managed Bitcoin fund with yield"
- The 12% STRC dividend yield suggests Strategy is competing for yield-hungry capital, not just Bitcoin bulls
- This could set a precedent for other Bitcoin treasury companies
