## Key Points

- The crypto market structure bill (CLARITY Act) would establish CFTC oversight for digital asset commodities and provide token classification clarity
- The Genius Act has already become law, providing regulatory clarity for stablecoins — a major milestone for the industry
- The bill addresses how digital assets are classified: security vs commodity, which determines which regulator has jurisdiction
- CFTC oversight of spot crypto markets would be a significant shift from the SEC-centric approach of previous years
- The legislation could reshape how exchanges like Coinbase and Kraken operate, providing clearer rules for intermediaries
- Token classification rules would determine which tokens can trade freely vs. which need securities registration

## Implications

Clear regulatory frameworks could unlock institutional capital flows into crypto. CFTC oversight is generally viewed as more crypto-friendly than SEC enforcement. The bill's passage would reduce regulatory uncertainty that has driven crypto companies offshore. Token classification clarity could enable a new wave of compliant token launches.
