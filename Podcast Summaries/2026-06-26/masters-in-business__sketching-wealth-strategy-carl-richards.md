# Sketching Wealth Strategy: Masters in Business with Carl Richards

## Key Points
- Carl Richards is known for his napkin sketch approach to explaining complex financial concepts simply
- The conversation covers behavioral finance and how investors' emotional decisions undermine long-term wealth building
- Richards emphasizes the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it in investing
- The discussion explores how financial advisors can better communicate with clients about risk and uncertainty
- Simple visual frameworks can be more powerful than complex financial models for investor education

## Overview
Barry Ritholtz interviews Carl Richards, the author and financial planner known for his behavioral finance napkin sketches, about wealth strategy, investor psychology, and the art of simplifying complex financial concepts.

## Implications
The behavioral finance gap — knowing vs. doing — remains the biggest challenge for individual investors. Financial advisors who can simplify their message and focus on behavior coaching will add more value than those focused on product selection.
