# Gene Munster Believes It's Different This Time

## Key Points
- Gene Munster argues the AI trade is still in the 'second inning' despite market skepticism
- There's a significant gap between AI hype and actual enterprise adoption that Munster sees as opportunity
- Cheap open-source AI models from China pose a threat to model pricing but not to the overall AI thesis
- Google has achieved the best return on its AI investment so far among big tech companies
- Micron's blowout quarter with 16 strategic five-year customer agreements may signal an end to historic memory chip cyclicality
- The SpaceX IPO one week in and the Tesla-SpaceX roll-up bet are key themes for the Musk ecosystem
- A fatal Tesla crash is now under federal safety investigation, adding risk to the FSD narrative

## Overview
Dan Nathan and Guy Adami are joined by Gene Munster for a wide-ranging conversation covering the AI trade (still 'second inning'), Micron's structural shift, Apple's pricing power challenges, and the SpaceX/Tesla ecosystem. Recorded as the market sold off into the close.

## Implications
The AI trade has more room to run despite near-term skepticism. Google may be the most undervalued AI play. Micron's long-term agreements could signal a structural change in memory chip valuation. The Tesla-SpaceX relationship creates unique cross-valuation dynamics.
