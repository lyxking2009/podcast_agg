# HIGHLIGHTS: Miljan Gutovic - CEO of Holcim

## Key Points
- Europe faces a shortage of nearly 10 million homes, driving massive construction demand
- Holcim is turning sustainability into a competitive advantage in the building materials industry
- The company is spinning off its North American business as a strategic move
- Latin America represents a booming growth opportunity for construction materials
- Gutovic's three leadership rules: control the controllables, fight complacency, and benchmark relentlessly

## Overview
Nicolai Tangen sits down with Miljan Gutovic, CEO of Holcim, to explore transformation in the global building materials industry. They discuss Europe's housing shortage, sustainability as competitive advantage, the North American spinoff, and Latin American growth opportunities.

## Implications
The global construction industry is at an inflection point driven by housing shortages and decarbonization. Companies that can combine scale with sustainability will win. Holcim's spinoff strategy reflects a broader trend of conglomerates simplifying to unlock value.

## Notable Quotes
- "Control the controllables, fight complacency, and benchmark relentlessly." — Miljan Gutovic's three leadership rules
