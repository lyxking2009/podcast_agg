# Why Fable's Shutdown Is a Warning for Every AI Lab: Uneasy Money

## Key Points
- A jailbreak of Anthropic's most powerful model, Fable, triggered US government export controls
- Dario Amodei's response during a call with the administration made the shutdown 'inevitable'
- Anthropic, a company poised to go public, lacked personnel in Washington who could 'speak the government's language'
- The shutdown drew no public backlash — a concerning signal for the entire AI industry
- Coinbase announced agentic payments on Base, tokenized stocks, and a ground-up trading rebuild
- Igloo (parent of Pudgy Penguins) built a financial instrument to list crypto tokens directly on the NYSE as securities

## Overview
The Uneasy Money hosts debate whether Dario Amodei talked his way into Anthropic's Fable model shutdown, what it means for AI labs navigating government relations, and why the lack of public backlash is concerning. The episode also covers Coinbase's major announcements and Igloo's NYSE listing innovation.

## Implications
AI labs need dedicated government relations teams NOW — technical excellence without political navigation is a liability. The Fable shutdown sets a precedent for how the government will handle frontier model incidents. Coinbase's agentic payments and Igloo's NYSE innovation represent the convergence of crypto and traditional finance.
