## Overview
Episode 248 of The Compound and Friends features Michael Batnick and Josh Brown with guests Ryan Detrick and Sonu Varghese of Carson Group. They cover Greenspan's legacy, Fed transparency, AI bubble fears, Micron, market rotation, small caps, inflation, gold, Bitcoin, and the bull market outlook.

The broadening market beyond mega-cap tech creates opportunities in small caps and value. The AI trade may have more room but selectivity matters. Gold and Bitcoin serve complementary roles in portfolio construction during inflationary periods.
