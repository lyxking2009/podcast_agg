## Overview

Larry McDonald, founder of The Bear Traps Report and author of "How to Listen When Markets Speak," joins Danny Moses on RiskReversal Pod to review the macro landscape and assess how several prior calls have held up. The conversation centers on a structural rotation he sees underway — away from technology and passive equity exposure and toward hard assets, energy, commodities, and selective emerging markets. McDonald frames this through the lens of a K-shaped economy where the upper tier benefits from AI capital expenditures and elevated money-market yields while the consumer-facing economy softens.

McDonald argues the conditions are building for a meaningful inflation rebound, driven by oil, summer energy demand, and Middle East tensions, which could accelerate the rotation from tech into commodities. At the same time, rising debt servicing costs limit government options and raise the risk of stagflation.

## Implications

The episode suggests investors should prepare for a prolonged period of commodity outperformance relative to high-multiple technology stocks, with gold miners, uranium, and nuclear infrastructure positioned as the primary beneficiaries of both the AI data center buildout and a supply-deficit-driven commodity cycle. Passive investors face underappreciated risks from index concentration and large incoming IPOs. Emerging markets like Brazil may offer episodic value as election cycles create dislocations, but require active timing rather than passive exposure.
