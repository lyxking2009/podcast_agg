## Overview

This is an introduction episode for the new spinoff series "Hidden History with Dr. Harini Bhat," featured on the Wiser World by Cloud10 feed. Dr. Harini Bhat is a clinical pharmacist and storyteller obsessed with moments in history that still can't be fully explained. The episode previews the series by touching on the mystery of the Mary Celeste, the infamous ghost ship found drifting in the Atlantic in 1872 with its entire crew vanished without a trace.

## Key Points

- This episode introduces the Hidden History spinoff series hosted by Dr. Harini Bhat
- Dr. Bhat is a clinical pharmacist and science storyteller who investigates historically unexplained events
- The featured mystery is the Mary Celeste, a brigantine merchant ship discovered adrift in the Atlantic Ocean in December 1872
- The ship was found with its cargo intact and no crew aboard, with no definitive explanation ever established
- Theories explored include explosive alcohol fumes, navigation errors, sea monsters, and conspiracy theories
- The series treats historical unknowns as open cases rather than dismissing them as myth
- Hidden History is a Rewind Original, powered by PAVE Studios, also available at @hiddenhistorypod on YouTube

## Implications

The Hidden History series represents a growing trend of science-literate storytellers applying rigorous inquiry to historical mysteries. By framing the unknown as "a constant in human experience" rather than a failure of explanation, the show invites listeners to sit with ambiguity rather than seek premature closure on history's enduring enigmas.
