## Overview

On this episode of Odd Lots, Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway speak with Jeremy Grantham, co-founder of GMO and one of the most recognized voices on market bubbles. Grantham shares his framework for identifying when bubbles are about to burst, drawing on decades of research into historical market cycles. The conversation covers valuation metrics, behavioral indicators, the role of narrative in driving prices, and his assessment of the current market environment.

## Implications

- Grantham's bubble identification framework provides a useful lens for evaluating current markets
- Historical patterns suggest certain behavioral and valuation signals precede major market reversals
- The AI-driven market may share characteristics with previous speculative bubbles
- Investors should be aware of the difference between narrative-driven and fundamentals-driven price appreciation
