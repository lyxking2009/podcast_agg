## Overview

Elad Gil and Sarah Guo sit down with Intel CEO Lip Bu Tan to discuss his ambitious turnaround plan for Intel. Tan explains how his startup culture experience informed his approach to driving cultural change at Intel — faster decisions, customer focus, accountability. He discusses the product roadmap centering CPUs for agentic AI, the Terafab collaboration with Elon Musk, strategic investments from Nvidia and Softbank, and the importance of domestic semiconductor manufacturing. The conversation covers physical limits to scaling, advanced materials, and what investors misunderstand about Intel.
