## Overview

Sam Enzer, Partner at Cahill Gordon & Reindel, joins Austin Campbell, Ram Ahluwalia, and Chris Perkins to assess the Fable 5 export ban's legal standing and crypto implications. Enzer draws parallels to Gensler-era regulation by enforcement and warns that without transparent frameworks, export controls could extend to crypto infrastructure. The conversation also covers the SpaceX IPO's crypto-like dynamics and the SEC's proposed Reg NMS rescission as a potential "generational unlock" for DeFi.
