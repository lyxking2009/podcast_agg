# How Robinhood is using the SEC's New Innovation Exemption | Johann Kerbrat — Bankless

## TL;DR

Johann Kerbrat from Robinhood discusses the SEC's new innovation exemption and its implications for tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. He shares metrics showing massive adoption ($2.1B tokenized assets, 70% weekend trading) and outlines plans for voting rights, in-kind redemption, expansion to other assets, and regulatory clarity needed for US perps. Kerbrat also discusses Robinhood's relationship with Ethereum and upcoming AI agentic trading features.

## Key points

- SEC innovation exemption for tokenization is exciting but has limits (5-year exemption, asset/volume caps) and needs more details.
- Robinhood tokenized stocks are backed 1:1 with actual stock purchases, minting tokens only when buying underlying stock.
- Robinhood Chain metrics: $2.1B tokenized assets, $1.5B deposited in apps (Morpho $500M, Athena $330M, Uniswap $300M), 750M transactions, 14M wallets, $50M revenue, $44M profit.
- 70% of stock token trading occurs on weekends/after hours, demonstrating demand for 24/7 trading.
- Price divergence can occur due to weekend news or token supply crunches; Robinhood is onboarding more market makers to improve liquidity and convergence.
- Voting rights and in-kind redemption for stock tokens are on the roadmap but lower priority until adoption increases; in-kind redemption already announced for market makers.
- Robinhood aims to tokenize more assets beyond stocks: gold already live, considering silver, copper, real estate, private equities.
- Robinhood's own stock (HOOD) not yet tokenized due to regulatory complexity as a public company issuing its own token.
- US regulatory clarity needed: innovation exemption helps tokenization, but state-by-state licensing (e.g., New York BitLicense, California) and staking/lending/DeFi reporting still need clarity.
- Perpetual futures with tokenized stocks are available on partners like LIDAR, and Robinhood has an EU perps platform; US perps could come with regulatory clarity.
- Robinhood uses ETH as gas for its L2, holds generated ETH from fees but has no plan to buy ETH for balance sheet; they value Ethereum's security and decentralization.
- Upcoming features: cross-chain swaps in Robinhood wallet without gas fees, improved asset discoverability, and AI agentic trading via RNCP with cap rails.
