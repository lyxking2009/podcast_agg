## Overview
Episode #638 features Rebecca Anderson, Senior Fellow at the McKinsey Global Institute, discussing her McKinsey report on the factors that have powered America's economic success over 250 years. The report identifies natural endowments, a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship, and institutional foundations as key drivers. Timely for the July 4th semiquincentennial.

## Implications
McKinsey's analysis of American economic exceptionalism provides a framework for understanding current challenges.
