**Show:** Unchained

- This is a clip from a longer conversation on Strategy's Stretch rescue plan and Bitcoin's options market.

## Overview
A short clip from Unchained's live show featuring David Lawant of Anchorage Digital discussing the structural challenge facing Bitcoin treasury companies: Bitcoin generates no native yield, creating constant pressure on companies like Strategy that hold BTC on their balance sheets. Lawant argues Saylor's playbook is evolving (not breaking) through preferred stock and options strategies.

## Implications
Bitcoin treasury companies face a fundamental structural challenge: the asset generates no yield, requiring creative financial engineering (preferred stock, options, lending) to generate returns. This pressure will persist and intensify as more companies adopt BTC treasury strategies. Strategy's evolving playbook (Stretch rescue plan) may set precedents for the broader DAT company ecosystem.
