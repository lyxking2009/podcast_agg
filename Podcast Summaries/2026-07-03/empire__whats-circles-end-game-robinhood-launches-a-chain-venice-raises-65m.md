# What's Circle's End Game, Robinhood Launches A Chain & Venice Raises $65M

**Show:** Empire
**Date:** 2026-07-03

## Key Points
- Robinhood launched the public mainnet of Robinhood Chain, a Layer 2 blockchain built on Arbitrum Platform, purpose-built for real-world assets.
- Robinhood Chain is natively connected to Robinhood's onchain users with deep integrations with Alchemy, BitGo, and Chainlink.
- New Stock Tokens enable 24/7 trading on Robinhood Chain in 120 countries (not available in US).
- Robinhood Earn rolling out to eligible US users for decentralized USDG lending.
- Venice raised $65M — discussed in the context of crypto venture and AI convergence.
- Circle's end game discussed — likely IPO and stablecoin market positioning.

## Overview
Empire's weekly roundup covers the state of crypto markets, Robinhood's major blockchain launch (Robinhood Chain on Arbitrum L2 for RWAs), Venice's $65M raise, and Circle's strategic positioning. The episode also covers crypto venture dynamics and the intersection of AI and crypto.

## Implications
Robinhood Chain represents a major TradFi-to-crypto bridge, building purpose-built RWA infrastructure on Ethereum L2. The 24/7 Stock Token trading (outside US) signals growing tokenized securities adoption. Circle's positioning ahead of potential IPO reflects stablecoin market maturation. Crypto venture is active despite market conditions.
