# HIGHLIGHTS: Andrew Forrest - CEO of Fortescue

**Show:** In Good Company with Nicolai Tangen
**Date:** 2026-07-03

## Key Points
- Andrew Forrest, Chairman and CEO of Fortescue, built a mining giant from scratch against a 60-year duopoly.
- Fortescue pledged to run the entire company on zero fossil fuels by 2030 with no offsets — a 'crazy brave Plan A' philosophy.
- AI is being used to run smart energy grids at Fortescue.
- Forrest has disagreements with some of the world's most powerful people over climate policy.
- He opens up about an accident that reshaped his priorities and his philanthropic mission tackling modern slavery and ocean conservation.
- His life advice: 'Be useful, and enjoy it.'
