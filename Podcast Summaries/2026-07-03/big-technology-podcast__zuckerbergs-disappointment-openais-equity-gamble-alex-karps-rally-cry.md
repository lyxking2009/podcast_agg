## Overview
Ranjan Roy from Margins joins Alex Kantrowitz for a wide-ranging weekly tech discussion. Major topics include Zuckerberg's admission that AI agent progress is lagging expectations and Meta's pivot toward selling excess compute, the growing concentration risk in AI (Anthropic/OpenAI as single points of failure), Palantir CEO Alex Karp's challenge to frontier labs, and the debate over whether OpenAI should give the US government equity. Lighter fare includes Taylor Swift wedding speculation.

## Implications
AI agent development is proving harder than expected — even Meta's massive investment isn't producing the desired results. The AI trade has dangerous concentration risk in just two companies (Anthropic and OpenAI). Meta's pivot to selling compute signals a shift from pure AI development to infrastructure-as-a-service. The OpenAI government equity proposal raises questions about the intersection of AI policy and corporate structure.
