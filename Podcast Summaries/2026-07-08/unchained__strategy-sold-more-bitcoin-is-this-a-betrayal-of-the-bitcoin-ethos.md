# Strategy Sold More Bitcoin. Is This a Betrayal of the Bitcoin Ethos?

**Show:** Unchained
**Date:** 2026-07-08
**Transcript source:** web

## Key Points
- Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) made its largest Bitcoin sale ever: 3,588 BTC for $260 million at a loss to fund preferred dividends
- Days earlier, Strategy unveiled a digital capital framework: 12-month coverage rule, hiked STRC dividend, and $10 billion buyback plan
- Parker White of Apyx argues the BTC sale is smart capital management, not a betrayal of Bitcoin's ethos
- Laura Shin presses on whether investors now bet on Strategy's team rather than Bitcoin itself
- Short sellers' calculus and the 2027-2029 convertible cliff (Matt Walsh pegs near $6.7 billion) are key risks
- Apyx's apxUSD, a tokenized yield wrapper around STRC and SATA, depegged to 72 cents during the turmoil
- White defends Apyx 2.0's redemption model against 'free put option' critics and responds to comparisons to Terra/Luna and FTX

## Overview
Laura Shin interviews Parker White, CFA, of Apyx about Strategy's unprecedented $260M Bitcoin sale to fund dividends. The conversation explores whether this signals a philosophical shift from Bitcoin maximalism to capital management, the risks of the convertible debt cliff in 2027-2029, and the stability of Apyx's tokenized yield products. White frames the moves as pragmatic treasury management while Shin probes whether Strategy has fundamentally changed its thesis.

## Implications
Strategy's BTC sales to fund fiat dividends represent a significant philosophical departure from its original Bitcoin maximalist stance. The convertible debt cliff in 2027-2029 could force more sales. Apyx's depeg to 72 cents highlights systemic risks in DeFi wrappers around traditional financial instruments. Investors need to reassess whether Strategy is a Bitcoin proxy or a leveraged financial engineering play.
