# The Perpetual Future Is Here with David Schamis & Jeroen Nieuwkoop of Hyperliquid Strategies

## Key Points
- Hyperliquid is a Layer 1 blockchain built for high-throughput exchange activity, best known for perpetual futures; the protocol's long-term vision is to be the "AWS of on-chain trading."
- Perpetual futures (perps) allow leveraged positions on assets with no expiration or delivery — a structural improvement over dated futures for liquidity and 24/7 price discovery.
- The HYPE token is designed to behave like equity: roughly 99% of protocol fees go to token buybacks and burns, not accumulated as protocol revenue or distributed broadly.
- Hyperliquid Strategies created PURR, a Nasdaq-listed Digital Asset Trust (DAT) via reverse merger, to give U.S. investors regulated access to HYPE without navigating offshore exchanges.
- The DAT accretes NAV per share by issuing stock at premiums (MNAV > 1) and buying back stock at discounts (MNAV < 1) — a closed-end fund arbitrage mechanism that management actively manages daily.
- Hyperliquid's permissionless HIP-3 framework lets anyone launch their own exchange on the protocol — live today with perp markets for U.S. equities, gold, silver, oil, and pre-IPO names including SpaceX.
- Silver reached approximately 2% of global silver trading volume within six weeks of launching on Hyperliquid — early evidence that real institutional flows are arriving on-chain.
- The biggest near-term growth lever is awareness: most financial professionals with decades of experience have still never heard of Hyperliquid.
- U.S. regulatory clarity on perps and DEXes remains the key risk; the Hyperliquid Policy Center is now actively engaged in Washington.
- The protocol runs with only 11 full-time employees by outsourcing functions to ecosystem partners — Hyperliquid Strategies handles TradFi business development and outreach.

## Overview
Dan Nathan hosted David Schamis (CEO) and Jeroen Nieuwkoop (COO) of Hyperliquid Strategies on RiskReversal Pod to explain what Hyperliquid is and why it matters to traditional financial participants. The conversation begins by establishing what a perpetual future is and why the structure is superior to dated futures for most use cases: no expiration means continuous liquidity, no delivery risk, and funding rates that anchor the perp price to spot. Hyperliquid executes these contracts on-chain with the speed and UI/UX that Schamis argues is genuinely comparable to centralized exchanges — something previous DEX iterations never achieved.

The thesis for Hyperliquid Strategies and the PURR DAT is modeled on early Bitcoin ETFs: access friction is the core value proposition. U.S. investors and many institutions cannot buy HYPE directly due to regulatory constraints and offshore exchange complexity. PURR in a brokerage account solves that. Schamis is clear that this access premium will compress over time as regulation normalizes, but argues the question is really whether HYPE itself goes from $30 to $90 as the protocol grows — the MNAV spread is noise in that context.

The broader vision is Jeff Yan's "AWS of liquidity": any two parties with a tradable asset and a market thesis can launch a permissionless exchange on top of Hyperliquid in hours, not years. Pre-IPO perps on SpaceX already trade on the protocol, enabling 24/7 price discovery for private company valuations outside traditional markets. Schamis and Nieuwkoop's explicit role is bridging TradFi to this world — their team's network can access essentially any major financial institution globally, complementing Hyperliquid's lean 11-person core team.

## Implications
Hyperliquid represents the most credible attempt to date to move traditional financial markets on-chain via permissionless perp infrastructure. If silver can reach 2% of global volume in six weeks from a standing start, the long-term case for equities, commodities, and pre-IPO assets trading 24/7 on-chain becomes more plausible. For TradFi participants the relevant question is not whether the technology works — it demonstrably does — but whether regulatory normalization happens fast enough to matter in investment horizon terms.

The DAT structure is evolving into a distinct and more defensible investment category: unlike pure crypto exposure, PURR offers a managed vehicle where experienced capital allocators are actively working to accrete NAV through spread arbitrage and yield generation. This is a meaningfully different value proposition than the passive Bitcoin proxy DATs that preceded it.
