## Key Points

- Hay prices have been rising significantly, prompting discussion on the r/homestead subreddit — one user suggested the poster should go on Odd Lots
- The hay market is fascinating because it's one of the few commodity markets with very little transparency and price discovery
- A 'vibecoded' newsletter (built using AI coding tools) is attempting to bring price transparency to the opaque hay market

## Overview

Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal explore the surprisingly opaque hay market after spotting rising price discussions on Reddit's r/homestead. The episode reveals how a newsletter built with AI coding tools ('vibecoded') is trying to bring transparency to one of agriculture's least transparent commodity markets. The conversation covers hay fundamentals, the challenges of price discovery in fragmented agricultural markets, and how technology is enabling new approaches to market transparency.

## Implications

The hay market episode exemplifies Odd Lots' signature approach: finding systemic significance in seemingly niche markets. The 'vibecoded' newsletter angle demonstrates how AI tools are enabling solo entrepreneurs to build market infrastructure that previously required institutional backing.
