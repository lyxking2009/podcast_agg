## Overview

Neil deGrasse Tyson and co-hosts Chuck Nice and Gary O'Reilly sit down with Alex Honnold — the world's most famous free solo climber — and neuroscientist Heather Berlin to explore the science of fear. The conversation examines how Honnold's brain processes risk differently, what neuroscience reveals about fear conditioning, and whether fear can be trained or is purely innate.

## Implications

The episode offers insights into risk tolerance and decision-making under extreme conditions — themes relevant to investing and entrepreneurship. Understanding the neuroscience of fear can inform how we approach high-stakes decisions in markets and business.
