# SpaceX's IPO Triumph, Anthropic's Fable Fumble, OpenAI's Price War

**Show:** Big Technology Podcast  
**Host/Author:** Alex Kantrowitz  
**Date:** 2026-06-12  
**Duration:** 56:33

## Key Points

- Three major AI/space IPOs expected within the next calendar year: SpaceX ($1.5-2T), OpenAI ($1T+), Anthropic ($1T+)
- Dick Costolo projects SpaceX will eventually buy Tesla, potentially creating a $3T+ combined entity
- SpaceX has a very small float expected, creating high demand and potential for extreme day-one price spikes
- Sam Altman has made over $1T in compute/deal commitments without clear corresponding revenue growth — OpenAI is the hardest IPO sell
- Anthropic is the 'middle of the road' story — more enterprise-focused, consistent messaging, less spotlight pressure
- Data centers in space are 10-12 years away minimum despite Musk's optimistic timelines
- IPO sequencing matters: first mover gets everyone's liquidity, third mover faces capital exhaustion
- The biggest emerging risk is data center backlash from communities resisting massive construction projects

## Overview

Alex Kantrowitz interviews Dick Costolo (ex-Twitter CEO, 01 Advisors managing partner) about the 'most epic run of IPOs of all time.' The conversation covers SpaceX's narrative advantage through Musk, OpenAI's challenging sell given Altman's massive commitments, and Anthropic's enterprise-focused middle ground. Costolo offers unique perspective from having taken Twitter public, warning about post-IPO volatility and the importance of early messaging.

## Implications

The IPO sequencing will determine capital allocation across all three offerings. Anthropic benefits most from going first. The secondary capital effects — massive distributions to university endowments and venture LPs — could trigger a 6-12 month window of excellent venture fundraising conditions.
