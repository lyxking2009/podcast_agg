# Blockworks Acquires Messari

**Show:** Empire  
**Host/Author:** Blockworks  
**Date:** 2026-06-12  
**Duration:** 28:59

## Key Points

- Blockworks acquired Messari for $10M following its Series A extension at $192M valuation
- The deal unites Messari's breadth of data, APIs, and research with Blockworks' on-chain analytics, investor relations, and disclosure products
- Blockworks aims to become the trusted data layer connecting issuers, investors, exchanges, regulators, and AI agents in on-chain markets
- The acquisition addresses crypto's trust problem and the need for standardized data and disclosures
- AI agent-readable infrastructure is a key angle — as trading becomes increasingly agentic, market data needs to flow through APIs that AI can call directly
- The deal marks the first major acquisition following Blockworks' recent Series A extension, which was raised specifically to consolidate crypto's fragmented data market

## Overview

In this special episode, Blockworks co-founders Michael Ippolito and Jason Yanowitz announce and analyze Blockworks' acquisition of Messari. The conversation covers how Messari's investor-side data platform complements Blockworks' issuer-side tools, the strategic vision for becoming crypto's single system of record, and the emerging importance of AI agent-readable infrastructure in on-chain markets.

## Implications

The Blockworks-Messari merger signals consolidation in crypto data infrastructure. The combined platform positions itself as the Bloomberg Terminal of crypto — a single system of record connecting all market participants including increasingly important AI agents.
