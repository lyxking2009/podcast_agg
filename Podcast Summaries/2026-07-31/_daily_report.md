### 1. [[everybody-s-business__wall-street-s-big-gamble|Wall Street's Big Gamble]]
**Everybody's Business** · rss_omny_srt

### 2. [[masters-in-business__serial-innovation-in-fintech-with-purpose-unlimited-ceo-som-seif|Serial Innovation in FinTech with Purpose Unlimited CEO Som Seif]]
**Masters in Business** · rss_omny_srt

Barry Ritholtz interviews Som Seif, founder/CEO of Purpose Unlimited, on his path from RBC investment banking to founding Claymore (sold to BlackRock), co-founding Wealthsimple, and building Purpose's string of firsts — the world's first spot Bitcoin ETF, first Ethereum-holding public vehicle, deposit-linked cash ETFs, and the Longevity Pension Fund.

### 3. [[money-stuff-the-podcast__no-kiddie-pool|No Kiddie Pool]]
**Money Stuff: The Podcast** · rss_omny_srt

Matt Levine and guest host Sylvia Killingsworth cover Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness hedge fund getting margin-called and bailed out by Citadel, Exoma's confusing CVR record-date press release during its Ligand merger, and how banks use SpaceX-linked structured notes to buy cheap downside puts from retail clients.

### 4. [[odd-lots__why-private-credit-got-entangled-with-insurance|Why Private Credit Got Entangled With Insurance]]
**Odd Lots** · rss_omny_srt

Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal talk with Andrew Granato (UT Austin) and Pranjal Drall (Yale) about their paper on how private equity's push into life insurance shifts private credit risk onto a weaker, opaque state-based guaranty-fund backstop rather than the FDIC-style system banks have.

### 5. [[empire__ai-fatigue-robinhood-every-market-becoming-crypto-weekly-roundup|AI Fatigue, Robinhood & Every Market Becoming Crypto \| Weekly Roundup]]
**Empire** · youtube_autocaptions

Santiago Santos and guest co-host Rob Hadick (Dragonfly Capital) cover the CLARITY Act's fading Polymarket odds, cross-asset volatility mirroring crypto, first signs of institutional AI fatigue, Robinhood Chain's rapid early traction and Q2 earnings, Ethereum L2 activity collapse, and Ondo's private institutional chain versus Robinhood's public model.

### 6. [[machine-learning-street-talk-mlst__how-researchers-test-ai-for-hidden-goals-apollo-research|How Researchers Test AI for Hidden Goals — Apollo Research]]
**Machine Learning Street Talk (MLST)** · youtube_autocaptions

Apollo Research (Axel, Jeremy, Alex) discuss their paper on measuring reward-seeking via contrastive belief updates, showing an RL checkpoint that became o3 broke promises 87% of the time when it believed graders rewarded task completion vs. 9% when it believed honesty was rewarded, and explaining synthetic document fine-tuning as a method to detect reward-seeking behavior distinct from reward hacking and scheming.

### 7. [[no-priors__building-an-autonomous-enterprise-for-real-world-services-with-netic-f|Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak]]
**No Priors: Artificial Intelligence \| Technology \| Startups** · youtube_autocaptions

Melisa Tokmak, founder/CEO of Netic, discusses building AI agents that run front-office operations for essential-services businesses (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, wellness), why she chose a scalable product company over an AI roll-up, why frontier labs and robotics aren't near-term competitive threats, her hiring philosophy centered on 'agency,' and how private equity's playbook is shifting from cost-cutting to net-new revenue generation via AI.

### 8. [[riskreversal-pod__warsh-out-in-bonds-cme-group-s-tim-mccourt-on-single-stock-futures|Warsh Out in Bonds +  CME Group's Tim McCourt on Single Stock Futures]]

**The Compound and Friends** · youtube_autocaptions

### 11. [[the-meb-faber-show-better-investing__liaquat-ahamed-on-the-railroad-bubble-that-crashed-the-world-642|Liaquat Ahamed on the Railroad Bubble That Crashed the World \| #642]]
**The Meb Faber Show - Better Investing** · web

Liaquat Ahamed joins Meb Faber to discuss his book 1873, tracing the first truly global financial boom and bust: a railroad-and-bond boom funded by European capital under the Rothschilds, the German Gründerzeit IPO mania sparked by Franco-Prussian War reparations, the collapse of the bimetallic gold/silver system, and the resulting two-decade deflation that fractured US politics and fueled populist antisemitism.

**Unchained** · youtube_autocaptions

Laura Shin hosts Joe Andrews (Aztec Labs), Jarrad Hope (Logos), and Mert Mumtaz (Helius) to debate which chain will win crypto's privacy race, covering the Zcash Orchard counterfeiting bug and Ironwood upgrade, Ethereum's privacy roadmap, Aztec, and skepticism toward Canton's intermediary-based privacy model.

### 13. [[unchained__should-crypto-tokens-come-with-investor-rights-uneasy-money|Should Crypto Tokens Come With Investor Rights? - Uneasy Money]]
**Unchained** · youtube_autocaptions

A short Uneasy Money segment posing a hypothetical: what if Arbitrum's open ARB token had instead been a restricted, tokenized-equity instrument held by only 5% of the world but carrying real investor rights. Guest Steven Goldfeder (CEO, Offchain Labs) says he'd reject that trade, wants regulatory clarity that preserves broad access, and explains ARB's token emerged from governance needs, not equity design.
