## Key Points

- Jenny Just and Matt Hulsizer co-founded PEAK6 Investments in 1997 and have achieved 28 consecutive years without a losing year — an extraordinary track record that spans multiple market crises.
- The couple started on the trading floor and transitioned to building institutional businesses, demonstrating that the discipline from trading is transferable to long-term business building.
- Technology's advance fundamentally transformed trading: what once required human intuition and speed became algorithmic, forcing traders to evolve their edge from execution to judgment.
- In 2012, PEAK6 purchased the company that became Apex Fintech Solutions, driven by a mission to make financial markets "more accessible, more efficient, and more inclusive."
- Risk-taking is framed not as recklessness but as a calculated philosophical stance — both in business decisions and in life choices — underpinning their long track record.
- Financial literacy for younger generations is a personal priority: democratizing not just access to markets but the knowledge to participate meaningfully.
- AI represents the next frontier for investment and business building, and the pair are actively thinking about how to mentor the next generation of entrepreneurs navigating that shift.

## Overview

Recorded at Apex Fintech Solutions' Ascend Innovation Summit, this Standing Table conversation features Jenny Just and Matt Hulsizer, the married co-founders of PEAK6 Investments. Host Dan Nathan draws out the story of how two options traders built one of Chicago's most durable financial firms — 28 consecutive non-losing years — and then parlayed that into Apex Fintech, which now serves as critical infrastructure for retail investing platforms.

The episode covers their trading-floor origin story, the philosophical approach to risk that defined PEAK6, and their thinking about financial literacy, AI, and what they look for when mentoring the next generation.

## Implications

The PEAK6 story is a case study in institutional edge evolving from execution speed (human trading) to technology leverage (algorithmic systems) to infrastructure ownership (Apex). As markets commoditize each prior source of edge, the sustainable advantage shifts to whoever owns the rails. Their 28-year no-loss streak also suggests that risk management discipline — not genius — is the primary driver of consistent returns.
