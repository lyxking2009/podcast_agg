# Jensen Huang – TPU competition, why we should sell chips to China, & Nvidia’s supply chain moat — Dwarkesh Podcast

> ⚠️ Summary based on episode description only — full transcript was unavailable.

## TL;DR
Jensen Huang breaks down Nvidia’s competitive moats—from its chokehold on advanced chip supply chains to its CUDA ecosystem—while addressing the competitive threat of Google’s TPUs, the logic behind not becoming a hyperscaler, the case for selling AI chips to China, and why Nvidia sticks to a single architecture.

## Key points
- Nvidia’s tight control over the increasingly constrained advanced chip supply chain acts as a formidable competitive barrier.
- TPUs are capable but limited by Nvidia’s entrenched CUDA software ecosystem and scale advantages; they are unlikely to dislodge Nvidia in the near term.
- Nvidia refrains from building its own hyperscale cloud to avoid alienating its CSP customers, who are critical to its data center business.
- Jensen argues that selling AI chips to China—even with restrictions—is strategically sound, citing business and security reasons.
- A single, versatile GPU architecture (combined with CUDA) maximizes R&D efficiency and developer lock-in, obviating the need for specialized training vs. inference chips.
- Supply chain bottlenecks are intensifying rather than easing, reinforcing Nvidia’s procurement and alliance advantages.
- Nvidia’s investment strategy prioritizes expanding its entire platform ecosystem over vertical integration into cloud services.
