# Introducing the Wiser World Book Club! — Wiser World

## TL;DR
The Wiser World podcast introduces its new Book Club, a three-month seasonal program that reads one historical fiction, one nonfiction, and one middle-grade book from different parts of the world. Members receive spoiler-free history/culture episodes, weekly multimedia resources, live wrap discussions, and access to a private online community, with no required meetings or pressure. The first season begins February 2027, priced at $65 (early-bird $55 by December 12, 2026), featuring books on WWII Britain, Israeli-Palestinian peace, and Afghanistan.

## Key points
- The Wiser World Book Club operates in three-month seasons, reading three books from different parts of the world: one historical fiction, one nonfiction, and one middle-grade book.
- Members receive a spoiler-free audio episode before each book exploring the history and culture behind the story; weekly extras like maps, recipes, historical photographs, videos, and other resources; a live discussion called The Wrap after each book; and access to a private online community of readers.
- There are no required meetings, quizzes, or pressure to keep up; participants can read one book or all three, with the goal to enjoy wonderful stories, learn something new, and connect with others.
- The first season begins in February 2027 and registration is open. Regular price is $65 per three-month season, with an early-bird price of $55 if you sign up by December 12, 2026. Books are purchased or borrowed separately.
- First three selections are The Bletchley Riddle (secrets, spies, and codebreaking in WWII Britain), The Future Is Peace (true story of an Israeli and a Palestinian working together for peace), and Sparks Like Stars (a moving novel about family, loss, and survival in Afghanistan).
- Wiser World Patreon supporters receive a special discount code for the book club. The regular Wiser World podcast and Patreon will continue. Learn more and sign up at wiserworld.com/book-club.
