## Key Points

- MicroStrategy's sale of 32 Bitcoin broke its core 'never sell' narrative, damaging investor confidence in the company's thesis.
- Dudas holds zero ETH exposure because the market lacks a coherent framework to value Ethereum, with 100+ major stakeholders split between 'money' and 'world computer' narratives.
- Solana has clearer value accrual communication than Ethereum, but is suffering from the decline of memecoin speculation that peaked in early 2025 without sustainable economic activity replacing it.
- Dudas compares Hyperliquid to Tether rather than other L1s, framing its competitive moat around asset quality and the massive no-KYC international derivatives market.
- Hyperliquid's 97% fee buyback model is cited as an exemplary token value accrual mechanism.
- Binance's success without U.S. operations demonstrates that international scale alone is sufficient, validating Hyperliquid's addressable market.
- Effective token design requires two things: programmatic value accrual mechanisms (such as buybacks) and consistent, clear leadership communication.
- AI agents trading crypto assets will outpace agentic payments use cases.
- Visa, Mastercard, and Stripe are moving too fast for crypto-native entrants to displace them in payments infrastructure.

## Overview

Laura Shin interviews Mike Dudas, Managing Partner at 6th Man Ventures, covering his contrarian positions on Ethereum and Hyperliquid. Dudas explains why he holds no ETH due to valuation confusion among stakeholders, while expressing strong conviction in Hyperliquid's fee buyback model and international market potential. The conversation also touches on MicroStrategy's credibility damage, Solana's memecoin hangover, and the trajectory of AI agents in crypto.

## Implications

Dudas's framework highlights a widening gap between crypto assets with clear, programmatic value accrual and those mired in narrative confusion, suggesting capital will increasingly concentrate in projects like Hyperliquid that offer transparent fee distribution and defined market positioning. The dismissal of crypto's payments opportunity in favor of trading infrastructure also signals where sophisticated investors expect near-term agentic activity to generate the most economic value.
