## Key Points

- Michael Saylor sold 32 Bitcoin for $2.5M, coinciding with BTC dropping from $72K to $62K — Qureshi framed this as a market rejection of the sale, arguing large Bitcoin holders cannot sell without undermining the system's confidence.
- MicroStrategy faces a trilemma: skip dividends to Stretch holders, dilute common shareholders, or sell Bitcoin — all options carry serious consequences, and the market signaled Saylor has no clean exit.
- Tom Lee's Bitmine filed for a 9.5% preferred stock offering funded by Ethereum staking yield rather than ETH sales, offering superior optics versus MicroStrategy's leveraged BTC model.
- Tokens like Humanity (+120%) and WorldCoin (+94%) surged despite BTC and ETH declines, attributed to revenue-generating projects showing real price discovery independent of Bitcoin correlation.
- The CFTC approved Bitcoin perpetual futures and allowed Coinbase to connect users to external perp platforms, though Qureshi predicted most volume will remain offshore due to lower leverage caps and regulatory friction.
- Coinbase acquired ENA tokens and partnered with Athena to offer delta-neutral yield products, potentially navigating FIT21 restrictions by generating yield through perps rather than direct stablecoin yields.
- Bitcoin traded at its 200-week moving average, and Stretch traded 5% below peg at $95.30, both signaling broader market confidence concerns.

## Overview

Bankless hosts David Hoffman and Haseeb Qureshi break down Michael Saylor's controversial Bitcoin sale and MicroStrategy's financial trilemma, while examining Bitmine's Ethereum-backed yield strategy as an alternative model. The episode also covers surging altcoin rotation despite BTC/ETH weakness, the CFTC's approval of U.S. perpetuals markets, and Coinbase's partnership with Athena for yield products.

## Implications

The episode highlights a critical stress test for Bitcoin treasury strategies built on perpetual accumulation without exit: if large holders cannot sell without triggering confidence crises, the model depends entirely on sustained market belief. Meanwhile, Ethereum's native staking yield is emerging as a structurally superior funding mechanism for corporate crypto strategies, and the U.S. perps market opening — while significant regulatory progress — may not meaningfully shift volume away from offshore venues.
