## Key Points

- Ryan Zurrer, an early SpaceX investor, outlined the bull case for SpaceX's IPO at a $1.75T valuation, citing Starlink's recurring revenue and Starship's transformative potential, while bears argue the valuation prices in decades of execution perfectly.
- The current bear market in crypto is described as feeling worse than prior cycles because retail participants entered at high valuations with leverage, leading to deeper emotional and financial damage.
- Takeaways from the Money 2020 and All-In Summit conferences pointed to cautious institutional sentiment and a disconnect between crypto narrative and on-chain activity.
- Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) selling Bitcoin was flagged as a significant bearish signal, raising questions about whether the company is facing liquidity pressure or changing its thesis.
- A BTC retrace to $40k was discussed as a realistic scenario given macro headwinds, overleveraged market participants, and weakening demand from institutional buyers.
- DeFi in 2026 is seeing renewed interest in yield-bearing primitives and real-world asset (RWA) protocols, with the panel identifying these as the most compelling on-chain opportunities.
- The aftermath of SpaceX's IPO could redirect capital flows away from speculative crypto assets as mainstream investors gain access to a high-growth technology narrative via public markets.
- The panel noted that regulatory clarity in crypto remains elusive heading into mid-2026, continuing to suppress institutional inflows.

## Overview

Hosts Jason Yanowitz, Santiago Roel, and Rob Hadick, joined by early SpaceX investor Ryan Zurrer, debate the bull and bear cases for SpaceX's landmark $1.75 trillion IPO and what it means for public markets. The conversation shifts to crypto's ongoing bear market, why this downturn feels psychologically worse than previous cycles, and emerging DeFi opportunities in 2026. The episode closes with analysis of Strategy's decision to sell Bitcoin and whether BTC could retrace to $40k.

## Implications

SpaceX's public debut at a $1.75T valuation represents a potential reallocation event for speculative capital, posing a competitive narrative to crypto at a time when the market is already under pressure from deleveraging and macro uncertainty. Strategy's Bitcoin sales and the prospect of a $40k BTC floor signal that the cycle reset may not yet be complete, with DeFi and RWA protocols potentially emerging as the next structural growth area.
