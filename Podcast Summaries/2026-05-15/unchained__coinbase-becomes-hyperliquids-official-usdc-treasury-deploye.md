# Coinbase Becomes Hyperliquid's Official USDC Treasury Deployer; USDH Sunsets

## Key Points

- Coinbase becomes Hyperliquid's official USDC treasury deployer under the AQAv2 framework, managing liquidity across spot, perpetual futures, and HIP-4 outcome-based markets. Coinbase will share the vast majority of USDC reserve yields with the protocol.
- USDH, the Hyperliquid-native stablecoin developed by Native Markets, will sunset. USDC supply on Hyperliquid doubled year over year to ~$5 billion, while USDH stalled at ~$100 million.
- Circle serves as technical deployer overseeing CCTP services and native cross-chain infrastructure, and plans to stake 500,000 HYPE tokens, moving toward becoming a Hyperliquid validator.
- Both Coinbase and Circle have committed to staking HYPE tokens to support AQAv2 activation. HYPE traded at ~$44, up 12% on the day.
- The stablecoin yield sharing model (AQAv2) could become a template for other L1s and perpetual venues, pressuring other ecosystems to negotiate similar arrangements.

## Overview

This Unchained article covers Coinbase becoming Hyperliquid's official USDC treasury deployer and the sunsetting of USDH. The deal, executed under Hyperliquid's AQAv2 (Aligned Quote Asset) framework, represents a major shift in stablecoin economics. Coinbase will share the vast majority of USDC reserve yields with the Hyperliquid protocol, a significant development that could pressure other ecosystems to negotiate similar arrangements. Circle acts as technical deployer and plans to stake 500,000 HYPE tokens, moving toward becoming a Hyperliquid validator. USDH, which launched in September 2025 after a competitive governance vote, is being sunsetted as USDC dominates Hyperliquid's stablecoin ecosystem with ~$5 billion in supply vs. USDH's ~$100 million. Native Markets will facilitate feeless conversions and fiat redemptions during the transition.

## Implications

For DeFi protocols: the AQAv2 model where yield is shared with the protocol sets a new precedent — expect other L1s to demand similar terms from stablecoin issuers. For stablecoin projects: USDC's dominance on Hyperliquid shows that network effects make it nearly impossible for chain-native stablecoins to compete once a major stablecoin is entrenched. For Hyperliquid: the deal adds a defensive moat against Tether entering the ecosystem and generates ~$450k daily in HYPE buy-and-burn revenue.
