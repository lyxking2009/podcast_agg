# Uneasy Money: Why the Broken Pre-IPO Secondary Markets Won't Be Fixed Anytime Soon

## Key Points

- Anthropic and OpenAI are voiding pre-IPO secondary trades, aiming to control their cap tables. The hosts predict enforcement will require a lawsuit to set precedent.
- Synthetic perpetual markets will eventually price every in-demand private company continuously, whether founders want it or not — bypassing traditional restrictions on private company trading.
- AI security threats are escalating: attackers now use local on-device Gemini APIs to dynamically generate malware on the fly, a new and elusive attack vector.
- An AI agent detected a slow-drain crypto attack in 90 seconds that human monitoring missed for 12 hours, demonstrating the growing gap between automated and manual security ops.
- Circle's Arc token is a 'brilliant move for Circle equity holders even if it changes nothing for ETH or Solana.'
- In the Aave/Kelp lawsuit, a lawyer argued every victim must appear in court — a position the hosts say is fundamentally incompatible with how onchain recovery works.

## Overview

This episode of Uneasy Money on Unchained covers the broken state of pre-IPO secondary markets. Major AI companies (Anthropic, OpenAI) are actively voiding secondary trades to control their cap tables, essentially declaring that any unauthorized transfer of shares is void. The hosts — Kain Warwick (Infinex/Synthetix), Taylor Monahan (Security Expert), and Luca Netz (CEO of Pudgy Penguins) — predict this will require a lawsuit to truly enforce. They explore the pre-IPO SPV fraud wave and discuss how synthetic perpetual markets will eventually create continuous market-driven prices for private companies regardless of founder preferences. The episode also covers cutting-edge AI security threats, including on-device API malware generation and the dramatic efficiency gap between AI security agents and human monitoring. Legal updates include the Aave/Kelp lawsuit and Circle's Arc token launch.

## Implications

For private company investors: secondary market investments in hot AI startups face legal uncertainty — companies can and do void trades unilaterally. For DeFi founders: synthetic perp markets for private equity are inevitable, so plan for continuous pricing of your company whether you want it or not. For security teams: the 90-second vs 12-hour detection gap means teams not using AI agents for monitoring are dangerously behind. For crypto legal: the Aave/Kelp court case sets important precedent — onchain recovery mechanisms may not map neatly to traditional legal procedures.
