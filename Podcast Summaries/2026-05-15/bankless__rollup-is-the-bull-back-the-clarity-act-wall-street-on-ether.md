## Key Points

- Markets defy gravity: S&P 500 hit 4th all-time high in 5 days, adding $11 trillion since the Iran war bottom. Despite 3.8% CPI (highest since May 2023), 6% PPI, oil above $102, and consumer sentiment at all-time lows, stocks keep rallying on AI momentum and Trump-Xi summit optimism.

## Overview

The May 15, 2026 Bankless ROLLUP covers a market that is defying gravity against all odds. Ryan and David open with the Trump-Xi summit, where Jensen Huang and Elon Musk accompanied the President to negotiate trade deals, resulting in NVIDIA hitting all-time highs on chip sales to China. Despite 3.8% CPI, 6% PPI, oil above $102, and consumer sentiment at all-time lows, the S&P 500 notched its 4th all-time high in 5 days — adding $11 trillion since the Iran war bottom.

The crypto segment is dominated by the Clarity Act's Senate vote, which compromised by blocking passive stablecoin yield. The hosts debate whether crypto even needs the Act anymore, given existing regulatory clarity. Circle's Q1 earnings reveal staggering USDC growth (+250% transaction volume) and the launch of its ARK L1 blockchain. The Hyperliquid-Coinbase USDC deal marks a major shift — Coinbase becomes Hyperliquid's treasury manager, USDH sunsets, and Hyperliquid's revenue grows 25%.

The biggest story is Anthropic and OpenAI voiding pre-IPO secondary trades — tokens crashed 34-40%, wiping out hundreds of millions in perceived value. The episode closes with ETF mania: Bitcoin ETFs smashing records, new HYPE and Zcash ETFs launching, and the broader question of whether crypto's bull market is sustainable against macroeconomic headwinds.

## Implications

For crypto founders: The Clarity Act's yield compromise means stablecoin business models need rethinking — pure payment rails have smaller TAM than yield-bearing products. For AI investors: pre-IPO secondary tokens are legally flimsy — don't treat them as real exposure to companies like Anthropic or SpaceX. For macro investors: the disconnect between market euphoria and deteriorating macro indicators (CPI at 3.8%, PPI at 6%, yields rising) is the critical debate — something has to give. For DeFi builders: the Hyperliquid model of treasury-managed stablecoin integration could become a template for other chains.
