# How To Build The Future: Paul Graham & Amjad Masad — Y Combinator Startup Podcast

## TL;DR
Paul Graham interviews Amjad Masad, CEO of Replit, about the company's journey to AI coding agents. Replit shipped an agent in 2024 that can turn plain English into working software, and its technology is now used by large companies to replace multi-million-dollar projects with low-cost Replit implementations. They discuss Replit's early positioning, the future beyond vibe coding, and Masad's vision of a self-driving company where AI agents build and operate software.

## Key points
- In 2024, Replit shipped an AI agent that could take a request in plain English, work through errors, provision its own infrastructure, and keep going until the software actually worked.
- Model companies reached out to ask how Replit had pulled off building such an agent.
- Amjad Masad had been thinking about making programming radically more accessible since 2008, and Replit’s original pitch deck predicted AI-generated code before the Transformer paper was published.
- Replit's technology is moving into banks, manufacturers, and other large companies, where Amjad says teams are replacing software projects once scoped at $10 million with $250,000 of Replit.
- The discussion covers why Replit was so well positioned when coding agents arrived, what comes after vibe coding, and the bigger vision of the 'self-driving company,' where AI agents increasingly build and operate the software a company needs.
