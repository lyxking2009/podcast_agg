## TL;DR
Physicist Alex Lupsasca reveals how OpenAI's GPT-5 and subsequent models are dramatically accelerating theoretical physics, from reproducing his best paper in 30 minutes to solving year-old research problems in days. Dubbed 'Vibe Physics', this approach generated 110 pages of novel quantum gravity calculations and proofs, extending the frontier of human knowledge. Lupsasca argues that AI's scientific breakthroughs are largely invisible to casual users, but for researchers, it's a paradigm shift enabling exploration of previously intractable problems.

## Key points
- Alex Lupsasca found that GPT-5 could reproduce his best paper, which originally took a long time to develop, in just 30 minutes, signaling a leap in AI's scientific reasoning capabilities.
- The AI's impact on science is largely invisible to users who only use AI for everyday tasks like writing emails, because the improvements at the scientific frontier are dramatic while mundane tasks show diminishing returns.
- By using a 'priming' trick—giving the model a warmup textbook problem—Lupsasca and OpenAI CRO Mark Chen enabled GPT-5 to solve a problem from a paper published after its training cutoff, demonstrating the model's ability to handle out-of-distribution research tasks.
- OpenAI's AI solved a year-long problem on single-minus gluon tree amplitudes for Harvard professor Andrew Strominger before his plane landed, highlighting the speed of AI-assisted discovery.
- The AI not only solved the problem but discovered an elegant limiting case (the half-collinear regime) and proved the result using a technique unfamiliar to the human researchers, showcasing its ability to innovate.
- In a 'Vibe Physics' experiment, the team prompted ChatGPT to extend gluon research to gravitons, and it produced 110 pages of novel physics, including new calculations and techniques, which were verified as correct over three weeks.
- Unlike vibe coding, which recombines existing patterns, vibe physics extends the frontier of human knowledge by generating genuinely new scientific results, as demonstrated by the gluon and graviton work.
- Lupsasca believes AI enables theorists to explore many more avenues and tackle much more ambitious problems, fundamentally expanding the scope of what a single researcher can achieve.
