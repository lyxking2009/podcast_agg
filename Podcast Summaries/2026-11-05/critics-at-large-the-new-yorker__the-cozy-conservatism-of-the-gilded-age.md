## TL;DR
Critics at Large hosts Vinson Cunningham, Naomi Fry, and Alexandra Schwartz discuss HBO's "The Gilded Age," examining why Julian Fellowes' high-society period drama attracts viewers despite criticisms that it trivializes social tensions. They focus on George Russell, a ruthless capitalist nicknamed "Train Daddy," and question why audiences cozy up to historical billionaires amid modern anti-billionaire sentiment. Schwartz argues the show lets politically opposed viewers indulge in wealth worship, calling it Fellowes' "sneak attack."
