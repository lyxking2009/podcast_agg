# Why Arthur Hayes Thinks the AI Bubble Bursting Could Spark a Crypto Bull Market

**Guest:** Arthur Hayes (CIO, Maelstrom)
**Host:** Laura Shin

## Key Points

- **Governments are aggressively printing money to finance artificial intelligence development and military spending**, which Hayes identifies as the primary market driver currently.

- **The "Tehran toll booth" scenario is now playing out**, where sovereign nations realize dollar assets cannot buy oil when critical straits are closed, forcing structural unwinding of petrodollar recycling.

- **An AI-driven deflationary bust could rival the 2008 financial crisis in severity**, creating conditions for massive central bank liquidity injections.

- **Central bank response to an AI bubble burst will be to inject massive liquidity**, which Hayes views as highly bullish for Bitcoin and crypto assets.

- **Hayes is deploying capital into "maximum disillusionment" in private crypto equity**, specifically targeting Hyperliquid, Zcash, and NEAR as highest-conviction positions.

- **The strategy involves entering positions at points of extreme pessimism** in private markets to capture asymmetric upside when sentiment reverses.

## Overview

Arthur Hayes presents a macro framework connecting global monetary policy, geopolitical shifts, and speculative bubbles to predict a massive Bitcoin bull run. He argues that governments are currently printing money to finance AI development and warfare, creating the liquidity conditions driving current markets. Hayes sees the structural breakdown of petrodollar recycling playing out through his "Tehran toll booth" scenario, where nations realize dollar assets become worthless when geopolitical tensions block critical oil shipping routes. The core thesis centers on an anticipated AI bubble burst that could match 2008's severity. Hayes expects central banks to respond with the same playbook used after the financial crisis: massive liquidity injections to prevent economic collapse. He views this as the ultimate catalyst for Bitcoin, anticipating that such monetary expansion will drive crypto prices substantially higher. Currently, Hayes is positioning for this scenario by investing in private crypto equity during what he calls "maximum disillusionment" periods, when pessimism creates opportunities for asymmetric returns.

## Implications

For crypto founders and investors, Hayes' framework suggests positioning for a major liquidity-driven bull market triggered by traditional financial system stress. Engineering teams and product builders should prepare for infrastructure demands that come with massive capital inflows. For portfolio managers and institutional players, this presents a timing strategy around monetary policy cycles and bubble dynamics. The emphasis on private equity during "maximum disillusionment" periods offers a contrarian investment approach for those with longer time horizons and capital access.
