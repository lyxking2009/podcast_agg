## Overview

Cameron Dawson, CIO of NewEdge Wealth, joins Dan Nathan to discuss her 'Metallica Market' outlook and the fundamental transformation underway in hyperscaler business models. They dig into Google's first negative free cash flow quarter since 2004, the concentration risks of the Mag 7 echoing the Nifty Fifty era, and why semiconductors (19% of the index) are carrying 2026's earnings growth. Dawson lays out the risk that forward earnings estimates may be peaking and what a potential Fed Chair Warsh rate move could mean for markets.

## Implications

The shift from capital-light to capital-intensive AI business models is a structural change that could compress free cash flow across big tech. Semiconductor concentration in the S&P 500 creates single-sector risk. If forward earnings estimates are near their peak, the market's risk-reward is deteriorating. Investors should consider diversification away from AI-heavy mega-cap exposure.
