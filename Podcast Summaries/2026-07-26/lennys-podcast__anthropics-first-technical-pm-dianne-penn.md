# Anthropic's first technical PM on token maxing, the jagged edge, and living in the future | Dianne Penn

## Key Points

- Dianne Penn joined Anthropic in 2023 as the first technical product manager when the product team was just five engineers — a lifetime in AI time.
- The inflection point for Anthropic was training Opus 3 to be better at code generation — people were using models for long-form code writing, not just autocomplete, and Anthropic seized that opportunity.
- Opus 4 and Claude Code created a magical flywheel: the model needed a great product vehicle (Claude Code) for adoption, and Claude Code needed a powerful model (Opus 4) to demonstrate its capabilities.
- Anthropic's product philosophy: 'Evals are the new PRDs' — the way to drive user value is through rigorous evaluation frameworks, not traditional product requirements documents.
- The 'token maxing' philosophy (inspired by Garry Tan's concept): if you spend $100K/year on tokens right now, you're living the way someone in 2028 will live — sweat the tokens as much as you sweat the pixels.
- Golden Gate Claude was a pivotal moment — an interpretability experiment that went viral and helped Anthropic find its identity and cultural voice in the AI space.
- Claude's willingness to push back and disagree is a deliberate product choice — Anthropic values authentic, honest AI over compliant AI.
- Dianne's background in high-yield bond trading at JP Morgan taught her that the best ideas win regardless of who presents them — a philosophy she applies to building inclusive product teams at Anthropic.

## Overview

Lenny Rachitsky interviews Dianne Penn, Head of Product for Anthropic's AI Research and Labs teams, about Anthropic's journey from a scrappy 5-person team to one of the fastest-growing companies in history. The conversation covers Anthropic's early identity crisis, the pivotal moment when they realized coding was the killer use case for LLMs, the synergistic relationship between Opus models and Claude Code, the 'evals are the new PRDs' product philosophy, and the concept of 'token maxing' — building for a future where AI token consumption is orders of magnitude higher than today. Dianne also shares insights on how Claude's personality (including its willingness to push back) is a deliberate design choice, and reflects on lessons from her earlier career as a bond trader at JP Morgan.

## Implications

The AI product management role is fundamentally shifting — traditional PRDs are being replaced by eval-driven development. Companies that understand the model-product flywheel (great models need great product experiences, and vice versa) will dominate. The 'token maxing' mindset suggests builders should be designing for 10-100x current token consumption levels. Anthropic's culture of intellectual honesty (including AI that pushes back) may become a key differentiator as users tire of overly compliant AI assistants.
