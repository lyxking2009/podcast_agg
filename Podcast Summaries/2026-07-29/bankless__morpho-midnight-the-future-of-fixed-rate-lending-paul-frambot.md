# Morpho Midnight: The Future of Fixed-Rate Lending | Paul Frambot

**Show:** Bankless
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Transcript source:** web_search_fallback

## Key Points
- Morpho Midnight is positioned as the successor to Morpho Blue, removing algorithmic/governance-set interest rates and letting the market price rates directly.
- The core primitive is a 'zero coupon obligation' — Paul Frambot argues fixed-rate, fixed-term lending needs its own primitive rather than being bolted onto variable-rate infrastructure.
- A Morpho Midnight market is defined by four components: loan token, collateral asset, maturity date, and permissions.
- Midnight obligations can be traded like bonds, with market price determining the effective interest rate.
- Institutional/TradFi players reportedly view DeFi's variable, algorithmic rate models as 'retail products' and want to control rate and term for a given risk profile.
- Frambot frames Morpho's broader mission as 'collapsing the cost of trust' between decentralized infrastructure and traditional finance.
- Regulatory discussion referenced SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce's comments on vault classification; Frambot says some vault structures could be deemed investment companies and Morpho's job is to keep infrastructure non-custodial.
- Frambot suggests Midnight could eventually enable under-collateralized lending via on-chain trust/identity signals.
- Growth projection: Frambot hopes for over $10 billion TVL on Midnight by 2027, and wouldn't be surprised to see it reach $100 billion.

## Overview
David Hoffman interviews Paul Frambot, founder of Morpho, about the newly launched Morpho Midnight — a fixed-rate, fixed-term lending market structure meant to succeed Morpho Blue. The conversation covers the technical design (zero-coupon obligation primitive, four-part market structure), why institutional capital has been asking for predictable fixed rates instead of algorithmic variable rates, regulatory scrutiny of DeFi vaults, and Frambot's long-term vision of Midnight enabling under-collateralized lending through on-chain identity signals, with bullish TVL projections for 2027.

## Implications
If Morpho Midnight gains traction, it could accelerate institutional DeFi adoption by offering TradFi-style predictable fixed-rate/fixed-term products bridging DeFi lending with bond-like tradable instruments. The regulatory commentary signals ongoing SEC scrutiny of DeFi vault structures. The identity-layer angle suggests a longer-term path toward under-collateralized on-chain lending.
