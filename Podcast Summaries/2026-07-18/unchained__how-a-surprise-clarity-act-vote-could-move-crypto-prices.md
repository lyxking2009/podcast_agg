# How a Surprise Clarity Act Vote Could Move Crypto Prices

**Show:** Unchained  
**Date:** 2026-07-18  
**Duration:** 6 min

## Key Points

- **Polymarket odds on Clarity Act passage have fallen sharply** — from roughly 75% in May 2026 to under 40% by mid-July — as the bill stalled past its original targets and unresolved disputes (including an ethics provision) dragged on.
- **A White House meeting on the Clarity Act's ethics provision was underway during this taping**, framed as one of the last obstacles standing between the bill and a possible Senate floor vote.
- **Andy Baehr's core argument is that a "surprise" vote could move markets more than a well-telegraphed one.** With passage odds having declined so much, much of the market is no longer positioned for the bill to pass — so an unexpected floor vote (or a positive surprise on the ethics dispute) could catch traders under-hedged and produce an outsized price reaction.
- **Ehrlich and Baehr debate whether crypto's recent strong week is a genuine rally or a relief bounce**, weighing improving fundamentals against the possibility that the move is mostly short-covering rather than durable buying pressure.

## Overview

This is a brief clip pulled from the Bits + Bips segment of the Unchained network, in which host Steven Ehrlich (Head of Research at Sharplink) talks with guest Andy Baehr (Managing Director of Asset Management at GSR) about the state of the Clarity Act and its potential effect on crypto prices. The discussion centers on the sharp decline in Polymarket's implied odds of the bill passing — from about 75% in May down to under 40% — against the backdrop of a White House meeting addressing the bill's ethics provision, one of the final sticking points before a Senate floor vote could happen. Baehr's central thesis is that because the market has priced in a much lower chance of passage than it did a couple months ago, an unexpected vote (in either direction) carries more potential to surprise and move prices than a widely anticipated one. The two also weigh in on whether the crypto market's recent gains reflect real strength or a temporary bounce.

## Implications

The episode underscores how much of crypto's price action in mid-2026 remains tethered to the Clarity Act's legislative fate rather than pure market fundamentals. With passage odds nearly halved since May, the "regulatory clarity premium" priced into crypto assets earlier in the year has largely eroded — meaning both a surprise breakthrough (e.g., resolution of the ethics dispute) and a surprise stall could each generate sharper-than-usual price swings, since positioning no longer assumes passage is likely. For traders and investors, this raises the stakes of monitoring the Senate calendar and White House negotiations closely heading into the August recess deadline, since low prediced odds combined with genuine event risk is a setup for volatility in either direction.
