## Overview
The trailer sets up a new investigative documentary series from Bloomberg's Odd Lots/Big Take team: a small Appalachian town becomes the site of a land grab and social experiment. Outsiders with ties to right-wing political movements are purchasing hundreds of acres, with ambitions to build intentional communities ('pioneers in Appalachia') embodying a Christian way of life. Longtime residents — many related to each other, going back generations — describe being caught off guard and are now organizing to resist. The show promises to examine what happens when ideological real-estate projects collide with existing communities, and how a town that never expected to be a test case decides to fight back.
