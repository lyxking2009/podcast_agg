# Is Variational the Next Hyperliquid? | CEO Lucas Schuermann and Justin Bram — Bankless

## TL;DR
Variational is a crypto derivatives platform using a broker-like model rather than an order-book exchange, acting as counterparty and hedging against aggregated global liquidity, including direct TradFi rails for real-world assets. This enables zero-fee trading, order-of-magnitude better spreads on RWA perps/swaps, and a new 'swap' instrument with flat funding. The team, led by Lucas Schuermann and Justin Bram, plans a 32% airdrop of VAR token in Q4 and aims to compete with retail brokers like Robinhood by onboarding non-crypto users through mobile and easier UX.

## Key points
- Variational is a broker-like crypto derivatives platform, not an order-book exchange; it acts as counterparty to every trade and hedges against aggregated global liquidity from crypto exchanges, DEXs, and TradFi rails, enabling zero-fee trading and hundreds of listings.
- Because it routes directly to TradFi for real-world assets, Variational can offer order-of-magnitude better spreads and execution than on-chain order books, which remain thin; it aims to bring TradFi liquidity on-chain rather than rebuild it.
- Variational positions itself long-term against retail brokers like Robinhood and Interactive Brokers, while exchanges like Hyperliquid serve as price discovery venues for institutional/HFT traders; the brokerage model is better suited for retail execution.
- The platform introduces a 'swap' instrument—a bilateral derivative similar to a perp but with flat, predictable funding (carry cost), ability to pay dividends, and direct mapping to TradFi liquidity; it is currently live, with US 100 swap costing about one-fifth of typical on-chain venues and carrying sub-5% bounded funding.
- Variational's ultimate vision is to be a single gateway to global markets: users deposit USDC and trade a portfolio of assets (eventually thousands) with leverage and long/short capability, all on one account.
- The founding team has deep quant and TradFi connectivity: Lucas Schuermann and co-founder Edward ran a $250 billion flow book at Genesis/DCG after a quant hedge fund, spent a decade in HFT/market making, and previously integrated DeFi protocols as a prop trading firm; team includes ex-Jane Street and IMC talent, currently ~25 people.
- Product trajectory: currently in private beta with invite codes and Arbitrum USDC, soon public mainnet, trading API, and mobile app with embedded wallets (Privy/Fun) to onboard non-crypto users; they cite FOMO's onboarding as inspiration.
- On-chain architecture: built on Arbitrum One, Variational is one of the largest gas spenders; all value movement (settlement, clearing, liquidations, PnL, collateral) occurs on-chain, with user funds segregated in isolated settlement pools rather than pooled.
- The OLP (Omni Liquidity Provider) is the internal counterparty and liquidity aggregator that takes the other side of trades, hedges intelligently across global sources, and generates the majority of revenue; the model is described as 'Robinhood and Citadel built on top of each other' to retain value in the protocol.
- Variational is profitable and well-capitalized, with OLP and company operations generating substantial revenue; the team says runway is measured in years/decades and does not rely solely on VC cash.
- Token and airdrop: the VAR token TGE is planned for Q4 after extending the points program; the team announced a 32% airdrop of total supply to points holders, positioning it as one of the largest airdrops in crypto history, following Hyperliquid (~31%) and Lighter (~25%).
- 2027 roadmap includes Variational Pro for institutional OTC derivatives (options, structured products, multi-dealer platform) and expansion of swaps/perps to more assets with 24/7 trading, plus mobile onboarding to attract non-crypto retail traders.
