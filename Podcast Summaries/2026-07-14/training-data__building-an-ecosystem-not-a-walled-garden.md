## Key Points
- Anthropic's platform team is building a layered developer stack for both internal products and external builders, with a roadmap progressing from knowledge and execution capabilities to coordination strategies
- Open ecosystem over walled garden: Anthropic bets on open standards like MCP (Model Context Protocol) and interoperable sandboxes rather than locking developers into proprietary infrastructure
- MCP is handed to the whole industry as a standard, with Anthropic building connectors on top of the spec to enable agents — Claude and non-Claude — to work together across systems
- Self-hosted sandbox partnerships with providers like Modal, Vercel, and Cloudflare are central to the platform's interoperability strategy
- "Strategies" as meta-harnesses: the team frames advanced agent coordination as strategies that assign different roles to tokens, sitting above the execution layer
- Harnesses should be tuned to Claude specifically rather than treating models as freely swappable commodities — model-agnostic abstractions sacrifice performance
- The electricity analogy: transformative infrastructure becomes powerful only when universally accessible, not when controlled by a single company
- Agent coordination is designed to work across Claude and non-Claude systems, reflecting a genuine ecosystem-first philosophy

## Overview
Sequoia Capital's Training Data podcast hosts Katelyn Lesse (Head of Engineering, Claude Developer Platform) and Angela Jiang (Head of Product, Claude Developer Platform) from Anthropic. The episode explores Anthropic's strategy of building an open developer ecosystem rather than a proprietary walled garden. Lesse and Jiang discuss their layered developer stack architecture, the role of open standards like MCP, interoperable sandboxes, and why they believe transformative AI infrastructure must be universally accessible rather than controlled by a single company.

## Implications
Anthropic's open ecosystem approach — standardizing on MCP, enabling interoperable sandboxes, and supporting cross-model agent coordination — positions it as AI infrastructure for the industry rather than a vertically integrated consumer product company. By handing standards to the whole industry while keeping Claude-specific harness advantages, Anthropic aims to grow the overall developer pie rather than win a zero-sum platform battle.
