## Key Points

- **SpaceX HIP-3 perp debut**: Trade.xyz launched a synthetic pre-IPO futures contract for SpaceX on Hyperliquid (using HIP-3 infrastructure) with a reference price of $150/share (~$1.78T market cap). It spiked to $216 (+44%) before settling ~$203, with $33M in trading volume in its first days.
- **Strategic timing**: Pre-IPO perps launch just weeks before the actual IPO — SpaceX's IPO is expected June 17 — making them more like short-dated settlement futures than long-dated speculative instruments (as Ventuals was doing).
- **Why companies restrict secondaries**: OpenAI and Anthropic are voiding secondary transactions to (1) maximize primary round capital by channeling supply into their own fundraise, and (2) minimize liability from managing complex SPV waterfalls at IPO.
- **Derivatives vs. spot tokenization**: Perps are far safer than tokenized spot shares. Tokenized shares risk violating SEC holding period exemptions (6 months), invite regulatory scrutiny, and expose investors to key-man risk from SPV mismanagement. Perps carry only market risk (ADL, price wicks), not counterparty/legal risk.
- **SPV waterfall risk**: In layered SPV structures (direct holder → SPV → sub-SPVs), broker/bank compliance departments may freeze shares at IPO due to uncertain transaction validity — especially if a company has publicly disavowed the transaction.
- **Spectrum of market participants**: SETR ($400B managed), Forge, and Hive for spot secondaries; Trade.xyz, Ventuals, and Entropy (HIP-3) for derivatives; tokenized trust structures on Solana/ETH (charging 20% one-time + 20% performance fees).
- **Robinhood tokenized shares**: Trust-style structure in a legal gray area; tokens are not redeemable until a liquidity event, so value depends on market belief — which collapsed after OpenAI's disavowal.
- **FTX/Anthropic shares**: Sold in bankruptcy court with all transfer restrictions and rights of first refusal waived; these shares likely have the cleanest legal status outside of direct company-approved holders.
- **Activity concentrated on Solana**: Retail crypto-AI crossover audience with high risk tolerance drove tokenized pre-IPO activity there.

## Overview

Laura Shin speaks with Dio Casares, founder and CEO of Patagon (a private neobank helping accredited investors access pre-IPO deals), about the surge in on-chain activity around pre-IPO markets — particularly the SpaceX HIP-3 perpetual futures contract launched on Hyperliquid by Trade.xyz.

Casares explains the mechanics: unlike Ventuals' long-dated ambiguous perps, the new generation of pre-IPO perps launch within weeks of the actual IPO, making them behave like short-dated settlement futures. Hyperliquid leveraged its existing playbook from altcoin pre-markets — launch a pre-market, capture volume, retain it when the asset goes live — and is now applying it to private equities.

The conversation digs into why companies like Anthropic and OpenAI are aggressively restricting secondary trades: they want primary round capital concentrated in their own fundraises ahead of trillion-dollar IPOs, and they don't want to inherit the legal liability of managing hundreds of complex SPV structures. Casares walks through the waterfall risk in layered SPV structures — where broker compliance departments can freeze IPO share transfers if the chain of custody looks suspicious.

A key thesis from Casares: derivatives (perps) are structurally superior to tokenized spot shares for pre-IPO exposure. Spot tokenization runs into SEC 6-month holding period regulations, invites company hostility (jeopardizing cap table access), and introduces key-man risk. Perps offer hedging with only market risk.

Patagon's business model is SPV-based fund structures for pre-IPO deals (Anthropic, xAI, Cohere, Circle, Kraken, Fluid Stack), now expanding into commodities (e.g., Argentine copper mining) and crypto custody.

## Implications

- Pre-IPO perps on Hyperliquid are a preview mechanism for IPO pricing — Wall Street bankers may start watching these markets to calibrate offer prices.
- The IPO wave (SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI all targeting $1T+ valuations) will drive continued growth in pre-IPO on-chain activity.
- Tokenized spot shares face an existential regulatory/company-relations problem; perps are the more durable on-chain instrument for pre-IPO exposure.
- FTX bankruptcy estate's Anthropic shares (sold with full waivers) may represent the cleanest secondary exposure available outside direct investment.
- As SpaceX perp settles successfully, expect Anthropic and OpenAI pre-IPO perp volume to accelerate.
