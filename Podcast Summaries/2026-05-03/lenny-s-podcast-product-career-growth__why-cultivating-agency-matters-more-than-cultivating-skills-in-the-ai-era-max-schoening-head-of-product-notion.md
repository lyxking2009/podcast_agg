# Why cultivating agency matters more than cultivating skills in the AI era | Max Schoening (Head of Product, Notion) — Lenny's Podcast: Product | Career | Growth

> ⚠️ Summary based on episode description only — full transcript was unavailable.

## TL;DR
Max Schoening argues that in the AI era, agency—the ability to take initiative and act—outweighs specific skills. He shares how Notion empowers designers and PMs to code, making the first 10% of projects effectively free with AI. This shift creates vast opportunities for those who adapt, while software quantity outpaces quality, opening doors for thoughtful builders.

## Key points
- Agency is the critical differentiator in the AI era; people who take initiative will thrive.
- At Notion, designers and PMs shipping code has dramatically accelerated product development.
- The “tiny core” theory: great products hinge on a single, focused interaction (e.g., pull request, multitouch).
- The first 10% of any project is now “free” with AI, enabling rapid prototyping and iteration.
- The “SaaSpocalypse” is overstated; incumbents can adapt and AI creates new possibilities.
- Software quantity has exploded, but quality hasn’t, creating a gap for builders who prioritize craft.
- Notion AI succeeded by deeply integrating AI into existing workflows rather than bolting it on.
- Taste in product development is built through rapid, high-volume iteration and refinement.
- Jobs-to-be-done remains a powerful framework for understanding user needs.
