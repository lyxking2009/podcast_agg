## Key points
- Payments sector activity has picked up meaningfully in the past three months, with the space becoming more global and less fragmented as companies seek to own entire steps of the value chain; many payments companies are trading at a discount relative to cash flow, making them likely targets for continued M&A.
- Agentic commerce is set to transform payments by driving transactions that require enhanced security against fraud, while stablecoin is becoming institutionalized and could disrupt traditional banking when paired with consumers' digital wallets.
- Information services companies' success hinges on whether their data is truly proprietary or easily replicable; strategics increasingly value a combination of proprietary data and hardware as a moat against AI-driven competition.
