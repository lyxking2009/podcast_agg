## Key Points
- Lifco AB is a Swedish industrial serial acquirer that has completed 275+ acquisitions since its 1998 founding, compounding free cash flow per share at ~23% CAGR since its 2014 IPO
- The company operates three segments: Dental (21.6% EBITDA margins), Demolition & Tools (24% margins), and System Solutions (23% margins, 54% of revenue), with an overall EBITDA margin of ~22.5%
- Lifco targets acquisitions at ~7x EBITDA in niche industrial markets with €1–20M in sales, specifically avoiding large-cap competitors, tobacco, weapons, fossil fuels, FMCG, and gambling
- Management uses put/call options (14–17% of deal value) instead of equity dilution, preserving capital allocation discipline and aligning seller incentives with long-term outcomes
- CEO Pair Waldemarsson has allocated 12.7B SEK since 2019, creating 6.4 SEK of value per 1 SEK retained — a strong capital allocation track record
- Former CEO Fredrik Karlsson compounded earnings at 25% annually over a 21-year tenure (1998–2019); when fired, the stock fell 10% but Karlsson immediately bought more shares in the open market
- Founder Carl Bennett owns 50% of shares with 69% voting rights, having established the decentralized culture and acquisition discipline through earlier work at Getinge and LickCare AB in the 1980s–90s
- Brock, a Lifco subsidiary, holds 70% global market share in small demolition robots with 30%+ margins — illustrating the niche market leadership Lifco seeks in every acquisition
- Base case 5-year valuation: 17% CAGR return to 580 SEK/share, assuming 4% organic growth, 10–12% total growth with acquisitions, margins expanding to 23.5%, and a 24x EV/EBITDA exit multiple
- Despite Demolition & Tools cyclicality, COVID performance showed resilience: revenue declined only 0.5% while EPS grew 11%, supported by the stable Dental and Systems segments

## Overview
Kyle Grieve and Shawn O'Malley deep-dive into Lifco AB (LIFCO-B.ST), a Swedish industrial serial acquirer with over 275 acquisitions and a 14% annual earnings CAGR since its 2014 IPO. The episode examines Lifco's three operating divisions — Dental, Demolition & Tools, and System Solutions — and how its decentralized structure, disciplined 8-step acquisition process, and founder-led governance have produced durable compounding. The hosts analyze the capital allocation track record of both former CEO Fredrik Karlsson and current CEO Pair Waldemarsson, drawing comparisons to Constellation Software and Chapters Group while noting Lifco's distinct industrial and niche focus. The discussion culminates in a detailed three-scenario valuation (bull/base/bear) with a probability-weighted intrinsic value around 545 SEK, and a consideration of whether Lifco belongs alongside Chapters Group in the Intrinsic Value Portfolio.

## Implications
Lifco's success highlights that serial acquirers can build durable compounding engines by targeting fragmented niche industrial markets where larger players lack interest, maintaining strict decentralization, and keeping acquisition multiples disciplined at ~7x EBITDA. The company's access to 1.6+ million potential European SMB targets suggests the acquisition runway remains long despite scale, though the law of large numbers will eventually constrain returns. For investors, Lifco represents a template for evaluating serial acquirers: prioritize management incentive alignment, capital allocation per dollar retained, organic growth durability, and the size of the addressable acquisition universe relative to current scale.
