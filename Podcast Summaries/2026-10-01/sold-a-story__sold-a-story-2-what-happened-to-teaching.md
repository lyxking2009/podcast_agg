# Sold a Story 2: What Happened to Teaching

## Overview

A three-minute trailer announcing Season 2 of APM Reports' Sold a Story, released on 1 October 2026. The trailer
does not carry substantive reporting; it sets up what follows. Season 1, published in 2022, was Emily Hanford's
investigation into why so many American schools taught reading using a cueing-based approach that cognitive scientists
had debunked decades earlier. Its impact was unusual for a podcast: more than half of U.S. states subsequently passed
laws aimed at the problems it described, and the series won a duPont-Columbia award alongside a Peabody nomination.
Season 2 widens the lens beyond reading to the science of learning more generally, and to why that science so often
fails to reach the classroom.

## Key Points

- This is a trailer, not a full episode - it is the Season 2 announcement published on 1 October 2026 and runs 3 minutes 10 seconds.
