# DEX in the City: The Hosts Sign Off With Their Best Advice for Women in Crypto

## Overview

The final episode of DEX in the City after more than a year of amplifying women's voices in crypto. Hosts
Katherine Kirkpatrick Bos (Chainlink), Jessi Brooks (Ribbit Capital) and Vy Le (Veda) skip the week's news to give the
advice they offer people breaking into the industry. They debate whether lawyers need to be on X at all, argue that
“getting into crypto” now means learning perps, tokens and prediction markets simultaneously, and discuss
Kirkpatrick Bos's view that women in male-dominated rooms often have to be the adult in the room. Le recounts breaking
down in a Senate office days after FTX collapsed - a story she says she had barely told anyone - and what it taught her
about separating the technology from the people who abuse it. Brooks recalls begging the FBI around 2016 to take cases
involving terrorists using Bitcoin. The episode closes on why crypto cannot be your whole identity, the strangest
moments of the past year, and the women and allies the hosts credit for paving the way.
