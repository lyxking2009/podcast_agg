# Jeff Bell & Richard Betts at Please Don't Tell | Standing Table

**Show:** RiskReversal Pod

## Key Points

- PDT (Please Don't Tell) is one of NYC's most iconic cocktail bars — Jeff Bell started as a bar back in 2010 and now owns the place.
- Richard Betts is one of fewer than 300 Master Sommeliers worldwide and has built distinctive brands including Sombra Mezcal, Astral Tequila, and Tequila Komos.
- Premium brands are winning in the spirits industry — consumers are trading up, not down.
- The business of restaurants and hospitality requires balancing craft with operational discipline.
- Building a luxury tequila brand requires authenticity, patience, and understanding of the agave supply chain.
- Younger people are drinking less overall but drinking better — quality over quantity trend is accelerating.
- Social media has transformed hospitality and customer experience — authenticity is the key differentiator.

## Overview

Dan Nathan visits PDT, the legendary East Village speakeasy, for cocktails with owner Jeff Bell and Master Sommelier Richard Betts. The conversation covers the business of premium hospitality, building luxury spirits brands (Sombra Mezcal, Astral Tequila, Tequila Komos), and the trend toward premiumization in alcohol consumption. Bell shares his journey from bar back to owner, while Betts discusses the craft and business of building distinctive spirits brands. They explore how younger demographics are drinking less but drinking better, and the role of social media in modern hospitality.

## Implications

The premium spirits trend continues to create opportunities for brand builders who prioritize authenticity and craft. The shift toward quality-over-quantity drinking among younger consumers suggests premium spirits brands have a long runway. Hospitality businesses that combine unique experiences with strong brand storytelling can command premium pricing.
