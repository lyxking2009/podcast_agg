## Key Points

- Orange Juice raised $40M to launch a permanent capital holding company that acquires, improves, and permanently holds cash-flowing businesses backed by a Bitcoin treasury.
- The strategy differs from existing Bitcoin treasury companies — Orange Juice focuses on diversified, uncorrelated, AI-resistant cash flows with Bitcoin as the treasury asset.
- The idea originated in early 2025, not a recent reaction to the Bitcoin DAT (Digital Asset Treasury) trend.
- The team includes partners from Ecotes Capital plus Ruben and Adrian from other ecosystems.
- A sum-of-parts analysis is the right framework — the cash-flowing businesses have standalone value, and the Bitcoin treasury adds a growth layer.
- Many existing Bitcoin treasury companies are pure-play Bitcoin exposure — Orange Juice differentiates by combining operating businesses with Bitcoin accumulation.
- Retained earnings from the operating businesses are accumulated into Bitcoin over time, creating a compounding effect.
- The approach targets businesses that are 'AI-resistant' — cash flows that won't be disrupted by AI automation.

## Overview

Laura Shin interviews Lyn Alden, co-founder of Orange Juice, about the company's $40M raise and its unique approach to the Bitcoin treasury model. Unlike pure-play Bitcoin treasury companies, Orange Juice acquires and permanently holds cash-flowing businesses, using retained earnings to accumulate Bitcoin. The strategy targets diversified, uncorrelated, AI-resistant cash flows. Alden discusses the team (including Ecotes Capital partners), the origins of the idea in early 2025, and why the sum-of-parts analysis makes sense. The conversation covers how this model differs from existing DATs (Digital Asset Treasuries) and why combining operating businesses with Bitcoin accumulation creates a compounding advantage.

## Implications

The Bitcoin treasury model is evolving beyond pure-play holdings — operating business + Bitcoin accumulation creates a differentiated structure. The 'AI-resistant cash flows' thesis suggests Lyn Alden is thinking about which businesses survive AI disruption. The $40M raise signals institutional appetite for this hybrid approach. This could become a template for other permanent capital vehicles combining real businesses with crypto treasuries.
