# Alex Sacerdote — Investing on the S-Curve

## Overview

Alex Sacerdote, founder of Whale Rock Capital (managing over $17 billion across hedge, long-only, and hybrid strategies), discusses his technology investment philosophy. The firm has compounded at ~44% per year recently. The conversation uses his highest-conviction position, Anthropic, as a case study to explore the entire AI stack and his 20-year refined framework focused on technology S-curves, durable competitive advantages, and underappreciated earnings power.

## Key Points

- **Highest Conviction — Anthropic:** Anthropic is Sacerdote's top conviction position. The S-curve framework helps identify and invest through technology cycles, distinguishing the eventual leader from the pack during the early, steep part of the curve.
- **S-Curve Framework:** The core investment model. Sacerdote applies this 20-year refined framework to identify technology adoption inflection points and invest through cycles.
- **Hardware Renaissance:** The AI build-out is driving a "hardware renaissance," turning commodity hardware (chips, infrastructure) into critical IP. This is a structural shift, not a temporary boom.
- **AI's Threat to Software:** AI agents pose a potential threat to traditional enterprise software business models. The application layer is where the next wave of value creation will happen.
- **Network Effects in the Agent Era:** New network effects are emerging as AI agents become more capable. The competitive moats of Anthropic and OpenAI are still being defined.
- **AI Changing Research:** Whale Rock uses AI internally to change the research process. The firm is a "learning machine" that continuously adapts.
- **Key Risks:** The conversation identifies key risks to the current AI bull case, including overvaluation and the pace of adoption.
