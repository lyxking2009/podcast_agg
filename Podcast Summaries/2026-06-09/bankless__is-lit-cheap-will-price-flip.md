# Is $LIT Cheap? — The Case for Lighter

## Overview

Delphi's Flip and DeFi investor Will Price join David to make the case for Lighter, a ZK-powered Ethereum L2 with zero-fee retail trading, white-glove market-maker service, and the most aggressive token buyback in crypto. The episode argues that LIT is significantly underpriced relative to its platform's capabilities and strategic positioning, especially compared to Hyperliquid (HYPE).

## Key Points

- **Market-Maker Flywheel Model:** Lighter charges market makers for access to trade against uninformed retail flow, rather than charging retail trading fees. This allows free trading for all users on opening and closing positions.
- **Request-for-Quote (RFQ) System:** Allows large market makers to quote prices on demand for assets with thin order-book liquidity. Better fills for traders, no capital tie-up for market makers. Designed to let large offchain institutions plug in and quote assets.
- **Technical Performance:** Operates at ~200ms latency, matching or exceeding Hyperliquid. First exchange to run more than one execution model at once (orderbook + RFQ).
- **Financial Fundamentals:** All revenue used for open-market LIT buybacks. Already handles ~20% of Hyperliquid's crypto trading volume. LIT trades at 20-25x revenue vs HYPE at ~70x revenue. Buyback pace is ~4x faster than Hyperliquid's recent quarter.
- **U.S. Regulatory Readiness:** Lighter is a U.S.-based company, Delaware C-corp, settles in USDC (GENIUS-compliant). Positioned to be among the first on-chain perps protocols to operate in the U.S.
- **RWA First-Mover:** Listed SpaceX pre-IPO market 2 weeks before Hyperliquid, plus markets for Nvidia H100 AI chips and stocks like Dell and IBM.
- **Token Value:** The no-KYC international market is enormous, and asset quality is the moat. Lighter's combination of retail-friendly pricing, superior technical architecture, and U.S.-compliant positioning creates a strong undervalued thesis.
