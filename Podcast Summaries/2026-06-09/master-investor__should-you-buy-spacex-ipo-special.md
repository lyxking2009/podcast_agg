# Should You Buy the SpaceX IPO?

## Overview

A special episode of The Master Investor Podcast exploring the upcoming SpaceX IPO — the largest IPO of all time. Host Wilfred Frost and guests break down the valuation, the investment case, the risks, and what retail investors should know before the listing.

## Key Points

- **Largest IPO Ever:** SpaceX's upcoming IPO is set to be the largest in history, with a valuation potentially exceeding $350 billion. The scale of the offering is unprecedented.
- **Investment Case:** SpaceX's core businesses — Starlink (satellite internet), Starship (deep space), and government contracts — provide multiple growth vectors. Starlink alone could be worth $100B+ as a standalone business.
- **Valuation Concerns:** At IPO-level valuations, much of the upside may already be priced in. The question is whether retail investors are getting in at a fair price or paying a premium for access.
- **Elon Musk Factor:** Musk's involvement is both a draw and a risk. His other ventures (Tesla, xAI, Neuralink) create both synergies and distraction risks.
- **Retail Access:** Unlike typical IPOs where institutions get first access, SpaceX's listing may offer more retail participation, but the high share price could still be a barrier.
- **Comparison to Other Mega IPOs:** Historical mega-IPOs (Facebook, Alibaba, Saudi Aramco) offer lessons about post-listing performance and the importance of entry price.
