# Big-Value Deals Set the Pace in Healthcare M&A

## Overview

An episode of Strategic Alternatives, RBC Capital Markets' M&A podcast, examining the resurgence of large-scale healthcare M&A transactions. The discussion covers the drivers behind mega-deals in the healthcare sector, regulatory considerations, and what's ahead for deal activity in 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mega-Deal Resurgence:** The healthcare sector has seen a resurgence in mega-deals (transactions exceeding $10 billion), mirroring the broader global M&A environment which experienced its most active year for mega-deals by volume in memory.
- **Healthcare M&A Drivers:** Key drivers include consolidation among providers, the push for scale to negotiate better rates, and the desire to integrate technology and data analytics capabilities.
- **Regulatory Environment:** Antitrust scrutiny remains a factor, but the current regulatory environment has been more permissive for healthcare combinations than in prior cycles.
- **Private Equity Activity:** Private equity continues to be active in healthcare services, particularly in areas like physician practice management, behavioral health, and healthcare IT.
- **Outlook for 2026:** The pipeline for healthcare M&A remains robust, with expectations for continued deal activity driven by strategic consolidation and PE platform building.
