# How Tokenized Stocks Could Undercut Interactive Brokers' 77% Profit Margin — Unchained

## TL;DR
Coinbase launched tokenized stocks for Apple, Nvidia, Meta, and Google on Base, with each token backed 1:1 by real shares held in trust, offering beneficial ownership. Aerodrome captured 25% of AMM volume for these assets within days, generating $80 million in volume, and demonstrated after-hours price discovery when Nvidia's post-close earnings pushed onchain prices higher. The move could disrupt traditional brokers like Interactive Brokers, which enjoys a 77% profit margin, and lays groundwork for broader tokenized asset expansion, though US users remain excluded pending SEC clarity.

## Key points
- Coinbase launched tokenized stocks for Apple, Nvidia, Meta, and Google on Base, with each token backed 1:1 by actual shares held in trust by a regulated custodian, using an ETF-like mint/redeem model that confers beneficial ownership.
- Earlier tokenized stock products were largely synthetic representations, debt claims, or derivative wrappers that explicitly disclaimed ownership of the underlying asset, adding friction, risk, and cost.
- Aerodrome, a decentralized exchange on Base, captured about 25% of automated market maker (AMM) volume for these tokenized assets across all exchanges and issuers, with roughly $25 million in 24-hour volume, $80 million since launch, and 5,000 wallets holding them.
- When Nvidia announced earnings after market close, onchain markets continued trading and the Nvidia token price rose above the official close, demonstrating onchain price discovery as a feature for after-hours and weekend trading.
- Tokenized stocks enable anyone to participate in market making onchain, potentially competing with traditional market makers like Ken Griffin, and offer cheaper access than incumbents such as Interactive Brokers, which has a 77% profit margin.
- The launch integrates with at least nine DeFi protocols including Morpho, Euler, 0x, 1inch, KyberSwap, CowSwap, and Wasabi, enabling use cases like lending, leveraged LP positions, and automated token portfolios.
- Chainlink currently provides price oracles for these tokens based on the underlying stock, but the team is working on 24/7 oracle coverage; meanwhile, deep onchain liquidity is intended to mitigate weekend price dislocations.
- US users are not currently eligible to trade these tokenized stocks; Coinbase has applied for an SEC innovation exemption, and a future US version may require KYC for certain benefits like voting, while keeping tokens permissionless.
- Aerodrome redistributes 100% of protocol revenue to token holders, and the new tokenized stocks generated tens of thousands of dollars in fees within days.
- Coinbase and Aerodrome plan to rapidly expand the range of tokenized assets, with Brian Armstrong's goal of bringing 10% of global GDP onchain within five years.
