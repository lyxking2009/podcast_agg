## Overview
Josh Brown and Michael Batnick host Rich Gelfond, the long-tenured CEO of IMAX, for a business-focused conversation about the premium large-format cinema business. Gelfond explains IMAX's remarkable economics — roughly 4% of global screens generating 20%+ of opening-weekend box office on major releases — and how the company positioned itself as the format that studios build their biggest movies around. The discussion covers the crucial Christopher Nolan relationship and the rollout of The Odyssey, the strategic question of why the IMAX network isn't larger given the demand, and where future profit growth will come from. Gelfond's three decades running the company provide perspective on how the theatrical premium format has survived streaming and the pandemic era.

## Implications
- For investors: IMAX's economics show premium formats capturing disproportionate box-office share — a durable niche even in a challenged theatrical market.
- For studios: event films built for IMAX (e.g. Nolan's The Odyssey) drive outsized opening weekends; the format is a marketing and revenue asset.
- For the industry: the constraint on more IMAX screens is a deliberate economic choice — scarcity supports the premium.
