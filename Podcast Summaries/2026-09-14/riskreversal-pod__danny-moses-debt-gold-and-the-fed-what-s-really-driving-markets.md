## TL;DR

Danny Moses joins Guy Adami for a macro conversation recorded September 11, and the framing is that the real driver of markets is not the single narrative most people are trading. Japan is positioned as an epicentre: with the Bank of Japan expected to raise rates, Moses focuses on Japan's capacity to sell over $1.2 trillion of US Treasuries and what that means for US yields against $40 trillion of debt, a $2 trillion deficit and heavy near-term refinancing needs.
