## Overview

Kevin Roose, author of The AGI Chronicles, joins Big Technology Podcast to discuss whether an AI industry rife with personal rivalries, infighting and mistrust can be trusted to develop technology more powerful than humans. Roose spent years reporting inside OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind, and emerged more concerned about AI safety rather than less — he now believes the industry should slow down. The conversation covers what happens if AI systems begin improving themselves, Sam Altman's leadership style, Dario Amodei's motivations, Sergey Brin's return to Google, Microsoft's AI missteps, and the behind-the-scenes drama of OpenAI's boardroom coup.

## Implications

The most useful contribution is that it treats safety concern as an output of access rather than of distance — someone who saw how the labs operate came away more worried, which undercuts the assumption that criticism comes from people who do not understand the technology.
