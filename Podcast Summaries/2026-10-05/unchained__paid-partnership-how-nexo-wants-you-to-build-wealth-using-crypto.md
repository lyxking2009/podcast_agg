# Paid Partnership: How Nexo Wants You To Build Wealth Using Crypto — Unchained

## TL;DR
In this paid partnership interview, Nexo US COO Neil Steinhar discusses the company’s report, The Future of Digital Wealth, which found that while 67% of high-net-worth investors own crypto, less than 5% are fully integrated into wealth-building strategies. He explains Nexo’s wealth platform, including crypto-backed credit lines up to $200 million, and argues that regulatory clarity and wealth transfer trends favor platforms like Nexo.

## Key points
- Nexo, a digital asset wealth platform operating since 2018, reports over 7 billion dollars in assets under management, 10 million users across 200+ jurisdictions, and 1.7 billion dollars in interest paid since inception, according to COO Neil Steinhar.
- Nexo commissioned “The Future of Digital Wealth” report, which introduced a proprietary Crypto Integration Index (CII) measuring five equally weighted dimensions: allocation size, timeline horizon, retirement integration, risk perception, and substitution of traditional assets.
- Across the US, UK, and Argentina, the average CII score was 4.83 out of 10, and less than 5% of surveyed high-net-worth investors were fully integrated, despite about 67% owning crypto.
- Ownership barriers shift with stage: pre-entry and small investors cite volatility as the main deterrent, but once people own crypto, they become more concerned with costs, tax implications, and security, according to the report.
- Age-based analysis shows 94% of 18-25-year-olds own crypto, but integration peaks at 35-44 where 28% of holders treat crypto as a retirement asset; risk perception explains only 13-14% of the variation in integration depth.
- Regional differences: Argentina had the highest crypto ownership but lowest CII, using it short-term to escape fiat volatility; the US had the lowest ownership but highest CII and greatest asset substitution; the UK fell in between.
- To reduce friction, Nexo emphasizes a simple, intuitive platform, human account management for affluent clients, institutional-grade custody via Bakkt, and increasing regulatory clarity like the stalled Clarity Act, passed Genius Act, and recent CFTC guidance on crypto infrastructure.
- Nexo’s crypto-backed lines of credit are over-collateralized, offer conservative loan-to-value ratios, allow loans up to $200 million, and can be instantly automated for amounts under $2 million, providing liquidity without triggering tax events.
- The credit product is used for buying cars, paying tuition, purchasing homes, funding further investments, and lifestyle expenses; Nexo also plans to bring an award-winning Mastercard debit/credit card program from Europe to the US.
- Nexo’s wealth club tiers, based on platform holdings, offer higher yield rates and lower borrowing costs, while branding focuses on experiences via sponsorships of Formula 1, tennis (Dallas Open), and golf.
- In Q2, overall crypto lending was down, but Nexo’s market share increased; it was the first quarter where centralized finance (CeFi) lenders outpaced decentralized finance (DeFi) lenders, which Steinhar attributes to simpler user experience.
- Steinhar cites a CNBC study that $35-106 trillion will transfer from baby boomers to younger generations with high digital-asset conviction, positioning Nexo favorably for long-term adoption.
