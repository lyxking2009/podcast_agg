## TL;DR
This episode features Rob Snyder, author of The Power of Pull, discussing his framework for finding product-market fit from idea to first deals. He explains the PULL acronym (Project, Unavoidable, List of options, Limitations), shares tactics for cold outreach and first sales calls, and advises against design partners. The conversation also covers goblin mode when nothing works and applying the framework inside larger companies.

## Key points
- Rob Snyder introduces the PULL framework, an acronym for Project, Unavoidable, List of options, and Limitations, which describes what makes customers buy. He explains that pull is discovered rather than created, and shares how his company Jump found pull with financial advisors.
- Snyder discusses the origins of strong startup ideas, emphasizing firsthand experience and testing a pull hypothesis with just two slides. He outlines how to recognize real buying intent, use the 'Hail Mary' question to unlock pivots, and avoid common traps.
- The episode covers cold outreach and sales tactics, including benchmarks for outreach, diagnosing what isn't working, running a first sales call, successful pitch tactics, closing deals, and setting pricing using the PULL framework.
- Snyder advises against design partners, arguing that founders should instead sell to a person rather than a company and focus on real customer conversations.
- He describes 'goblin mode' for when nothing is working: revising the hypothesis, designing onboarding to keep customers, learning from churn, and building a repeatable case study factory.
- Finally, Snyder explains how to apply the PULL framework inside established companies and answers lightning-round questions.
