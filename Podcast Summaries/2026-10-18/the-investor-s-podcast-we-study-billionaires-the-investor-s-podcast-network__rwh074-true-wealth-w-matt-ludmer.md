## TL;DR
William Green interviews Matt Ludmer, founder and CEO of Aligned Wealth Management, about his book "The Right Mountain" and his journey from Wall Street adviser to seeking true wealth. Ludmer discusses balancing financial success with fulfillment, relationships, meditation, and practical investing principles. The episode covers topics such as diagnosing life balance, building community, using low-cost ETFs, and finding a career that lights you up.

## Key points
- Matt Ludmer is the founder and CEO of Aligned Wealth Management and a financial adviser to wealthy individuals and families, including clients who have entrusted him with tens or even hundreds of millions of dollars.
- After decades working on Wall Street, Ludmer realized he had misunderstood the meaning of wealth, which led him to write "The Right Mountain: Practical Wisdom for Achieving True Wealth."
- He shares his personal background, including starting out shining shoes and selling frozen food, revering his father as his greatest teacher, and landing an investment adviser job at Morgan Stanley.
- The conversation explores what happens when hunger for success goes too far, how to diagnose where your life is out of balance, and how loneliness led him to create a "heartfelt community."
- Ludmer discusses why many top investors view meditation as a smart investment and how to build rich relationships by meeting people where they are.
- On investing, he explains that his confidence in stocks is built on a century of data, why it is psychologically shrewd to divide assets into three pots, and why he prefers low-cost ETFs to actively managed funds.
- He also addresses how to find a career—and design a life—that lights you up, and why the ultimate prize is unconditional contentment.
