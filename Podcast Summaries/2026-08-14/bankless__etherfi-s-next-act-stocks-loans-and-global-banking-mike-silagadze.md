# EtherFi’s Next Act: Stocks, Loans, and Global Banking | Mike Silagadze — Bankless

## TL;DR
EtherFi is evolving into a full neobank, adding tokenized stocks, metals, and an integrated Aave V4 lending market to its existing crypto card and yield products. The new release aims to be normie-friendly, offering global fiat rails (70 currencies) and conservative borrowing against a diversified portfolio. Mike Silagadze sees EtherFi as the first non-gambling crypto consumer app, targeting the $300B neobanking market with programmatic token buybacks and future privacy and social features.

## Key points
- EtherFi's product evolution began as a yield layer (staking), then added liquid vaults and a crypto credit card, and now with 'EtherFi Summer' introduces tokenized stocks, metals, and an Aave V4 market.
- Tokenized assets: Initially launching with X-stocks (tokenized stocks) and tokenized gold/silver, with plans to become permissionless and support any Ethereum-based tokenized asset.
- Aave V4 integration: Uses a single Aave market (spoke) on Optimism, with risk parameters set by a risk curator; revenue split is 80% EtherFi / 20% Aave.
- Lending market design: Borrowers are EtherFi users; anyone can deposit as lender; conservative risk model with two thresholds (one for borrowing limit, higher for liquidation) to avoid user blowups; historically only a few thousand dollars liquidated out of $200M.
- Fiat rails: Support for 70+ currencies, deposit/withdraw with low fees (~25bps vs previous 3-4%), deposit from multiple chains including Tron, Solana, and Tempo.
- Swaps: Uses Enso for routing and optimal pricing, aiming to be as instant as centralized exchanges and often better priced.
- Programmatic buybacks: Every revenue-generating action in the product programmatically buys back ETHFI token, similar to Hyperliquid, to create trust and align token value with protocol health.
- Future releases: 'EtherFi Autumn' will include more normie-friendly branding and a social layer, e.g., sending money that creates a fully functional account for the recipient.
- On-ramps and end game: On-ramps have improved but vertical integration and eventually a bank charter are needed; ultimate goal is crypto-to-crypto payments, reducing reliance on TradFi.
- Market opportunity: Neobanking is a $300B revenue industry, 300x larger than DeFi; EtherFi aims to attract tens of millions of users, moving crypto beyond gambling.
