# The Chopping Block: threadguy on Crypto's Social Trading War, Trader Celebrities & AI Agents — Unchained

## TL;DR
Haseeb Qureshi, Tom Schmidt, Tarun Chitra, and Robert Leshner host threadguy ("Titan of the Trenches") for a wide-ranging debate on the social-trading war between FOMO and Pump.fun, why traders are becoming crypto's new celebrities, whether the game was easier for OGs, and what AI agents mean for memecoin trading.

## Key points
- threadguy's viral take: "you were day trading against plumbers" — 2017's altcoin seasons were trivially easy vs. today's trenches, where on-chain trading looks like a Fortnite pro match (memescope, wallet trackers, hundred tokens a minute). Robert counters: there were always disasters (Mt. Gox, BitMEX), and pre-stablecoin crypto had brutal counterparty risk (withdrawal limits, kimchi-arb traps) — the game evolved, not changed.
- Social trading war: FOMO (mobile, follow-the-trader) vs. Pump.fun (signing traders/influencers to exclusive deals, ~$20K offers; making ~$2M/day vs. FOMO's $400-500K/day); Uniswap launched a launchpad, Binance is teasing a social app; Robinhood Social does P&L cards. threadguy: "every exchange is going to go really hard at this" — like what Basis tried with Jesse Pollak, wrong time/people.
- Traders as the new celebrities: verifiable on-chain P&L beats influencer soft power — Remis turned $500 into $1.2-2M on White Whale and instantly had 60K followers; the "death of soft power amongst the influencers." Old echoes: BitMEX/FTX leaderboards made Three Arrows famous; Michael Burry is a legend 20 years later with a single monster trade.
- Who wins the social graph: Farcaster lost because "when something crazy happens in crypto, Kobe's first post is on Twitter, not Farcaster" — the 51% problem (like Mixer buying Ninja but Twitch keeping the audience).
- Memecoins are more durable than priced: market cap hasn't rebounded but trading volume has inflected dramatically; "the right tail of memecoins having a foundational niche in finance is super underpriced"; Pump.fun kept making $1M+/day even after the October 11, 2025 (post-1010) collapse.
- AI agents: later segments debate whether AI agents can trade memecoins and whether Gen Z's risk appetite is permanent — a capabilities jump for crypto's next phase.
