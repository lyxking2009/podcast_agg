## Overview

Josh Brown and Michael Batnick dissect whether the AI-driven market rally is a sustainable multi-decade spending wave or a speculative bubble, concluding it is a wave given the profitability and scale of the companies involved. They preview Nvidia's upcoming earnings (79.1B revenue expected, 80% YoY growth), review Google I/O's shift to always-on agentic AI, highlight three speculative space stocks, and discuss Trump's trading disclosures and Michael Burry's contrarian bets.

## Implications

The "wave not bubble" framing supports staying invested in AI infrastructure plays (especially Nvidia) through near-term volatility, while the extreme semiconductor-vs-software performance spread and Michael Burry's contrarian positioning suggest selective opportunities in beaten-down software and international equities. Space stocks remain highly speculative but warrant monitoring as SpaceX's anticipated IPO could catalyze the sector.
