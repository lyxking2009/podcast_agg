# The Chopping Block: FOMO's Co-Founder Defends the Memecoin Trenches, Hunter Biden's $LAPTOP, and AMC vs Robinhood — Unchained

## TL;DR

Robinhood chain's second rally has produced 125,000 active wallets, 5.7 million daily transactions, and $1.2 billion DEX volume, driven primarily by memecoins paired with tokenized stocks like HIMS/BONER and AMC/CINEMA. The panel debates whether this is onboarding real users or merely a new speculative game where retail hands money to arbitrageurs, with Solana's dominance questioned and Hyperliquid-Kraken onshore talks also discussed.

## Key points

- Robinhood chain has surged to 125,000 active wallets, 5.7 million daily transactions, $1.2 billion DEX volume, and 2 million REV in a single day, marking a second explosive rally beyond its initial launch.
- Much of the activity is driven by memecoins paired with tokenized equities, such as BONER paired with HIMS and CINEMA paired with AMC, where minting the memecoin requires buying the underlying stock and locking a portion into liquidity.
- The mechanism effectively uses memecoins as liquidity incentives to mint real-world assets (RWAs); total RWA issuance on Robinhood chain has risen to roughly $70-80 million, up 50% week-over-week, though still tiny compared to $3-4 billion across other platforms.
- Weekend trading caused HIMS and AMC tokenized stocks to deviate significantly from their real-world prices, creating arbitrage opportunities for hedge funds that retail participants are effectively funding when they buy the meme tokens.
- Tarun observed that about 90,000 of 125,000 Robinhood chain addresses originated from FOMO, indicating that memecoin traders are no longer loyal to Solana and will follow whichever application or chain offers the best experience.
- Haseeb criticized the trend as a 'guaranteed loss' mechanism, noting that users think they are making money but are likely to be exploited by arbitrageurs, and referenced 2025 data showing 95% of pump.fun buyers lost money.
- Laura argued the phenomenon is generational and cultural, similar to GameStop mania and 'financial jackass,' where entertainment value and nihilistic fun drive participation despite poor financial odds.
- Tom noted the activity uses no new code—it's a mashup of existing protocols like Ohm forks (NET Capital, Pawns) and Uniswap v4 hooks, but it finally incentivized usage of these older primitives.
- Flood tweeted that Solana is in its most perilous position, as Robinhood chain now ranks #2 by 24-hour DEX volume ($1.5B vs Solana's $2.5B) but still has much lower TVL ($730M vs Solana's $5.7B), suggesting the rally may be concentrated among existing crypto traders rather than new retail.
- Bloomberg reported Hyperliquid Labs is in advanced talks with Payward (Kraken's parent) to use Bitnomial's license and clearing to launch an onshore, KYC-compliant product; CME stock fell and HYPE token rose on the news.
- Experts expect the onshore Hyperliquid product to differ fundamentally from the offshore DEX due to US clearing, market surveillance, and liquidation requirements, similar to how Polymarket US differs from global Polymarket.
- Laura expressed concern that regulated US crypto markets could become walled off like China's internet, while Haseeb argued informal and formal markets will coexist, with retail accessing offshore venues despite restrictions.
