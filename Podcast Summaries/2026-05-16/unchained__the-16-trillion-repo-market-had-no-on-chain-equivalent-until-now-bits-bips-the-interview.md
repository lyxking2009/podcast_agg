# Bits + Bips: The Interview — The $16 Trillion Repo Market Is TradFi's Central Nervous System. It's Finally Coming Onchain

**Show:** Unchained — Bits + Bips: The Interview | **Guests:** Craig Burchell, Matteo Pandolfi  
**Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Duration:** ~45 min

## Overview

This episode of Bits + Bips: The Interview, hosted by Steven Ehrlich, tackles one of the biggest gaps in on-chain finance: the nearly complete absence of the $16 trillion repo market from DeFi. Craig Burchell, Head of Lending at FalconX, and Matteo Pandolfi, CEO & Co-Founder of Pareto, break down how repurchase agreements work in traditional finance, why the 2019 repo crisis exposed fragility in the plumbing, and why bringing repo on-chain is the next big unlock for DeFi. The conversation covers how short-term borrowing secured against Treasuries (the 'oil that makes everything go') can be replicated on-chain with faster settlement, transparent collateral management, and 24/7 operations. Both guests present their views on the timeline and scale of adoption — Matteo predicts $1 trillion in on-chain repo within five years, while Craig provides both the bullish case (clear institutional demand, efficient settlement) and the honest obstacles (regulatory hurdles, legacy infrastructure inertia, adoption friction).

## Key Points

1. The repo market is $16 trillion globally and is the plumbing of TradFi — short-term borrowing secured against Treasuries. It's almost entirely absent from on-chain finance, creating real problems for RWA liquidity, stablecoin swap desks, and DeFi protocols managing redemption queues.

2. Craig Burchell (FalconX) and Matteo Pandolfi (Pareto) explain how repo works, what broke in the 2019 repo crisis, and why repo translates extremely well into on-chain finance — faster settlement, transparent collateral management, and 24/7 operations.

3. Matteo Pandolfi predicts $1 trillion in on-chain repo within five years, citing clear institutional demand, efficient settlement, and the natural fit between repo mechanics and smart contract infrastructure.

4. Craig Burchell offers both the bull case (clear need, efficient settlement, institutional demand) and the bear case (regulatory hurdles, legacy infrastructure inertia, adoption friction) — providing a balanced view of the opportunity.

5. Pareto provides the on-chain credit infrastructure layer, while FalconX brings institutional lending expertise. Together they're building the plumbing for on-chain fixed-income markets.

6. The on-chain equivalent could solve redemption queue management for DeFi protocols, improve stablecoin swap liquidity, and increase RWA collateral efficiency.
