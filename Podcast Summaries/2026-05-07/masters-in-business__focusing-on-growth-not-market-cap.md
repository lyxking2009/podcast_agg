# Focusing on Growth (Not Market Cap) — Masters in Business

## TL;DR
Rob Arnott of Research Affiliates introduces the RAFI Growth Index, which selects stocks based on fundamental growth (sales, profits, R&D) rather than valuation, and weights by dollar growth magnitude. Backtests show 4.5% annual outperformance over Russell Growth across nearly 30 years, with live tracking since March 2024 already beating by 13 percentage points. The index excludes slower-growing giants like Amazon and Microsoft, offering a concentrated alternative to cap-weighted strategies amid concentration concerns.

## Key points
- The RAFI Growth Index selects companies in the top 25% by average growth rates of sales, profits, and R&D spending, explicitly avoiding valuation-based classification into growth or value.
- It weights constituents by the dollar magnitude of their contribution to US economic growth, resulting in top weights (around 10%) for Nvidia and Apple, which have generated the largest absolute growth in sales and profits.
- Back-tested over 28 years, the index outperformed the Russell Growth Index by an annualized 4.5 percentage points, with a win rate of about 7 in 10 years; live tracking since March 2024 shows a 13 percentage point lead.
- Amazon and Microsoft are excluded because their recent growth rates no longer rank in the top quartile, despite their past rapid expansion; Apple qualifies due to sustained percentage growth from a high base.
- The index carries slightly higher volatility than cap-weighted benchmarks and can underperform in typical down years by about 2 percentage points, but it also can outperform by 12 points in good years.
- Estimated capacity for an eventual ETF is $1.5–3 trillion, roughly 10–20% of S&P 500 index assets, given higher turnover and a narrower focus on growth stocks.
- Arnott stresses a hypothesis-driven, scientific approach to index construction that avoids data mining; both the original RAFI value index and the new growth index showed real-world performance consistent with their backtests.
