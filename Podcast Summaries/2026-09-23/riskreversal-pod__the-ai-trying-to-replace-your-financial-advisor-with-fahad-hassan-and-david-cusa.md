# The AI Trying to Replace Your Financial Advisor with Fahad Hassan and David Cusatis — RiskReversal Pod

## TL;DR

Range is an AI-native wealth management platform founded by technologists with no finance background, targeting high-net-worth individuals (average starting account over $750,000) with complex lives. It combines human CFPs, proprietary software, and an AI agent called Rye to provide planning, investment management, tax, and estate services at roughly 90% lower cost than traditional advisors, eschewing the 1% AUM fee. Having crossed $1 billion AUM and approaching $1.5 billion, Range emphasizes trust-building, regulatory compliance, and agentic actions like text-initiated Roth conversions, positioning to capture younger clients (median age 41) and disrupt incumbents.

## Key points

- Range was founded by Fahad Hassan and David Cusatis, both with computer science backgrounds and no prior finance experience, after Fahad's frustrating search for a financial advisor in 2020; the company launched in 2023 and has grown to nearly 5,000 clients and over $1 billion AUM (approaching $1.5 billion).
- Range positions itself between robo-advisors and traditional incumbents like Creative Planning, LPL, Mariner, and Corient, targeting high-net-worth individuals with complex financial lives, with an average starting account size over $750,000 and median client age 41, capturing clients a decade earlier than the industry average of 52-53.
- The company avoids the traditional 1% AUM fee, instead charging a flat fee around $3,000, which is roughly 90% cheaper than most incumbents; Fahad argues the value of a traditional advisor over 22 years can cost around $4 million, and many advisors merely park clients in the S&P 500.
- Range combines human CFPs (about 35 advisors for 5,000 clients) with proprietary software and an AI agent named Rye; the AI handles planning, investment management, tax strategy, estate planning, and can execute actions like backdoor Roth conversions via text message.
- The company spent years building internal financial software and testing an internal AI agent with its CFPs before launching Rye publicly about a year ago; this included encoding nuanced financial calculations like time value of money for annuities (end-of-period vs beginning-of-period).
- Trust is a central challenge: while AI may be better than human advisors, public trust is still developing, especially for high-stakes million-dollar accounts; Range addresses this with a compliance AI agent that monitors all AI interactions for fiduciary duty, care, and supervision, and they work with regulators and former SEC officials.
- Range's business model includes a vertically integrated stack of planning, tax, and investment management software that the AI can use, plus a sales agent built internally to replace tools like Salesforce; Fahad notes they are building their way out of Salesforce because custom AI-based CRM can be built without coding.
- Customer acquisition is aided by custodial partners like Altruist (acquired by Vanguard for $5 billion) and Apex; Range's gross margins are at least 2x better than the best industry players due to software and AI doing work humans used to do.
- The company does not take clients with more than $10 million AUM, focusing on a middle ground of complexity to prove the model before moving down market, similar to Uber's strategy of starting with Uber Black before UberX and Uber Pool.
- Range sees itself as capturing clients earlier and keeping them longer, potentially 30-35 year LTV, by offering transparent pricing and AI-driven personalized service that can increase financial literacy.
- Fahad and David highlight that AI agents like Meta's Muse and Anthropic's Claude are already performing multi-step tasks (booking flights, changing Instagram algorithms) and this same agentic capability is being applied to wealth management actions.
