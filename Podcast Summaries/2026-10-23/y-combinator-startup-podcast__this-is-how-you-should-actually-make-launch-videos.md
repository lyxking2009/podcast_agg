# This Is How You Should Actually Make Launch Videos — Y Combinator Startup Podcast

## TL;DR
Farza Majeed, founder of Clicky and formerly Buildspace, shares his playbook for creating effective launch videos without high production costs. He emphasizes treating launches as an everyday part of building, scripting videos to feel off the cuff, and constantly testing channels to see what converts.

## Key points
- Many founders wrongly assume launch videos must be expensive, high-production commercials, but Farza Majeed challenges this misconception.
- Farza has made videos for over a decade, and his posts regularly get millions of views; he previously built Buildspace, one of the largest online schools for builders.
- He treats launches not as rare, high-stakes moments but as part of the everyday process of building a company.
- His approach involves scripting videos that still feel off the cuff, turning demos into compelling stories that people want to watch.
- He advises founders to launch constantly and figure out which distribution channels actually convert for their product.
