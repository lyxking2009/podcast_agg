## Overview

Aaron Levie (CEO of Box) joins Alex Kantrowitz live at the Big Technology AI Summit to discuss the government-mandated recall of Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models. Levie argues this is effectively the 'AI pause' that critics demanded, and that the government is now in the model-approval business. The conversation explores unintended consequences: how the ban could advantage China, what it means for AI regulation going forward, and the new economics of AI including token costs and enterprise adoption patterns.

## Implications

For AI practitioners and investors: The Fable ban sets a precedent for government model approval — expect more regulatory scrutiny of frontier models. The ban may paradoxically advantage Chinese AI by constraining US development. Enterprise AI economics are shifting from per-seat to consumption-based pricing, with token costs becoming a major budget consideration. The regulatory landscape is now a first-order factor in AI investment theses.
