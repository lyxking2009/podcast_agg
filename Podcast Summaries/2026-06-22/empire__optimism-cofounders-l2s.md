## Key Points

- The L2 'reckoning' is fundamentally about hype meeting reality — crypto shifted from speculative blue-sky promises to actually trying to deliver value to end users
- L2s got the delivery vehicle right (extending L1 capabilities, differentiating block space) but many failed to actually differentiate their products
- Many L2s launched as shortcuts to the 'new validator set hype chain thing' rather than focusing on building real products
- Optimism's strategy focuses on the OP Stack and Superchain — making it easy to build on-chain products rather than competing on tokenomics
- The L2 space lost the ability to not hold itself accountable — the 'vibes era' of crypto gave way to requiring real product development
- Delivering value to end users requires real product development, knowing what value you'll generate, and providing a cohesive product — a 'serious shortcoming' of many crypto projects
- Crypto's deflating from hype is actually healthy — the industry needs to 'button up' to take things to the next level
- The OP Stack approach treats the customer as B2B2C — not just end users but also developers and chains building on the Superchain

## Overview

Ben Jones and Karl Floersch, two of the three co-founders of Optimism, join Empire to discuss the L2 reckoning. They candidly address why L2s went from being crypto's hottest narrative (2021-2022, $5-10B valuations) to facing a credibility crisis. The conversation covers what L2s got right (the delivery vehicle) and wrong (failing to differentiate products), and how Optimism's OP Stack and Superchain strategy represents a more sustainable approach focused on actual product development rather than token hype.

## Implications

For crypto investors: The L2 space is bifurcating between projects with real product differentiation and those still riding hype. Optimism's OP Stack strategy — treating L2s as infrastructure for product builders rather than standalone chains — may prove more sustainable. The broader lesson: crypto projects that survive the 'hype to reality' transition are those that focus on end-user value rather than tokenomics.
