## Overview

Michael Batnick and Ben Carlson are joined by Dr. Ankur Crawford from Alger for a 'Talk Your Book' episode arguing that AI is not a bubble. Crawford makes the case that the AI build-out has unusual staying power due to supply chain consolidation, and that earnings estimates matter more than multiples for evaluating AI stocks. The conversation covers overlooked opportunities in power and industrials as AI infrastructure, and the debate between open-source and closed AI models.

## Implications

For investors: The 'AI is a bubble' narrative may be overstated — supply chain consolidation creates structural advantages that differ from dot-com era dynamics. Power and industrials as AI infrastructure plays are underappreciated. Focus on earnings growth rather than valuation multiples when evaluating AI names. The Alger CNEQ fund's concentrated positions (Nvidia 14.9%, TSMC 5.8%) reflect conviction in specific AI infrastructure winners.
