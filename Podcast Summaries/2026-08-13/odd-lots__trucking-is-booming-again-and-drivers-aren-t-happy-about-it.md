# Trucking Is Booming Again, And Drivers Aren't Happy About It — Odd Lots

## TL;DR
Trucking is rebounding with spot rates up sharply and trucking stocks like Knight-Swift and JB Hunt outperforming, but the upturn is driven as much by supply constraints as demand. The Trump administration's crackdown on non-domiciled CDLs, English proficiency requirements, and a Supreme Court ruling exposing freight brokers to liability have removed capacity and raised costs. Meanwhile, drivers face chronic parking shortages and low morale, and data center construction is creating pockets of high demand.

## Key points
- Spot rates rose from $1.94 per mile in December to over $3.00, according to the Internet Truck Stop Van Rate Index, while Knight-Swift (KNX) and JB Hunt shares have surged, with Knight-Swift outperforming Meta year-to-date.
- Capacity is tightening due to federal scrutiny of non-domiciled CDLs and English language proficiency, forcing many immigrant-owned small fleets to shed drivers and making hiring difficult; this may be structural rather than cyclical because renewal of non-domiciled CDLs is becoming harder.
- A Supreme Court ruling in the Montgomery case raises the possibility that freight brokers can be held liable for carrier negligence, threatening the brokerage business model and small carriers that depend on brokers, potentially stratifying rates based on safety ratings.
- Cargo theft has surged, with coordinated crime rings targeting high-value freight, celebrity liquor brands, and even celebrity-owned vehicles; a Roadmaster Group stat indicates cargo theft jumped 60% last year, increasing broker vetting burdens.
- Truck parking is a major driver pain point: electronic logging devices fix drivers' hours, forcing a daily trade-off between safe parking and maximizing paid miles; Truck Parking Club's Airbnb-style marketplace adds private parking spots, growing faster than traditional truck stops.
- Driver morale is low because technology like ELDs, in-cab cameras, and speed limiters reduced the traditional freedom of the road, and many drivers would not recommend the job to a friend; autonomous trucking adds long-term career uncertainty.
- Demand pockets exist around data center construction, which drives flatbed demand and requires coordinated staging and parking; Truck Parking Club is actively working with data center builders and autonomous truck companies to plan reliable staging locations.
