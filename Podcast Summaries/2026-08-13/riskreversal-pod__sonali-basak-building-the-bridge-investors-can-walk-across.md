# Sonali Basak: Building “The Bridge” Investors Can Walk Across — RiskReversal Pod

## TL;DR
Sonali Basak joins Dan Nathan and Guy Adami on the one-year anniversary of her move from Bloomberg Television to iCapital, where she is Chief Investment Strategist. The conversation centers on AI economics — how declining token costs shift value along the "AI food chain," with hyperscalers capturing a large share while software and enterprises benefit as costs fall, and Jevons-paradox-style demand potentially sustaining aggregate spend and capex. They cover why frontier AI labs are pursuing IPOs amid heavy cash burn and broad access to debt and equity, the new NVIDIA-led $500B compute financing platform, abundant 2026 liquidity that may tighten, oil's drag on consumers, hedge-fund crowding and the "situational awareness" leverage unwind, valuation dispersion between semis and financials/utilities, and rate risks — Treasury basis-trade leverage, a 10-year yield range of 4-4.8%, Japan's carry trade, and selective interest in Japan and parts of APAC for international exposure.

## Key points
- Basak discusses her media series "The Bridge" and iCapital's reach across wealth managers and asset managers — the thesis being a bridge between public and private markets for advisors and investors.
- AI food chain economics: as token/unit costs fall, value shifts along the chain — hyperscalers capture a large share, while software and enterprises benefit from falling costs; demand (Jevons paradox) may keep aggregate spend and capex elevated.
- Frontier AI labs are going public: heavy cash burn combined with broad access to debt and equity makes IPOs attractive despite profitability questions.
- Wall Street is crowding into AI, including the new NVIDIA-led $500B compute financing platform; investors struggle to diversify as infrastructure, power and data centers converge.
- Liquidity: 2026 has seen abundant liquidity, but the risk is it tightens; oil's impact on consumers argues for second-half caution.
- Hedge-fund crowding and the unwind of "situational awareness" trades; valuation dispersion is wide — semis vs financials and utilities.
- Rates: Treasury basis-trade leverage is a risk; a 10-year yield range of 4-4.8% is the working framework; Japan's carry trade remains a live risk factor.
- International positioning: selective interest in Japan and parts of APAC for diversification.
