# The AI "FOMO Feeding Frenzy" with Amish Jani of FirstMark Capital — RiskReversal Pod

## TL;DR
Amish Jani, co-founder of FirstMark Capital, tells host Dan Nathan that the AI shift is the fastest, largest-magnitude change he's seen in his ~20-year VC career. He argues frontier-model demand will keep growing even as enterprises optimize token usage, but flags real risks: heavy debt financing, Nvidia's customer-concentration exposure, circular chip-financing deals, and the chance a non-transformer architecture could strand today's infrastructure buildout. He expects value to migrate from infrastructure to vertical application software and predicts an Anthropic and/or OpenAI IPO will end the private-market 'FOMO feeding frenzy.'

## Key points
- Jani calls the current AI shift the fastest and largest-magnitude change in his ~20 years investing, saying old valuation frames no longer apply.
- He describes a shift from 'token maxing' to token optimization, framing it as 'and' not 'or' — optimization coexists with continued frontier-model growth since enterprise agentic adoption is still very early.
- OpenAI and Anthropic have unprecedented revenue growth but are funding infrastructure partly via tens of billions in debt, not just equity.
- Nvidia has roughly 50% free cash flow margins; its biggest risk is customer concentration, creating circular financing like Anthropic reportedly securing loans against Google TPU chips.
- The biggest long-term risk is architectural disruption: a fundamentally more efficient approach could emerge and strand current infrastructure investment.
- He pushes back on the 'SaaS apocalypse' narrative, arguing tested, secure enterprise software beats fragile internally 'vibe-coded' tools — a 'software wisdom of the crowds.'
- Jani predicts an IPO from Anthropic and/or OpenAI (both recently raised at roughly $900 billion valuations) will be a 'seminal event' that shifts the market from FOMO-driven private dynamics to broad public trading.
- FirstMark's current focus: deeply vertical AI applications, infrastructure/security tooling (portfolio companies Onyx and Daytona), consumer AI agents, and stablecoin/blockchain adoption.
