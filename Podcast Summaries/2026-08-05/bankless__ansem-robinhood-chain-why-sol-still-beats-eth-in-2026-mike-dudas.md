# $ANSEM, Robinhood Chain, & Why SOL Still Beats ETH in 2026 | Mike Dudas — Bankless

## TL;DR
David Hoffman talks with Mike Dudas (Six Man Ventures) about meme coins, tracing Bonk's origin as a 2022 Solana community airdrop through to Ansem's new meme coin and the shift of trench activity from Base to Robinhood Chain. Dudas argues meme coins are a durable, if short-duration, cultural/attention asset class, previews Six Man's bets on stablecoin finance and DeFi with real-world assets, and states his fund is structurally bullish on SOL over ETH for the next five years.

## Key points
- Bonk launched in late 2022 at the bottom of the Solana bear market; ~20 community members airdropped 50% of supply to builders/users, the token hit a $400M valuation within weeks.
- Dudas is a disclosed investor in Ansem's company Bullpen; he frames Ansem's meme coin as an attempt to recreate the 2022 Bonk moment, distinguishing it from 2025's celebrity/creator coins where insiders dumped on retail.
- On Robinhood Chain vs. Base: Dudas says Base's creator-coin/AI-coin push was badly timed near the meme market top, while Robinhood's meme-coin embrace was well-timed.
- Six Man Ventures' investment focus for the rest of the decade: stablecoin finance, DeFi incorporating real-world assets, and consumer crypto — especially collectibles as a successor to 2021-era PFP NFTs.
- Asked to choose between ETH and SOL for a 5-year hold, Dudas says his fund is Solana-bullish due to higher real usage/activity and a fee mechanism that lets Solana capture more revenue.
- Dudas compares crypto's slow progress unfavorably to AI's rapid recent gains, attributing AI's speed partly to it not needing to displace an incumbent financial system.
