# EARLY ACCESS - "We Want to Be Bigger Than the CME" | Kalshi's John Wang — Bankless

## TL;DR
David Hoffman interviews Kalshi's John Wang about the company's ambition to become the world's largest exchange, bigger than the CME. Wang explains Kalshi's onshore/regulated strategy versus offshore rivals like Polymarket, its new perpetual futures product (the first regulated US perps exchange), how it polices insider trading, and its push into institutional adoption via block trades and index products.

## Key points
- Kalshi says crypto is its second-largest business line behind sports, with 90% market share in crypto prediction markets and routing partnerships with Coinbase, Robinhood Crypto, and Phantom.
- Kalshi deliberately takes the regulated, onshore path so it can advertise, list on app stores, and integrate with major brokers, unlike Polymarket's offshore approach.
- Kalshi launched the first regulated perpetual futures exchange in the US, now listing Bitcoin, ETH, Hype, Solana, Zcash, Near, SUI, Doge, and XRP; gold and silver perps are filed and expected within weeks.
- Wang frames Kalshi's core tension as balancing informed 'alpha' traders who make prices accurate against retail traders who fund that price discovery but feel extracted from if permissiveness goes too far.
- Kalshi enforces insider-trading rules by barring politicians/politically-associated persons from trading political markets and maintaining watchlists.
- Wang says Kalshi has pulled roughly 4-5x ahead of Polymarket in crypto volume, and states the company's ultimate goal is to be the largest exchange on the planet.
