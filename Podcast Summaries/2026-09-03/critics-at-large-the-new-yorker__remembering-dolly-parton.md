## TL;DR
In this Critics at Large episode, hosts Vinson Cunningham and Naomi Fry, along with New Yorker writers Kathryn Schultz, Amanda Petrusich, and Lauren Michele Jackson, remember Dolly Parton following her death. They discuss her 'Oprah-like' business acumen and philanthropy, distill her vast discography to three essential tracks, and analyze how her carefully crafted persona transcended race and class while celebrating femininity.
