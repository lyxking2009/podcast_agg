# The Chopping Block: Robinhood Chain's Memecoin Mania and Tokenized Stocks — Unchained

## TL;DR
Robinhood Chain's second explosive rally, driven by memecoins paired with tokenized stocks like BONER/HIMS and CINEMA/AMC, has reignited debate about L1 value capture, Solana's position, and retail harm. The panel discusses how FOMO app users are flocking to these new RWA-memecoin games, while Haseeb argues they are structurally certain to lose to hedge funds; meanwhile Robinhood Chain now ranks #2 in 24h DEX volume. They also cover reports of Hyperliquid's onshore deal with Kraken parent Payward and the likely bifurcation of US-regulated vs global crypto markets.

## Key points
- Robinhood Chain has entered a second, more explosive rally with 125,000 active wallets, 5.7 million transactions per day, $1.2 billion in DEX volume, and $2 million in realized value (fees) in a single day, driven largely by memecoins and tokenized stocks.
- Approximately 90,000 of the 125,000 active addresses originated from the FOMO app, indicating that memecoin traders are no longer chain-loyal and will follow wallets and apps across different blockchains.
- A new speculative primitive combines memecoins with tokenized stocks via OlympusDAO-style bonding: users must mint and lock stock tokens (e.g., HIMS, AMC) to receive memecoin rewards like BONER or CINEMA, effectively using the memecoin as a liquidity incentive for RWA minting.
- The weekend trading creates massive deviations between tokenized stock prices and their underlying real-world counterparts, guaranteeing arbitrage opportunities for hedge funds when markets reopen on Monday, which panelist Haseeb argues is a machine for retail capital destruction.
- Total tokenized stock issuance on Robinhood Chain has grown from near zero to around $70–80 million, still a fraction of the $3–4 billion total tokenized equities market, but evidence that memecoin mechanisms can bootstrap RWA liquidity.
- Solana is perceived as threatened: Flood tweeted that Solana is in its most perilous position, and Robinhood Chain now ranks #2 in 24-hour DEX volume at $1.5 billion versus Solana’s $2.5 billion, though Solana still leads on 30-day DEX volume ($62B vs $17B) and TVL ($5.7B vs $730M).
- Robinhood Chain originally considered building on Solana but ultimately chose the Arbitrum stack, largely because running its own L2 flips the economics from paying fees to earning them; this has benefited Arbitrum while Ethereum only captures small data availability fees.
- The panel debates whether memecoin trading is harmless entertainment or predatory; Haseeb argues that unlike casinos where players know the odds, memecoin influencers delude users into thinking they will profit, causing more severe reputational harm to crypto retail.
- On Hyperliquid: Bloomberg reports that Hyperliquid Labs is in advanced talks with Kraken’s parent Payward to launch a US-regulated product using Bitnomial’s clearing license, likely resulting in a KYC’d, centrally cleared instance separate from the global DEX.
- The US regulatory approach may create a bifurcated market: formal, regulated venues for institutions and domestic users, while retail can access global DEXs anyway via VPNs and informal channels, mirroring dual-market structures in other countries.
