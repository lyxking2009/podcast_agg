## Overview

Dan Nathan and Guy Adami host Ben Emons of FedWatch Advisors for a deep dive into macro risks. The conversation covers the Fed's shift away from strong forward guidance under Kevin Warsh, the distinction between disinflation and deflation, 'funflation' in consumer categories, and whether AI is inflationary. IBM's earnings warning serves as a case study for enterprise spending rotation. The episode highlights the 'Parabolic Seven' — a basket of memory and chip stocks showing extreme volatility and leverage that could trigger broader market rotation.

## Implications

For investors, the 'Parabolic Seven' concept identifies a specific cohort of semiconductor stocks (SanDisk, Marvell, Micron, etc.) that have gone parabolic and are vulnerable to a correction. IBM's warning about clients shifting CapEx to servers/storage/memory suggests the AI infrastructure buildout is crowding out traditional enterprise spending. The Fed's communication shift under Warsh means less forward guidance and more data-dependence, increasing market uncertainty.
