## Overview

This Unchained episode covers three major DeFi controversies: the Venice/VVV token economics debate, the BonkDAO governance exploit where one wallet legally drained a $20 million treasury, and the ENS founder control debate. The conversation also touches on Lighter's Robinhood Chain integration and what these events collectively mean for DeFi's future. The hosts analyze whether these are design flaws or features of decentralized governance.

## Implications

For DeFi investors, the BonkDAO exploit is a stark warning about governance design — 1% quorum rules can be weaponized. The ENS controversy highlights the tension between founder vision and community governance. These episodes suggest DeFi governance needs significant redesign before it can be trusted with large treasuries.
