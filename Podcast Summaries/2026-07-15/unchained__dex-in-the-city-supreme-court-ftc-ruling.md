## Overview

This DEX in the City episode explores how the Supreme Court's FTC ruling could fundamentally reshape crypto regulation. Hosts Jessi Brooks, Katherine Kirkpatrick Bos, and Vy Le discuss the implications for regulatory authority, the language around crypto crime, and whether operational security (not smart contract bugs) is the real DeFi vulnerability. The conversation also touches on whether the US should actively recover stolen crypto from North Korea.

## Implications

For crypto companies and investors, the Supreme Court's FTC ruling creates a potential regulatory vacuum that could be filled by more crypto-friendly agencies. The operational security argument suggests that DeFi's security problems are human, not technical — which has implications for how protocols should be audited and insured.
