## Key Points
- Public market concentration and multiple expansion in mega-cap tech are directly influencing private market valuations, creating both opportunity and risk for venture investors
- AI adoption is accelerating rapidly — Anthropic's run-rate growth and broad "token path" benefits for infrastructure companies signal that AI spending is real and compounding
- Cybersecurity is emerging as a top beneficiary sector as AI agents proliferate and increase enterprise attack surfaces, with CrowdStrike and Palo Alto highlighted as key plays
- Enterprise "sticker shock" on AI costs is a near-term headwind — companies are grappling with consumption-based pricing and difficulty measuring ROI in output terms
- The IPO pipeline featuring SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI represents a potential market-defining moment; Richards notes that public listings would provide much-needed transparency on actual financial performance
- Richards recently purchased Meta stock, citing a compelling valuation-to-AI-investment-return ratio compared to other mega-cap peers
- Notable Capital (formerly GGV Capital) has evolved its thesis toward AI and vertical software, with portfolio companies spanning infrastructure and application layers
- Patience is warranted — stretched valuations in mega-cap tech argue for waiting for pullbacks rather than chasing momentum
- Tech M&A is accelerating through talent-driven acqui-hires and consolidation, compressing the exit timeline for certain venture-backed companies

## Overview
Dan Nathan hosts Jeff Richards, Managing Partner at Notable Capital (formerly GGV Capital), for a wide-ranging conversation on how to navigate AI investing at a moment when public market euphoria is beginning to bleed into private market valuations. Richards frames the discussion around a core tension: AI adoption metrics look genuinely strong — Anthropic's growth trajectory and token consumption across infrastructure companies are real signals — yet valuations in mega-cap tech have stretched to levels that demand caution. The episode explores where durable value is being created versus where hype has outrun fundamentals. Richards shares how Notable Capital has repositioned its portfolio around AI and software, and why cybersecurity stands out as a structural beneficiary of the agentic AI wave. The conversation also covers the approaching IPO super-cycle with Anthropic, OpenAI, and SpaceX all moving toward public markets, and what that transparency will mean for the broader AI investment thesis.

## Implications
The episode captures a pivotal inflection point: AI is transitioning from hype cycle to measurable business impact, but the market pricing has not yet fully differentiated between companies with durable AI advantages and those riding the macro wave. Richards' framework — focus on sectors with compounding AI benefit (cybersecurity, infrastructure), wait for pullbacks in richly valued names, and watch the IPO pipeline for ground-truth financials — offers a disciplined VC lens applicable to public market investors as well. If the Anthropic, OpenAI, and SpaceX IPOs proceed as anticipated, 2026 could mark the moment when AI's financial reality either validates or deflates the current valuation regime across both public and private markets.
