## Overview
This episode of Bits + Bips centers on the escalating clash between JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon and Coinbase's Brian Armstrong over the Clarity Act, a landmark crypto regulatory bill that would allow crypto platforms to offer yield on deposits. The hosts argue this confrontation is not merely a personality dispute but a structural battle over which institutions — traditional banks or crypto exchanges — will intermediate the next generation of capital markets. They characterize the present moment as crypto's "peak political power" window, one they believe will close after the midterm elections. Beyond the Dimon-Armstrong saga, the episode covers Anthropic's confidential IPO filing at a near-trillion-dollar valuation, MicroStrategy's first Bitcoin sale since 2022 as its preferred stock dividend obligations mount, Hyperliquid's extraordinary growth as a lean on-chain exchange surpassing NASDAQ in volume, and the DTCC's selection of Stellar to begin tokenizing U.S. equities.

## Implications
The Dimon-Armstrong clash represents a defining moment for crypto's regulatory trajectory: if the Clarity Act passes while crypto holds peak political leverage, it could unlock trillions in deposit flows from banks to crypto platforms, fundamentally reshaping the financial system. The DTCC's move to tokenize equities on Stellar signals that institutional infrastructure is shifting from electronification to blockchain-based 24/7 markets, normalizing on-chain settlement for mainstream assets. Hyperliquid's rise — a tiny team outpacing NASDAQ by volume — challenges assumptions about the necessity of large institutional intermediaries and previews a future where decentralized exchange infrastructure competes directly with legacy venues.
