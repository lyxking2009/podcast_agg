# #212 - Callum Smiles - "They Are Stealing The Country": Border Gangs, Fake Deliveroo Accounts & The Civil War — The Peter McCormack Show

## TL;DR

Callum Smiles discusses the UK's migration crisis, arguing that organized crime and NGOs facilitate Channel crossings while government contracts enrich insiders. He claims the UK national debt has soared to £3 trillion, public services are failing, and political elites are complicit. He advocates building parallel institutions like the Wyvern Fellowship to bypass government and warns of civil war if the country continues on its current path.

## Key points

- UK national debt escalated from £527 billion (36% debt-to-GDP) in 2007 to £3 trillion (94%) by 2025, adding £2.5 trillion in under two decades, which Callum Smiles argues is not austerity but misdirected spending.
- NHS procurement examples demonstrate waste: a single air freshener cost £75, and three beds were moved for £676 because staff lack accountability for spending other people's money.
- Channel crossings have totaled over 200,000 people since 2018, with peak annual numbers around 46,000; smugglers charge roughly £3,000 per seat, offering summer discounts as low as £1,000, making a 200-person crossing worth £200,000.
- Smugglers operate like airlines: they oversell boat seats to mix gang members among migrants, use tidal forecasts to avoid rough seas, and instruct migrants to call UK authorities once inside British waters so the RNLI must respond.
- Gang members are identifiable by their footwear—new clean Jordans—while genuine migrants wear worn shoes; many boat drivers are migrants with no experience, contributing to sinkings.
- NGOs such as Utopia 56 provide SIM cards and phones to migrants; Callum claims removing NGOs could stop crossings overnight, though lower-level workers are well-intentioned but 'useful idiots'.
- Women make up only about 15% of camp occupants; rape and sexual exploitation are common, with some women trading sex with traffickers for passage; Callum argues this pattern correlates with UK becoming Europe's 'rape capital'.
- Criminal gangs exploit delivery platforms: they rent Deliveroo/Uber Eats accounts for £40–100 per week, buy uniforms on eBay for under £40, and use forced labour to deliver drugs; over 100,000 people subscribed to related Facebook groups in three years.
- The UK government sold 57,000 MoD homes for £1.66bn in 1996 with a 200-year leaseback, then bought back 36,347 properties for £6bn in 2025; Annington Homes owns the portfolio, and Liam Fox's alleged stake was not confirmed.
- National Lottery funding includes £254.9 million across 3,86 grants (since 2004) for refugee/asylum programmes, rising to £324.8 million across 4,767 grants when migration-related projects are included, which Callum calls a scandal.
- A deportation case was blocked partly because a child disliked foreign chicken nuggets; Callum argues such human-rights legal arguments prevent border control, while states like UAE enforce strict rules.
- The Ceuta border incident saw 60,000–80,000 migrants cross into the Spanish enclave with 115+ deaths; Morocco's king pardoned 1,800 prisoners shortly before; Spain has begun frog-marching migrants out, which Callum sees as evidence citizens can resist.
- UK voter concerns rank immigration top (50%), then economy, defense, crime, health, welfare, environment, housing, tax, and Europe; Callum claims these require a right-wing government to fix.
- Callum promotes the Wyvern Fellowship, a non-political membership network (£5/month or £50/year) focused on mutual support, heritage, and resilience; first local chapter launches in two weeks, aiming to build parallel institutions independent of government.
- Peter McCormack describes economic decline: private school fees rose from £45k to £75k per term (2021–2026), London flats dropped ~20% in value, and hospitality venues are half-empty; he says the UK is in collapse.
- Both agree government institutions have failed; Peter challenges anyone to name one government service improved in 20 years, and Callum argues parallel institutions are the only path, possibly leading to civil war if unresolved.
