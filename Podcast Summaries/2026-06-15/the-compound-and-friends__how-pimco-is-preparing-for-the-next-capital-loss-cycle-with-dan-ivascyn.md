## Key Points

2. Four primary forces driving change: geopolitical uncertainty, tariffs and deglobalization, AI infrastructure spending, and rising debt levels.
3. The conversation centers on how PIMCO is positioning for the next potential capital loss cycle in markets — a shift from the extended period of capital gains investors have enjoyed.
4. Dan Ivascyn, PIMCO's Group CIO, discusses how fixed income investors need to prepare for a fundamentally different environment than the post-GFC era.
5. The episode explores how deglobalization and tariff policies are creating new risks and opportunities in credit markets.
6. AI infrastructure spending is identified as both a driver of economic growth and a potential source of excess if investment outpaces returns.

## Overview

Michael Batnick and Downtown Josh Brown are joined by Dan Ivascyn, PIMCO's Group CIO, for a wide-ranging conversation about the forces reshaping global markets. The discussion centers on PIMCO's 'Rupture and Resilience' secular outlook, covering geopolitical uncertainty, tariffs, AI infrastructure spending, and rising debt levels. Ivascyn explains how PIMCO is preparing for the next capital loss cycle and what fixed income investors should be positioning for.

## Implications

The conversation suggests PIMCO sees a regime change ahead — from the extended period of low rates and capital gains to one where capital preservation and income generation become more important. For fixed income investors, this means potentially higher yields but also higher credit risk, requiring more selective positioning.
