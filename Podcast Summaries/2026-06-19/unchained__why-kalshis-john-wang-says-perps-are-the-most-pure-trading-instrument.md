# Why Kalshi's John Wang Says Perps Are 'the Most Pure Trading Instrument'

**Show:** Unchained  
**Guests:** John Wang (Head of Crypto, Kalshi), Laura Shin (host)  
**Date:** 2026-06-19  
**Transcript source:** web_unchained

## Key Points

- Kalshi, valued at $22B, has launched the first CFTC-approved crypto perpetual futures (perps) in the United States, targeting a $90 trillion offshore market
- John Wang argues perps are 'the most pure trading instrument' due to their continuous exposure structure
- Pushback against CME Group CEO Terry Duffy who called crypto perps 'a disaster waiting to happen' — Wang notes CME's own futures carry higher leverage than Kalshi's
- Kalshi differentiates from offshore venues (Hyperliquid) with a guarantee fund and segregated accounts for customer protection
- Insider trading protections built around athlete and congressional staff lists
- Only 0.2% of the US population has adopted perpetual futures — 'the real growth has barely started'
- Kalshi competes with Hyperliquid, Coinbase, and Kraken in the US market

## Overview

Kalshi's Head of Crypto John Wang explains the company's landmark launch of CFTC-approved crypto perpetual futures, positioning Kalshi as the regulated alternative to the $90 trillion offshore derivatives market. The conversation covers why perps are structurally superior trading instruments, how Kalshi's risk management differs from offshore venues, and the massive untapped US market for these products.

## Implications

Kalshi's regulated perps launch is a major milestone for US crypto market structure. The 0.2% US adoption rate suggests enormous growth runway. CFTC approval gives Kalshi a significant competitive advantage over offshore venues.
