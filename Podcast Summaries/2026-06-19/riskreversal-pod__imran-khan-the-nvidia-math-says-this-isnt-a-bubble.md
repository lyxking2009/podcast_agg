# Imran Khan: The Nvidia Math Says This Isn't A Bubble

**Show:** RiskReversal Pod  
**Guests:** Imran Khan (Proem Asset Management), Dan Nathan (host)  
**Date:** 2026-06-19  
**Transcript source:** web_substack

## Key Points

- Nvidia trades at a below-market multiple relative to its growth, with a $20B debt raise and buybacks signaling management confidence — argues against bubble narrative
- Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta) historically underperform during heavy CapEx phases, which Khan argues are 'historically the best time to buy'
- Google's Gemini won in the market despite being arguably inferior to competitors — distribution beats technology. Grok (xAI) failed due to limited distribution
- Meta is 'cheap on earnings but not cheap on free cash flow'; the 'Zuckerberg big swing' risk from massive AI bets is the key concern
- Micron's high-bandwidth memory is sold out through 2027 — the critical question is whether memory demand is cyclical or secular
- Investors are chasing the 'bottleneck trade' in the most constrained part of the AI supply chain; earnings durability is the key metric to watch
- Energy limitations and data center delays are the primary physical bottlenecks for AI scaling
- Khan holds a contrarian view that AI will NOT create structural unemployment
- SpaceX's $2.5T valuation: significant portion is not a 'pure space story' — rocket launch, Starlink, and xAI cloud ramp are the components
- Upcoming IPOs/market entries of OpenAI and Anthropic are potential major catalysts for the broader AI trade
- The 'customers-are-competitors' problem: major GPU buyers (Google, Amazon, Meta) are also developing their own chips

## Overview

Imran Khan, founder of Proem Asset Management and former Snap executive, argues against the AI bubble thesis using Nvidia's valuation math as the primary counterpoint. Dan Nathan pressure-tests the bear case across hyperscaler CapEx cycles, the cyclical-vs-secular memory debate, energy constraints, and SpaceX's complex valuation. Khan's contrarian stance on AI employment and his view that distribution beats technology are the episode's most distinctive arguments.

## Implications

The below-market multiple on Nvidia at $5T market cap is a strong data point against bubble calls. The bottleneck trade requires careful earnings durability analysis. OpenAI/Anthropic IPOs could be the next major catalyst for AI re-rating.
