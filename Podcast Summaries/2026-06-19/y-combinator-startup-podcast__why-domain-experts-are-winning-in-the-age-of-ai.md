## Overview

Y Combinator's Lightcone podcast features Bryant Chou, who co-founded Webflow and is now building Ploy in the current YC batch. The conversation explores how AI is changing the nature of startup building — making execution cheap while elevating the importance of domain expertise, taste, and curation. Chou demonstrates Ploy's anti-slop engine and discusses the emerging paradigm of 'agents as customers' through MCP and CLI interfaces.

## Implications

Domain expertise is the new moat in the AI era — when anyone can build, knowing what to build becomes the scarce resource. The 'agents as customers' paradigm (MCP, CLI, AEO) represents a new distribution channel that most startups aren't thinking about yet. AI-native tools like Ploy are displacing traditional website builders.
