## Key Points

- The underlying deficit-and-debt case for both gold and Bitcoin hasn't changed — Pandl is calling the dip a buy for longer-horizon investors

Grayscale's head of research Zach Pandl makes the case that the gold and Bitcoin selloffs were driven by speculative rotation rather than fundamental deterioration. He presents a constructive medium-term view on both assets and introduces Grayscale's DCF-based approach to DeFi token valuation, with Hyperliquid and Aave as key positions.

## Implications

The speculative rotation thesis suggests the selloff is temporary. Grayscale's DCF approach to DeFi tokens signals institutional-grade valuation frameworks entering crypto. Hyperliquid's revenue model makes it the benchmark for DeFi protocol evaluation.
