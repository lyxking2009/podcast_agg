## Key Points

- Anthropic's Fable model was shut down by US government export controls after a reported jailbreak — the hosts argue Dario Amodei's response made the shutdown inevitable
- Core critique: 'A company about to go public had no one in Washington who could speak the government's language'
- The shutdown drew no public backlash, which is notable and concerning for the AI industry
- Porter Stowell (Coinbase alum) analyzed Coinbase's announcement day: agentic payments on Base, tokenized stocks, and a ground-up trading rebuild
- Luca Netz announced Igloo Inc. (Pudgy Penguins parent) has built a financial instrument to list crypto tokens directly on the NYSE as securities, with protocol revenue flowing to token holders
- Strategy (MicroStrategy): selling 32 Bitcoin then buying back 1,587 two weeks later exposes the structural risk of a belief-based asset with one dominant buyer

## Overview

The Unchained Uneasy Money panel dissects the Anthropic Fable shutdown as a cautionary tale about AI labs' political naivety, analyzes Coinbase's strategic pivot, and discusses Igloo Inc.'s groundbreaking plan to list crypto tokens on the NYSE. The conversation highlights the growing intersection of AI regulation and crypto market structure.

## Implications

AI labs need Washington expertise, not just technical talent. Coinbase's agentic payments on Base signal the future of crypto-native financial infrastructure. NYSE-listed crypto tokens with protocol revenue would be a structural breakthrough.
