# HIGHLIGHTS: Sridhar Ramaswamy - CEO of Snowflake

**Show:** In Good Company with Nicolai Tangen  
**Guests:** Sridhar Ramaswamy (CEO, Snowflake), Nicolai Tangen (host, CEO of NBIM)  
**Date:** 2026-06-19

## Key Points

- Snowflake serves half of the addressable (non-China) Global 2000 companies with its consumption-based data platform that separates storage from compute
- AI model companies (like Anthropic) are now Snowflake's biggest competitive threat — more than AWS or Microsoft — because they represent 'the industrialization of software'
- The software engineering role is shifting from 'craft' requiring 10,000 hours to conceptual management: 'The modern AI-driven software engineer is much more conceptual. They are actually managing a team of agents'
- Snowflake Intelligence: an agentic interface allowing natural language queries of enterprise data. Native coding agents (Cortex Code) automate data pipelines 10-20x faster
- MCP (Model Control Protocols): an interoperability layer for language models and coding agents to securely communicate with Snowflake data
- Sridhar's failure lesson from Neva (privacy search engine): consumer products need to be 10x better, not marginally better than incumbents
- Weekly war rooms: Sridhar plans Monday, wants results by Friday — tight feedback loops between product, engineering, and customers
- GDPR viewed as 'mixed bag': empowered consumers but raised costs and disproportionately burdened smaller European startups vs. large incumbents
- Quantum computing poses security risks but also promises optimization breakthroughs; Snowflake is preparing secure infrastructure
- Consumption-based pricing aligns Snowflake's revenue with customer value creation: 'We recognize revenue when you and your team use Snowflake'

## Overview

Snowflake CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy discusses with NBIM's Nicolai Tangen how AI is transforming the data platform business. The conversation covers Snowflake's consumption-based model, the existential threat from AI model companies, how software engineering is being reinvented by coding agents, and lessons from Sridhar's failed startup Neva. The episode provides a clear view of how enterprise AI adoption actually works — from data cleaning to agentic interfaces.

## Implications

AI model companies are disrupting not just software but data platforms — the competitive landscape is shifting rapidly. Consumption-based pricing models are better positioned than subscription models in the AI era. The 'managing agents' future of engineering is already here at Snowflake.
