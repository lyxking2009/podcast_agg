# The Rise of Robinhood Chain: Tokenized Stocks, Perps, and 27M Users | Johann Kerbrat

## Key Points

- Robinhood unveiled its blockchain infrastructure (Robinhood Chain) at the "The World is Flat" event in London; built on Arbitrum Orbit technology
- Tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain are one-to-one backed with dividend and governance rights, freely transferable across the Robinhood Chain and Ethereum ecosystem, and require no KYC for recipients
- Robinhood Earn offers 7% yield on USDG stablecoin deposits via Morpho integration, accessible directly from the main Robinhood app
- Regulated perpetuals (Non-crypto: Gold, Silver, QQQ, WTI with up to 10x leverage) are available via Bitstamp in Europe; DeFi-native perps with 20–50x leverage on 100+ crypto assets are available globally through Robin Wallet via Lidar integration
- The strategic vision: traditional finance infrastructure gradually migrates to blockchain for superior speed, transparency, and decentralization — starting with Robinhood's 27M user base as the on-ramp

## Overview

David from Bankless interviews Johann Kerbrat, VP of Crypto at Robinhood, about the launch of Robinhood Chain. The conversation covers the full product suite: tokenized stocks that bring dividend and governance rights on-chain, a 7% yield stablecoin product through Morpho, regulated perpetuals for commodities and equities indices in Europe, and DeFi-native perpetuals available globally through the Robin Wallet. Kerbrat frames the mission as bridging the UX gap between traditional brokerage accounts and blockchain infrastructure — "Blockchain was made by engineers for engineers" — by making the on-chain experience invisible to ordinary users. The strategic partnerships (Arbitrum Orbit, Paxos/USDG, Morpho, Lidar, Uniswap, Bitstamp) represent Robinhood's approach to assembling DeFi infrastructure without building every component in-house.

## Implications

- Robinhood Chain could accelerate mainstream retail adoption of tokenized real-world assets by combining 27M existing users with permissionless blockchain rails
- The combination of regulated (European) and unregulated (global DeFi) perpetuals offerings allows Robinhood to serve different regulatory environments simultaneously
- The 7% USDG yield product creates a sticky on-chain experience for non-crypto users, potentially serving as the entry point for broader DeFi engagement
- Robinhood's architecture — existing app + smart wallet extension — is a template for how traditional financial platforms can add DeFi capabilities without disrupting existing user flows
