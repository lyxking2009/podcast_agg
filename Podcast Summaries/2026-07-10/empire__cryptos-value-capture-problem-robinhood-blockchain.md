- The competitive landscape between centralized (Robinhood) and decentralized (DeFi) approaches to crypto trading is intensifying

## Overview

A deep dive into one of crypto's most persistent problems: value capture. Despite creating enormous value through decentralized protocols, crypto projects struggle to build sustainable business models. The episode examines Robinhood's bold move to build its own blockchain as a way to vertically integrate and capture more value. The hosts discuss how AI and distribution advantages are changing the competitive landscape, and whether traditional finance companies or crypto-native projects are better positioned to win.

## Implications

Robinhood's blockchain strategy signals a new phase where traditional finance companies build their own infrastructure rather than relying on existing protocols. This could fragment liquidity but also create more sustainable business models. The value capture problem remains crypto's biggest unsolved challenge.
