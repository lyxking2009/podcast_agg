# China Enters the Data Center, AI Profits Walk Out — with Louis-Vincent Gave & Peter Boockvar

**Show:** RiskReversal Pod
**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Guest(s):** Louis-Vincent Gave (CEO, Gavekal), Peter Boockvar (CIO, One Point BFG Wealth Partners), Dan Nathan (host)

## Key Points

- China is planning to spend nearly $300 billion on data center construction over the next 5 years, creating a massive infrastructure buildout
- The economic competition between the US and China has evolved from trade wars to a technology infrastructure race centered on AI
- China's response to the 2018 semiconductor embargo led to significant industrial advancements — the embargo may have accelerated Chinese self-sufficiency
- The implications for global markets are profound as China's AI infrastructure buildout could shift the balance of technological power
- US companies face increasing competition from Chinese firms that benefit from state capitalism and industrial policy
- The discussion covers how AI technology is becoming a key battleground in the US-China rivalry
- State capitalism gives China advantages in coordinating massive infrastructure investments that the US private sector struggles to match
- The episode explores whether the US can maintain its AI lead given China's infrastructure spending and manufacturing capabilities

## Overview

Louis-Vincent Gave and Peter Boockvar join Dan Nathan to discuss the evolving economic competition between the US and China, with a focus on the AI infrastructure race. China's planned $300 billion data center buildout represents a massive bet on AI dominance. The conversation covers how past trade embargoes backfired by accelerating Chinese self-sufficiency, the implications for global markets, and whether state capitalism gives China an unfair advantage in the AI race. The guests explore the strategic shifts required on both sides.

## Implications

The US-China AI infrastructure race is intensifying, with China's state-directed spending creating a formidable challenge to American dominance. Investors need to consider how this rivalry affects semiconductor, energy, and AI companies on both sides. The 2018 semiconductor embargo may have been counterproductive.

## Notable Quotes

- "China's response to the semiconductor embargo wasn't to back down — it was to build everything themselves." — Louis-Vincent Gave
- "$300 billion in data centers is not a policy — it's a declaration of technological independence." — Peter Boockvar
- "State capitalism gives China the ability to coordinate infrastructure investment at a scale the US private sector simply can't match." — Louis-Vincent Gave
- "The AI profits are walking out of the US and into countries that can actually build the infrastructure." — Dan Nathan
