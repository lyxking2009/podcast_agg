# HIGHLIGHTS: John Graham - CEO of CPP Investments

**Show:** In Good Company with Nicolai Tangen
**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Guest(s):** John Graham (CEO, CPP Investments), Nicolai Tangen (host, CEO of NBIM)

## Key Points

- CPP Investments manages $800 billion on behalf of 22 million Canadians — the largest pension fund in Canada
- The 'Canadian model' of pension fund management is widely imitated but often misunderstood by imitators
- John Graham calls diversification 'an act of humility' — acknowledging that you can't predict which asset class will outperform
- CPP weighs its allocation across private markets, the US, and China with a focus on long-term value creation
- Graham was a scientist before becoming an investor, bringing an analytical perspective to portfolio management
- Investing is 'quantitative art' rather than pure science — the combination of data and judgment is essential
- AI's role in investment decisions is uncertain but growing — CPP is exploring how AI can enhance rather than replace human judgment
- Leading through COVID required rapid decision-making and adaptability in an unprecedented environment
- The Canadian model's success comes from long time horizons, governance independence, and the ability to invest in illiquid assets

## Overview

A highlights episode featuring John Graham, CEO of CPP Investments (manager of $800 billion for 22 million Canadians), in conversation with Nicolai Tangen. Graham discusses the celebrated 'Canadian model' of pension fund management, why diversification is 'an act of humility,' how CPP balances private markets with public equities, and his belief that investing is 'quantitative art' rather than science. The conversation also touches on AI's uncertain but growing role in investment decisions and leadership lessons from COVID.

## Implications

The Canadian model's success offers lessons for other institutional investors: long time horizons, governance independence, and the ability to invest in illiquid assets are key differentiators. AI will likely augment rather than replace human judgment in institutional investing.

## Notable Quotes

- "Diversification is an act of humility." — John Graham
- "Investing is quantitative art, not science." — John Graham
- "We manage $800 billion for 22 million Canadians — that's a responsibility we take seriously." — John Graham
- "The Canadian model works because of long time horizons and governance independence." — John Graham
