# OpenAI Finally Ships Its Superapp, Meta's AI Price War, ChatGPT Cheating At Brown

**Show:** Big Technology Podcast
**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Guest(s):** Alex Kantrowitz (host), weekly discussion panel

## Key Points

- OpenAI has finally shipped its long-awaited 'superapp' — an AI interface that consolidates multiple computing tasks into a single platform
- Meta is engaging in an AI price war, aggressively cutting prices on its AI services to compete with OpenAI and Anthropic
- A report from Brown University reveals students are using ChatGPT to cheat on assignments, raising questions about AI in education
- The superapp concept represents OpenAI's bet that AI will become the primary interface for computing, not just a chatbot
- Meta's pricing strategy reflects its belief that distribution and scale matter more than margin in the early AI market
- The education sector is struggling to adapt to AI tools that can complete academic work at a high level
- The competitive landscape among AI companies is intensifying as they all converge on the superapp vision

## Overview

A weekly roundup of the biggest tech stories, focused on three major developments: OpenAI finally launching its superapp (an AI interface that handles multiple computing tasks), Meta's aggressive AI pricing strategy that undercuts competitors, and reports of ChatGPT-enabled cheating at Brown University. The discussion covers the strategic implications of each development and what they signal about the direction of the AI industry.

## Implications

The AI superapp race is intensifying, with OpenAI, Meta, Google, and Anthropic all converging on the same vision. Meta's price war could commoditize AI services faster than expected. The education sector faces an existential challenge as AI tools make traditional assessment methods obsolete.
