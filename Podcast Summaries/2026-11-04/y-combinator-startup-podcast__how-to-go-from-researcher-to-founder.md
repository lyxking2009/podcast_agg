# How To Go From Researcher to Founder — Y Combinator Startup Podcast

## TL;DR
YC Visiting Partner Francois Chaubard, a PhD student and former researcher in Fei-Fei Li's lab who founded Focal Systems, explains why the instincts that help researchers succeed in academia often work against them as startup founders. He breaks down 10 mindset shifts researchers need to make, including learning to thrive without grades or deadlines, distinguishing interesting problems from important ones, moving faster with imperfect information, and starting with what customers actually want.
