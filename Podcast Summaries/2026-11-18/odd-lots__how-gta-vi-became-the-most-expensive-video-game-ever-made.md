# How GTA VI Became the Most Expensive Video Game Ever Made — Odd Lots

## TL;DR
Odd Lots discusses GTA VI's unprecedented scale with Bloomberg's Jason Schreier. The game, developed by Rockstar over roughly eight years with a team of 4,700 and a rumored budget exceeding $1 billion, follows criminal couple Jason and Lucia in a satirical Florida. Its success is driven by GTA Online's microtransaction revenue and massive anticipation, but it may be an anomaly in an industry struggling with rising costs and AI-induced hardware shortages.

## Key points
- Grand Theft Auto V sold 235 million copies, making it the best-selling non-mobile game of all time, which sets the stage for GTA VI's massive launch.
- GTA VI's budget is rumored at $1-1.5 billion, with a team of 4,700 people; full production began after Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018, though research started in 2015.
- Take-Two, Rockstar's parent, derives roughly 80% of its revenue from in-game monetization, not game sales; GTA Online alone generates an estimated half a billion dollars per year from microtransactions.
- The success of GTA Online reduced the incentive to rush a sequel, contributing to the 13-year gap between GTA V and GTA VI.
- GTA VI features dual protagonists Jason and Lucia, a Bonnie-and-Clyde criminal couple in fictional Miami (Vice City) and surrounding Florida; players can rob any building, hijack cars, and encounter absurd Florida satire like alligators and backyard wrestling.
- The game is digital-only and exceeds 210 gigabytes, so no physical disc is available; this reflects industry shift to digital distribution and higher profit margins.
- Anticipation is unprecedented: PlayStation and Xbox both report record pre-orders, possibly over 10 million copies; Netflix's gameplay premiere was the #1 content on the service, beating a new Robert De Niro film; many people are taking PTO to play.
- Console and GPU markets are strained by AI chip demand: PlayStation 5 base model now costs $650 (up from $500 at launch), high-end GPU prices have doubled or tripled, and next-gen consoles might exceed $1,000.
- Game development costs have ballooned industry-wide, but sales have not kept pace; most companies seek to lower budgets, making GTA VI an anomaly because Rockstar can guarantee returns from the franchise's power.
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick joked about giving Rockstar an unlimited budget, comparing it to his son's wedding budget, underscoring Rockstar's unique financial position.
- Violence in video games is no longer a major controversy; society now treats game violence similarly to violence in films by Scorsese or Tarantino.
- Gamers tend to associate quality with studios like Rockstar or Larian rather than individual auteurs, though indie solo developers such as Eric Barone (Stardew Valley) are recognized.
- Rockstar may leverage GTA VI's existing world for future expansions, but a brand-new game from the studio would likely still take another seven to eight years.
