## Key Points
- Crypto markets fell 3–7% as Strategy-related concerns weighed on sentiment, with Bitcoin trading near $63,600 amid $2.97B in record ETF outflows and a Fear and Greed Index at 23 (extreme fear)
- The prediction market space is heating up: Kalshi is targeting a $40B valuation vs Polymarket's $15B, with Kalshi holding 58% of market flow and Polymarket at 28%, plus new entrants like Coinbase threatening further disruption
- Crypto VC deal volume slowed in Q1 2026 — $4B raised, a 50% quarter-over-quarter drop — with capital shifting from L1s and meme coins toward stablecoin infrastructure, RWA tokenization, and custody solutions
- Kraken's parent Payward is in advanced talks to acquire a 15% equity stake in Aave Group, paying 35,000 ETH for 250,000 AAVE tokens and common equity at a $385M Aave valuation (~$71M deal)
- Aave founder Stani Kulechov denied the reported deal terms — specifically rejecting any suggestion of a token sale at a 70% discount — and teased "Aavenomics 3.0" buybacks
- The Kraken-Aave deal is part of a broader Payward pre-IPO diversification push; the firm previously acquired crypto derivatives exchange Bitnomial for up to $550M
- Social trading app Fomo raised $75M in a Series B at a $550M valuation, backed by Index Ventures, Union Square Ventures, and Benchmark; the app has 625,000+ traders and $4B in volume with only 17 employees
- Prediction markets faced regulatory pressure: Spain ordered ISPs to block Polymarket and Kalshi in May, and the U.S. CFTC opened a rulemaking on whether sports event contracts are "contrary to the public interest"

## Overview
In this weekly roundup, hosts Jason Yanowitz, Santiago Roel, and Rob Hadick assess a crypto market under pressure from macro headwinds and Strategy-linked concern, with Bitcoin sitting around $63,600 amid record ETF outflows. The episode then pivots to two major stories: the fierce competition in prediction markets between Kalshi and Polymarket — both in valuation wars and regulatory battles — and Kraken's reported attempt to buy a 15% stake in Aave Group as part of Payward's pre-IPO expansion playbook. The show closes with coverage of Fomo's $75M Series B, a sign that institutional capital is still willing to bet on consumer crypto applications.

## Implications
The Kraken-Aave deal, if consummated, would mark one of the largest CeFi-DeFi convergence moves to date and signal that top-tier exchanges are moving beyond spot trading into protocol equity stakes ahead of their own public listings. The prediction market war between Kalshi and Polymarket reflects a rapidly maturing sector that is drawing both massive private capital (combined valuations near $55B) and increased regulatory scrutiny, with the FIFA World Cup driving record volumes and reinforcing sports as the dominant use case. The broader crypto market pullback, driven by ETF outflows and macro risk-off sentiment, suggests the current cycle may be entering a consolidation phase, with VC dollars also rotating toward infrastructure and revenue-generating businesses over speculative tokens.
