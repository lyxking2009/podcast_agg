# Anthropic's Labs Lead On Fable's Capabilities + Building AI-Native Products

## Key Points
- **Fable and Mythos pulled from market:** Anthropic was forced to withdraw its frontier models shortly after release, possibly linked to providing SK Telecom access. Krieger notes "we're dealing with unprecedented times."
- **Fable's workflow revolution:** Krieger would queue up overnight work for Fable — a full Python-to-TypeScript codebase conversion (hundreds of thousands of lines) completed in about an hour, then idle for 7+ hours.
- **"Theory of project":** Fable exhibits advanced understanding of how changes impact the broader system, similar to a senior engineer — a key advancement beyond raw code generation.
- **Anthropic Labs' mission:** Build next-gen AI-native products that close the gap between model capabilities and current product offerings. Labs focuses on fundamental interaction paradigms.
- **Platform vs. product tension:** Anthropic navigates being both a platform (API) and product (Claude). Krieger: Anthropic should only enter a space if it can push the industry forward in a new way.
- **Safety is "material, not marketing":** Krieger states safety concerns are genuine. "Uplift" is a key metric — measuring how much a model enhances a layperson vs. an expert.
- **Token intelligence > token maxing:** The most productive users aren't the highest token consumers. Focus shifting to getting more valuable work per token.
- **Outcome-based pricing:** Anthropic experimenting with pricing tied to task completion, not just token usage.
- **Instagram team size insight:** With modern AI tools, the core Instagram team (13 at acquisition) could have been 4-6 people.

## Overview
Mike Krieger, head of Anthropic Labs and Instagram co-founder, joins Alex Kantrowitz live from the Big Technology AI Summit. The conversation covers the unprecedented government-mandated withdrawal of Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models, how Fable fundamentally changed Krieger's daily workflow, and Anthropic Labs' vision for AI-native products. Krieger provides rare insight into how frontier models transform engineering work, the tension between being a platform and product company, and Anthropic's approach to safety as substance rather than marketing.

## Implications
- The government model withdrawal sets a precedent for AI regulation that could reshape the industry
- Fable's capabilities suggest AI coding assistants are approaching (or surpassing) senior engineer-level understanding
- The shift from token consumption to token intelligence will reshape AI pricing and product design
- Anthropic's platform/product dual role creates inevitable tension with partners like Cursor
