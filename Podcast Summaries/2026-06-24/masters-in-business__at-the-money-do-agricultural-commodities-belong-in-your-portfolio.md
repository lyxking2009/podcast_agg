# At The Money: Do Agricultural Commodities Belong in Your Portfolio?

## Key Points
- Agricultural commodities as a portfolio diversifier — analysis of whether they deserve allocation in modern portfolios
- Historical performance and correlation patterns of agricultural commodities vs. traditional asset classes
- Impact of climate change, geopolitical tensions, and supply chain disruptions on agricultural commodity prices
- Role of agricultural commodities as an inflation hedge
- Practical considerations for retail investors: ETFs, futures, and direct commodity exposure

## Overview
Barry Ritholtz explores the investment case for agricultural commodities in this "At The Money" segment. The discussion covers the historical performance of agricultural commodities as portfolio diversifiers, their correlation with inflation and other asset classes, and practical ways for investors to gain exposure. The conversation examines whether the current macroeconomic environment — marked by climate volatility, geopolitical tensions, and supply chain disruptions — makes agricultural commodities more or less attractive as a portfolio component.

## Implications
- Agricultural commodities may offer genuine diversification benefits in inflationary environments
- Climate change introduces both risk and opportunity in agricultural commodity markets
- Investors should consider the roll yield and contango/backwardation dynamics when evaluating commodity exposure
