# Holcim CEO: Europe's Housing Shortage, Decarbonising Cement and Why Complacency Is the Enemy

## Key Points
- **Concrete is the second most consumed material on earth** after water — approximately 30 billion tons used annually. It's a bedrock of modern civilization.
- **Europe is missing close to 10 million homes.** Since H2 2025, permitting approvals are rising across key markets, signaling near-term recovery.
- **Sustainability drives profitable growth:** Eco-friendly products now represent >⅓ of sales with cost advantages from reformulation. Europe's profit rose 430 basis points over 5 years.
- **Latin America delivers highest EBIT margins.** Mexico targets 1.8M new homes in 5 years. Peru booming from mining/agriculture investments.
- **AI will deliver 200M CHF net benefits by 2028.** Predictive maintenance in cement plants (avg. size: 1km x 500m) prevents unplanned downtime.
- **Cement accounts for 8% of global CO₂ emissions** — higher than aviation. Key levers: product reformulation, waste-to-energy, carbon capture R&D.
- **Net-zero target: 2050** — first in the industry.
- **Leadership rules:** (1) Control the controllables, (2) Complacency is the biggest enemy, (3) Benchmark relentlessly.
- **Retail model innovation:** 2,500 "Sensor" franchise shops serve self-builders (45% of customers), covering 85% of their needs.
- **North America spin-off** separated to unlock growth in Europe/LatAm.

## Overview
Nicolai Tangen interviews Miljan Gutovic, CEO of Holcim, about the transformation underway in global building materials. They discuss Europe's housing crisis, how sustainability is driving profitability, the booming Latin American market, AI implementation in industrial operations, and Gutovic's leadership philosophy of combating complacency through relentless benchmarking. The conversation also covers Holcim's decentralized organizational model and the company's net-zero 2050 commitment.

## Implications
- The European housing shortage creates sustained demand for construction materials
- Sustainability is no longer a cost center but a profit driver in heavy industry
- Latin America represents a high-margin growth opportunity for industrial companies
- AI-driven predictive maintenance is delivering measurable ROI in traditional manufacturing
- The North America spin-off may create value by allowing focused regional strategies
