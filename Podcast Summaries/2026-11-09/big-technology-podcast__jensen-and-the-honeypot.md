# Jensen and The Honeypot — Big Technology Podcast

## TL;DR
A solo Alex Kantrowitz episode on the story that detonated over the weekend: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted an extramarital affair with Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman and former executive at Singapore-based chip firm Megaspeed whom U.S. intelligence assesses was working for China's Ministry of State Security. ## Key points
- Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation, to be run by independent directors with outside legal counsel, into whether Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence operation.

- Huang admitted the affair in a message to employees, apologised to his family, colleagues, board and shareholders, and said he took full responsibility and had fallen short of his responsibilities.

- Context that makes it worse: AI chips are the central lever in the U.S.-China trade war, and Huang has cultivated a close relationship with President Trump while Nvidia's export positions are politically contested.
