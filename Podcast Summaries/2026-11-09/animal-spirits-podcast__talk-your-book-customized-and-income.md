# Talk Your Book: Customized and Income — Animal Spirits Podcast

## TL;DR
A two-segment sponsored 'Talk Your Book' episode. Michael Batnick and Ben Carlson first talk separately managed accounts with Kacie Walsh Jason of Dimensional Fund Advisors, then option income ETFs with Wes Mathews of NEOS - two different answers to the same investor question: how do you get the exposure and the income you actually want inside a taxable portfolio?

## Key points
- Segment 1 - SMAs with Dimensional: the pitch is customization plus tax management. Direct indexing lets an investor exclude concentrated positions, tilt toward factors, and harvest losses at the individual-security level rather than the fund level.
- The trade-off discussed: SMAs cost more than a fund and add operational complexity, so the tax alpha has to be large and repeatable to justify the fee - it is most compelling for taxable accounts with a low basis or a concentrated legacy holding.
- Segment 2 - option income ETFs with NEOS: the category has exploded because yields look attractive in a high-rate world, but the economics are simple - you are selling volatility, so you cap upside and take the downside, with the premium stream substituting for price return.
- The hosts press on what the yield actually represents: distribution rate is not total return, and covered-call/put-writing structures can look smoother than the underlying while still carrying full equity beta.
- Practical framing: these are portfolio-construction tools for specific objectives (income need, tax management, concentration risk), not free lunch replacements for core equity exposure.
- Both segments converge on the same discipline Animal Spirits preaches: decide what job the sleeve is doing before choosing the product, then size it accordingly.
