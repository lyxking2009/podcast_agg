# Blue Sweep, Red Screens: The Midterms Hit the AI Trade with Liz Thomas + Bill Capuzzi, CEO of Apex Fintech Solutions — RiskReversal Pod

## TL;DR
Guy Adami and Liz Thomas work through a sharp selloff led by semiconductors and the AI/data-center complex, with the NASDAQ slipping into correction after the midterm blue sweep put data-center policy risk on the table. Then, from Apex's Innovation Summit in Austin, Dan Nathan and Guy interview Apex Fintech CEO Bill Capuzzi on custody, real-time ledgers and why tokenizing public stocks is not the near-term prize.

## Key points
- Semis peaked in mid-June and have been the source of the market's weakness since; the post-election rotation out of chips into hyperscalers on Wednesday turned into a broader AI unwind on Thursday with almost nowhere to hide in tech.
- Thomas argues large single-day drops are a periodic feature of this market, not evidence the AI capex cycle has broken - the debate is about duration of the de-rating, not the existence of the theme.
- She walks through her tactical bond call: 10-year yields falling from roughly 5.25% to around 4.87%, and what that implies for the December Fed decision.
- Fed puzzle: jobs data has been strong, but year-over-year CPI comparisons are distorted because last year's government shutdown broke the data series - making the inflation read genuinely ambiguous going into the meeting.
- Tightening financial conditions plus an AI-capex-driven growth scare is the combination markets are now pricing; healthcare (XLV) rallied then saw profit-taking, and energy/refiners remain a high-uncertainty bucket.
- Consumer risk is the sleeper issue: the rotation is a bet that the AI trade's wealth effect stops doing the heavy lifting for consumer spending.
- Capuzzi segment: Apex sits behind hundreds of fintechs, so it sees retail flow directly; he is sceptical of near-term tokenization of public equities, arguing the real utility is in private securities where settlement is genuinely broken.
- Apex's pitch is infrastructure - a real-time ledger and Ascend OS replacing batch clearing - and Capuzzi argues prediction markets need hedging use cases (not sports betting) to become a lasting market structure.
